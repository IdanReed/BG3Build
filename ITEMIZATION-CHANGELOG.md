# Itemization changelog

Tracks every gear decision in `content/characters/*.md` and `content/loot.md`, so a
change to one character's loadout can be traced to the reason it was made and to the
source that backs it.

Newest entries first. Each entry names the character, the act, the slot, what changed,
and why.

## Reference: what the videos actually rate

only incidentally inside a build guide. This matters because it bounds what "deviating
from the videos" can even mean.

   *"Equipable gear only; weapons and consumables are excluded."* All 20 entries are
   Act 1.
2. **`TheBESTSTAVESinBaldur'sGate3`** — a complete S-through-D tier list of staves

Acuity, Bhaalist Armour, Spellmight Gloves, Gontr Mael, Gloves of Soul Catching, Risky

the source material, not an unexplained deviation.

## Deviation register

Every case where this party does something the ranked videos do not, with the argument
for it. A deviation is only listed here if the video actually rates the item; gear the
corpus never mentions is not a deviation.

**D1 — Gloves of Dexterity (#1 of 20) go to Bonbon, not Gale.**
The video's case for the Sorcerer is the respec trick: drop DEX to 8, reclaim the points.
That trick does not pay here. The gloves are a Crèche pickup, so a character who
pre-spends DEX 8 spends the whole first half of Act 1 at DEX 8 — for Gale that is his
entire AC, because Draconic Resilience is 13 + DEX and Armour of Landfall is 13 + DEX.
On Bonbon the gloves are worth more than the DEX alone: the **+1 Attack Rolls** rider
applies to every projectile of a Slashing Flourish and every hand-crossbow shot, which is
four or more attack rolls a turn against Gale's zero weapon attacks. Gale's hands are
committed to the Reverberation engine (Gloves of Belligerent Skies) and later Spellmight
Gloves, so he could not wear them anyway.

**D2 — Hellrider's Pride (#6 of 20) is not a standing pick.**
It is the other half of the healing-trigger package whose first half, The Whispering
Promise (#10), this party does take. The ring costs Bonbon a ring slot she can spare; the
gloves would cost her the #1 item in the same list. The party also has no dedicated
healer — the video's own advice is to "build a healer around" the pair, "ideally with
bonus-action or area healing," and Bonbon's bonus action is already committed to Healing
Word plus Whispering Promise plus Broodmother's Revenge firing together. Kept in the bag
for a stretch of heavy physical damage, which is exactly when Blade Ward's
bludgeoning/piercing/slashing resistance is worth more than DEX 18 and +1 to hit.

**D3 — Shapeshifter's Boon Ring (#9 of 20) is skipped outright.**
The video rates it for exploration and says in the same breath that it "offers little
direct combat value" and only works while disguised or shapeshifted. The party's check
coverage is already Expertise ×4 plus Jack of All Trades on Bonbon, Expertise in Sleight
of Hand and Stealth on Asterion, and at-will Guidance from the Silver Pendant. Both of
Bonbon's ring slots are contested by items that work every combat round.

**D4 — Boots of Speed (#14 of 20) go to Bonbon, not Asterion.**
The obvious home looks like the monk, and it is the wrong one: Step of the Wind
already gives Asterion bonus-action Dash and Disengage from Monk 2, so the boots
would buy him nothing while displacing Disintegrating Night Walkers (#11) and their
short-rest Misty Step. Bonbon is the character the video actually describes — "the
character most likely to waste a turn out of position" — because she has no innate
mobility at all and an otherwise-empty Act 1 boot slot. Note the video's own flag
that the opportunity-attack rider is bugged: take these for the Dash.

**D5 — The two Mithral ores buy Adamantine Splint (#15) and Adamantine Scale Mail
(#16). The Adamantine Shield (#13) is not forged.**
Grymforge yields exactly two ores and the guide previously named three Adamantine
pieces. The Shield loses because its whole case rested on an unverified reading — that
a shield sitting in an *inactive* melee set still confers crit immunity while its
wearer fights from the ranged set. The wiki confirms only that the **AC bonus**
carries over, and says nothing about crit immunity. That gamble is also unnecessary:
Bonbon is the party's only Heavy-armour wearer (Fighter 1 taken first), so the Splint
gives her AC 18 and crit immunity outright while she keeps the Knife of the
Undermountain King in her melee off-hand for its global crit-threshold reduction.
The second ore goes to Charles as Scale Mail — medium armour, which he is proficient
with — for AC 18, crit immunity and −1 damage through Act 2. Crit immunity matters on
both of them for the same reason: a concentration save is DC = half the damage taken
or 10, whichever is higher, so a critical hit roughly doubles it, and both are holding
concentration in every fight.

**D6 — Ring of Flinging (#18 of 20) is skipped.**
The video scopes it to Tavern Brawler *throwers*. Asterion is a Tavern Brawler who
punches: his Action is Extra Attack unarmed and his bonus actions are Flurries. No
character in the party throws a weapon as its damage loop.

**D7 — Breastplate +1 (honourable mention) is skipped.**
It is a 15 AC medium armour, and the only medium-armour wearer is Charles, who has
Luminous Armour — also 15 AC, plus the Radiating Shockwave engine. Strictly worse for the
one character who could wear it.

**D8 — Corellon's Grace (S tier) is retired at character level 5. This is not a
deviation.** The video says outright that "Tavern Brawler Monks eventually replace it."
Asterion uses it levels 2–4 and unequips it when Tavern Brawler comes online, because
from that point a held staff makes his Attack action swing the staff instead of punching.

**D9 — Mourning Frost (S tier) is skipped.**
Its value is a cold-damage engine and the Chilled → cold-vulnerability chain. Gale is a
Red Draconic fire Sorcerer with Elemental Adept: Fire; every one of those riders is dead
on him. Its residual value as a generic late off-hand staff is beaten by Melf's First
Staff (also S) in Act 1 and by Rhapsody or Staff of Spellpower in Act 3.

**D10 — Staff of Cherished Necromancy (S tier) is skipped.**
Its payoff is a free Necromancy spell on a spell kill plus necromancy-save disadvantage.
No character in this party casts a Necromancy spell. Its secondary use as a Markoheshkir
dual-wield partner loses to Rhapsody, which stacks +3 attack, +3 damage **and** +3 spell
save DC, with the damage applying per Scorching Ray ray.

**D11 — Caitiff Staff (A tier) is skipped.**
The video calls it "a direct Warlock upgrade." The party's two Warlocks cannot bank the
value: Charles is Warlock 5 but both hands hold Shadow Blade and Phalar Aluve, and Gale
is Warlock 1, so the staff would restore a single level-1 pact slot per long rest.

**D12 — Staff of Interruption (A tier) is skipped.**
The video routes it to "an Archer or other character whose main weapon slot is not
vital." That does not describe Bonbon — her hand crossbows *are* the engine, because
every hit stacks Arcane Acuity. Gale already has Counterspell on his own spell list.

**D13 — Staff of the Emperor (A tier) is skipped.**
It is a defensive off-hand for a durable caster. Gale's off-hand is spoken for in every
act: Melf's First Staff in Acts 1–2, then Rhapsody or Staff of Spellpower in Act 3.

**D14 — Incandescent Staff (B tier) is skipped, with a named exception.**
It is the most on-theme Act 2 staff for a fire Sorcerer, and the guide records it as a
per-fight swap rather than a rejection. Standing loadout keeps Melf's First Staff,
because Melf's is rated S against Incandescent's B and gives +1 spell **save DC** that
Gale needs for Command and Hold Person — Incandescent only improves ranged spell attack
rolls. Swap it in for a fight where fire resistance on Gale is worth more than the DC.

## 2026-09-10 — Gloves of Battlemage's Power, reaction-smite correction

**Charles, Acts 2-3, hands.** No loadout change. The stated trigger list was wrong about one
entry and the build's nova reasoning rests on it.

The guide listed "any smite spell, and Divine Smite" as flat triggers. Per
`research/gloves_of_battlemage.txt` (community code-dive, Appendix and "Special Case: Reaction
Smite"), reaction Divine Smite **inherits the attack it rides on** and triggers nothing by
itself: +0 off a plain weapon swing, +4 chained off an attack that already triggered, +2 only
when Divine Smite is cast directly from the action bar.

This costs Charles nothing as built — he leads with Booming Blade or a smite, and every Shadow
Blade swing is itself a trigger, so each attack-plus-reaction-smite is +4, with the Luminous
Armour shockwave resetting the once-per-attack limit between attacks. It does bite in one
contingency the guide already recommends: **binding Phalar Aluve main hand against
Psychic-immune targets** removes the Shadow Blade trigger, so ordinary Phalar swings and the
reaction smites hanging off them give no Acuity. Both `charles.md` and `loot.md` now say so.

Also confirmed, against an earlier reading of the bg3.wiki Bugs section: **Shadow Blade weapon
attacks still trigger the Gloves post-Patch-8.** The hotfix removed only the broader bug where
*any* damage triggered them while a Shadow Blade was held; the narrow weapon-attack trigger
(`HasStatus('SHADOW_BLADE', GetActiveWeapon())`) is intact. The permanent hireling-exploit
Shadow Blade does **not** carry that status and does not work.

## 2026-08-15 — full itemization review, all four characters, all three acts

### Summary

Every equipment slot for every character now has a named default in every act, every
cross-character contest is resolved in writing, and each act states what is held over
instead of leaving it implied. `tools/check_itemization.py` enforces the first of those
mechanically and currently reports OK.

Before this pass: 16 slot-gaps, 17 unresolved conflicts, and Acts 2–3 were nearly empty
for Asterion and Bonbon — 2 and 3 entries respectively across two whole acts.

### Rules corrections — the guide was wrong about these

These matter most, because the previous plan contained gear that does not work, and
excluded gear that does.

1. **Covert Cowl and the Dark Justiciar Helmet are illegal on Asterion.** Both require
   armour proficiency, and the wiki states that helmets and gloves "marked as Light,
   Medium or Heavy Armour count as armour, and prevent Unarmoured Defence from working."
   Any such helmet also switches off Bracers of Defence. His Act 1–2 head is now the
   **Circlet of Psionic Revenge**, which carries no proficiency requirement.
2. **Charles CAN wear Helldusk Armour.** The guide asserted he was locked to medium
   armour, which is what made Helm of Balduran "the answer to Charles's crit problem."
   The wiki is explicit that the armour grants its own proficiency: "You are considered
   Proficient with this armour while wearing it." His Act 3 chest is now Helldusk Armour,
   AC 21 with −3 damage from every source.
3. **Stunning Strike does not use Spell Save DC.** It uses the weapon-action DC —
   8 + proficiency + the higher of STR/DEX — and the wiki notes "the DC in the tooltip
   incorrectly shows the Spellcasting DC." Every +Spell-Save-DC item is therefore dead on
   Asterion, which removes Hat of Uninhibited Kushigo, Hood of the Weave and Fistbreaker
   Helm from consideration for him.
4. **The Resonance Stone does not help Stunning Strike either.** Its aura imposes
   disadvantage on *mental* saves (INT/WIS/CHA); Stunning Strike is a CON save. It still
   doubles Manifestation of Mind and still sets up Gale's Command and both Hold spells,
   which are WIS saves — the guide now distinguishes what it does from what it does not.
5. **The Shadow Blade Ring costs no ring slot.** Since Patch 8 its blade needs no
   concentration and lasts until long rest, and the wiki says "the ring can be unequipped
   after summoning the shadow blade." Charles's four-rings-for-two-slots problem was not
   real; he runs Risky Ring + Killer's Sweetheart and treats the Shadow Blade Ring as a
   pre-combat button.
6. **Caustic Band and Strange Conduit Ring do not work on unarmed strikes.** Both are
   confirmed to cover melee, ranged and Thrown attacks "but not Unarmed Strike," so
   neither can ever move to Asterion despite his high hit count.
7. **Gale cannot wear Boots of Persistence or Vital Conduit Boots.** Both require Medium
   Armour proficiency, which Sorcerer 11 / Warlock 1 never grants. His Act 3 boots are
   Evasive Shoes, and the guide now records why the obvious picks are illegal.
8. **There are no magical cloaks in Act 1.** The Deathstalker Mantle is the only one, and
   it is a Dark Urge reward. Three of the four Act 1 cloak slots are now explicitly and
   deliberately empty rather than silently missing.

  where it previously had two entries: Amulet of the Harpers, Flawed Helldusk Gloves,
  Shadow-Cloaked Ring, plus an explicit held-over line covering the five unchanged slots.

  Act 3). Act 2 gained chest, hands and cloak; Act 3 gained chest, feet, amulet, cloak,

### Intra-act progression now stated explicitly

Each character gained a **Progression** tab listing all ten slots with their Act 1 → 2 → 3
chain. Several within-act upgrades that were previously implicit are now written down:

- Asterion, Act 1 hands: Bracers of Defence → offensive gloves when kills beat 2 AC.
- Bonbon, Act 1 head: free Grymskull Helm → Diadem of Arcane Synergy at the Crèche.
- Bonbon, Act 1 chest: Protecty Sparkswall → Adamantine Splint once the Forge opens.
- Bonbon, Act 1 hands: Gloves of Archery → Gloves of Dexterity at the Crèche.
- Charles, Act 1 hands: Gloves of the Growling Underdog → Gloves of Baneful Striking.
- Charles, Act 1 weapons: bound weapon → two-handed Phalar Aluve from the Underdark.
- Gale, Act 1 weapons: Spellsparkler alone → Spellsparkler + Melf's from character
  level 4, which is when Dual Wielder makes the pair legal.

### Wording

Every line that described a previous version of a build was rewritten to state the final
answer. The purge covered `party.md`, all four character files and `loot.md`, removing
the "Storm build" / "old plan" / "PROMOTED" / "DEMOTED" / "REASSIGNED" / "CONTEST
RESOLVED" / "previously" framing. Genuine playtest flags ("VERIFY IN PLAY") and genuine
patch notes were kept, because those describe the game rather than an earlier draft.

### `loot.md` — the acquisition route was realigned to match

The act-by-area loot guide is what you actually follow in play, so it was brought back
into agreement with the character pages:

- **12 items changed owner**, matching the conflict table above. The reassignments with
  real consequences are Ring of Protection → Gale, Boots of Speed → Bonbon,
  Hellrider's Longbow → Gale, Amulet of Greater Health → Charles, Adamantine Scale Mail
  → Charles, and Cloak of Protection → Charles.
- **17 items were added** that the character files name but the route did not list —
  most of Asterion's Act 2–3 kit (Amulet of the Harpers, Shadow-Cloaked Ring, Flawed
  Helldusk Gloves, Vest of Soul Rejuvenation, Cloak of Displacement, Ring of Free
  Action), Bonbon's boots and cloaks in every act, and Charles's Helldusk pieces.
- **The Adamantine Shield was removed from the ore plan** and the two crafted pieces are
  now separate entries with their owners named.
- **Two items had contradictory or wrong entries.** The Amulet of the Devout was listed
  as both "no longer Gale's" and "Gale keeps the Devout" in the same file; it now goes to
  nobody. Helldusk Boots were filed under the House of Hope — they are in a locked gilded
  chest on the top floor of **Wyrm's Rock Fortress**, which is now its own area entry.
- **17 more lines of prior-build-version wording** were rewritten.

The route now carries 136 items across 27 areas, with ownership spread roughly evenly
(Gale 27, Bonbon 26, Asterion 23, Charles 23, plus 31 marked `any`).

### Known open questions, deliberately left flagged in the content

- **Spellmight Gloves** — whether the +1d8 applies per ray or once per spell is not
  addressed anywhere on the wiki, and the two readings differ by roughly 27 damage on a
  7-ray cast. Flagged in Gale's traps; test it on the first cast.
- **Resonance Stone after Act 2** — the wiki notes it "sometimes ceases to function upon
  leaving Act Two." Charles's Act 3 weapon plan depends on it, so the fallbacks (Nyrulna,
  the Charge-Bound Warhammer) are documented alongside it.
