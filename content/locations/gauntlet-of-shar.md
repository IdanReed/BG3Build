quests:
- name: 'Find Ketheric Thorm''s Relic'
  steps: |-
    - Z'rell at Moonrise (or Balthazar's notes in his room, or Thisobald's drunk rant) sends you to the Thorm Mausoleum. Raphael waits at the gate (X -191, Y 112); see Kill Raphael's Old Enemy.
    - **Mausoleum.** The Herald of Balthazar skull at Melodia's tomb tells you to leave. The Stained Book on the east altar gives the order: "from splendour, to tragedy, to infamy". In the north chamber press the buttons under **Moonrise Towers**, then **Grief**, then **General**. The traps there are already disarmed (Perception 10 shows them). The walls open on a disc; the Traversal Gem takes you down.
    - **Entrance hall.** Shar speaks through her statue; a forcefield knocks anyone who walks up to it. Snuff the eight Mystic Thuribles: levers in the four alcoves off the east and west halls (one pressure plate and one vent each, Sleight of Hand DC 10 to disarm, a Void Cloud if tripped), then touch each thurible. Ray of Frost, Arrows of Ice or thrown water snuff them from range. Unequip torches and the Moonlantern; Pixie Blessing does not count. In full dark a glyph ring appears: walk the gaps to the pedestal and touch it. Or Misty Step someone to the statue. This opens the north stone door and the west doors and kills the traps.
    - **Balthazar.** West of the north door three skeletons talk, then Umbral Tremors pour out Dark Justiciars; the skeletons fight beside you. Each tremor spawns three Justiciars then closes, so kill Justiciars rather than tremors for XP. A second, larger wave in the antechamber ends with a Deep Umbral Tremor at X -820, Y -786 that spawns a Justiciar Crusader. Balthazar's door at X -852, Y -786 then opens. He wants the trials cleared for him. Get the bell if you want it, then kill him here (see npcs).
    - **Gems.** Yurgir, Soft-Step, Self-Same, Faith-Leap. Trial hall entrance at X -758, Y -783 between the two stairs to Balthazar. Spiral stairs at the far end go down to Faith-Leap and the Silent Library; the lift by the waypoint goes down once the Lever Handle is fitted.
    - **Pedestal of Reckoning**, north of the entrance hall down the stairs: one gem in the empty slot, a platform unlocks. Ungroup, walk each character onto the middle, use the Traversal Gem on the edge. Lower altar: three gems, the great doors open. Knock also opens them.
    - **Verge of Shadows.** Waypoint and the pool. The pool is the Shadowfell.
  outcome: 'Aylin freed flies to Moonrise; Ketheric is mortal on the roof. The exit portal drops you outside the mausoleum. Next: Jaheira''s assault.'
- name: The Chosen of Shar
  wiki: The Chosen of Shar
  steps: |-
    - Bring Shadowheart. At the Pedestal of Reckoning she asks to complete the trials: answer **"All right, I'll help if I can"** (Nightsong Point). Let her give blood at each trial bowl (+5 each, -1 if refused; the donor bleeds one turn).
    - With four gems she asks for the Spear of Night. Agree. Refusing twice (at the pedestal, then after her prayer at the Verge) makes her hostile and she is gone.
    - Silent Library: book on the pedestal, spear from the shrine. Keep it in the active party's inventory.

    - Shadowfell with her present: Balthazar first if alive, then her choice at Aylin. - name: 'Kill Raphael''s Old Enemy'
  steps: |-
    - Raphael at the mausoleum gate. Persuasion DC 10 then Insight DC 10 make him name the orthon. If Astarion is an Origin character both must pass or Raphael leaves without giving the quest; with companion Astarion the rolls can fail. He offers to read Astarion's scars for the kill.
    - East wing past the north door: jump the broken stairs. Nessa the displacer beast runs off; follow her and nothing else. Coming in from the far stairs (X -673, Y -789) or the gap at X -669, Y -786, or being seen approaching, means a fight with no dialogue.
    - Talk: **"Put that thing down. I'm here to talk"**, **"Wait - you know Raphael?"**, **"I've had dealings with that devil. Maybe we can help each other."**, **"Let's share our experiences about Raphael."** He sings the contract. Passive Insight DC 14 spots the clause; Bards and Warlocks pass automatically, so Bonbon or Charles.
    - Talk-down, Bonbon speaking: Persuasion DC 16 **"The lyrics are a trick. You've always had an audience - your followers. Get rid of them."** (Bard line, same DC) and the merregons kill themselves. Persuasion DC 21 **"You're not finished yet - the displacer can hear you, can't she? Kill her."** and he shoots Nessa. Persuasion DC 21 **"Kill yourself, complete the contract, and you'll be reborn in Avernus. Free."** (Bard line, same DC) and he dies. Skipping the Nessa step costs DC 30. Gale's Fiend patron has a one-step DC 21 line straight to suicide.
    - To spare Nessa: investigate the spider carcass first (Investigation DC 14, Arcana DC 16), then Speak with Animals on her, Persuasion DC 16 that her meat is dosed. Persuasion DC 30 after that turns her against Yurgir. Or Detect Thoughts the Hoarding Merregon and claim her feeding.
    - Fight otherwise: see npcs.
  outcome: 'Yurgir dead either way: Hellfire Hand Crossbow, Infernal Metal, Umbral Gem, and Raphael comes to camp to read Astarion''s scars. Act 3: a killed Yurgir needs Persuasion DC 30 to side with you in the House of Hope.'
- name: 'Break Yurgir''s Contract'
  steps: |-
    - The alternative to killing him. In the talk pick Persuasion DC 16 **"There must be something you missed. Let me search this place for you."** A fail is a fight.
    - Attack any rat. The swarm flees down to the Broken Effigy at X -708, Y -722 under the giant statue. Read One Becomes Many and touch the effigy; a rat appears and offers a treasure deal. Refuse it.
    - Waves: rats, then Necrotic Rats, then Soporific Rats (sleep burst on death; Fey Ancestry is immune). Then Lyrthindor, level 7, 112 HP, Blight and Sleep. The talk goes nowhere; he attacks. Ring the bell for Flesh here.
    - Return to Yurgir. Raphael appears, re-signs him, and both leave for the House of Hope. Works in either order: kill Lyrthindor first and Yurgir is friendly on arrival.
  outcome: 'Umbral Gem; Justiciar''s Greatshield and Scimitar from Lyrthindor. Yurgir sides with you for free in the House of Hope and joins Gather Your Allies. Lost: Hellfire Hand Crossbow, Infernal Metal, Raphael''s scar reading. Astarion demands Persuasion or Deception DC 14, or pick **"No one''s forcing you to be here."**'
  lockout: 'Taking the rat''s treasure deal ends the quest without freeing him. Killing Yurgir first still allows the rat fight for the Justiciar loot.'

tips: |-
  **Order.** Yurgir first (Boots, gem, iron), then the two upper trials, then Balthazar and his vault, then the lift down to Faith-Leap and the Silent Library, then the Chamber of Command if you want the key and the cracked mirror. Gems into the altars last. Run the pre-Shadowfell checklist before the pool.

  **Curse.** The graveyard walk is Reithwin, deep curse; keep the Pixie Blessing up to the mausoleum gate. Nothing inside the gate or the Gauntlet needs protection; verify in game that the mausoleum foyer does not tick. The Shadowfell gives everyone Feather Fall.

  **Rest and travel.** Fast travel and camp work anywhere in the Gauntlet except inside a trial after its door is entered. Inside the Grand Mausoleum everyone has Glutted Catacombs: Animate Dead needs no corpse.

  **Talk-down casting.** Bonbon has Expertise and the Bard auto-pass on the contract clause. Gale's Fiend patron line is the short route if Bonbon is not the one talking. Charles is a Warlock and also auto-passes the clause.

  **Knock.** The lower altar doors open to Knock, so the trials are skippable. Do them anyway: the Self-Same Trial is where Killer's Sweetheart drops.

  **Mirror of Loss here is cracked.** Lore only. The working mirror is in the Cloister of Sombre Embrace in Act 3: each character gets one Religion DC 25 prayer (fail once, locked out) and then a 60% roll at +2 to an ability. Enhance Ability, Guidance, quicksave. Details on the Party tab.

  **Balthazar's Experiment** (Gale's Shadow Lantern or Mystra's blessing) is the ritual circle in Balthazar's secret workshop at Moonrise Towers, not here. See the Moonrise page.

  **Astarion.** Killing Yurgir (fight or talk-down) pays Raphael's deal and progresses The Pale Elf. Freeing Yurgir does not, and forces a DC 14 check to keep him. The route kills Yurgir.
