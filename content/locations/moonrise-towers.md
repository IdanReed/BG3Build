- name: Infiltrate Moonrise Towers
  steps: |-
    **Visit 1 (stop 13).**
    - Zealot Malik and Adept Merim at the stairs probe the speaker's mind and accept the party as True Souls. Charles as Dark Urge is welcomed "back". Do not mention the Harpers' hideout (-1 with three companions, no gain). They send you to Z'rell.
    - Throne room: Ketheric judges the goblins (Fezzerk, Sazza or Darra) and Minthara if she lived. Say what you like; the sentence never changes. Drenn attacks him and dies. He leaves for the roof and Z'rell hands the goblins to you. Spare them (Sazza freed here completes She Cannot Be Caged) or kill them by hand. Never by mind control (Vengeance oathbreak) and never attack Ketheric.
    - Z'rell, top of the stairs behind the throne. She reads the goblins' fate: if spared, Deception or Persuasion 16. Then proof of faith: Bonbon Deception 14 "Fake it" or Persuasion 14 (a romance thought), Asterion's Monk line "Empty your mind" with no roll, Charles Dark Urge Intimidation 15. She gives the relic task and Balthazar's Room Key and tells you to take the Moonlantern and touch nothing else. Ask "What exactly was this relic?" (passive Insight 14; Detect Thoughts helps it appear) then Persuasion 18 for Lann Tarv's stash. Each party member can try once. Asking her to show off her power may get Glourik killed.
    - Shop, loot the upper floor, run the prison rescue, then leave for the Gauntlet of Shar. Do not open the Ornate Door.

    **Visit 2 (stop 17).**
    - After Aylin is freed she flies to Moonrise and Jaheira's Harpers, the Flaming Fist (if Florrick came to Last Light) and the freed gnolls attack. Talk to Jaheira at the entrance.
    - Main floor: Z'rell leads with Mirror Image and Fire Shield: Chill and Counterspells the first spell. Cast a cantrip first, then Gale's real spell. The Warden, Keris and Nadi fight here if they still live.
    - Prison (optional): a Death Shepherd, ten Necromites and three Mistresses of Souls who summon more every turn. The Flaming Fist help. Jaheira will not go down.
    - Radija, four Necromites and Glourik at the Ornate Door on the first floor. Then the roof.
    - Rooftop: Susdera stands in front, Ketheric on the balcony behind, eight Necromites on the stairs, Squire unless placated on visit 1, Kar'niss if he lived. Talk first: with the Letter to Ketheric read, Persuasion 10; otherwise Persuasion 18 or Intimidation 21 then Persuasion 30. Aylin interrupts and he attacks regardless, but success unlocks the Colony DC 18. He has AC 22, Divine Smites, Deadly Orders on one target, Incubate Death each turn. Susdera throws Fireball and Bone Chill: Silence or Darkness her. Give him Prone, Blinded or Faerie Fire for advantage; Elixir of Radiant Resistance blunts the smites. Below about 40% HP he summons a tentacle, grabs Aylin and drops into the Colony. Finish the rest.
  outcome: 'Quest complete; Defeat Ketheric Thorm starts. Fast travel and camp are open again. Jaheira offers herself as a full companion here and insists on a party slot to go down. The way down is the hollow side tower; the jump is an area change with no fall damage. The Mind Flayer Colony has its own page.'
