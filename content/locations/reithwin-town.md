- name: Lift the Shadow Curse
  steps: |-
    - The town holds three pieces of this quest. **Battered Lute**: Malus Thorm in the House of Healing (its page). Play it for Art Cullagh at the inn, then hold Halsin's lakeshore portal for four turns.
    - **Oliver**: after Halsin brings Thaniel back, find Oliver at the House in Deep Shadows east of the inn and follow him through his portal to the central square. He hides under a Nightdome that reflects any damage dealt to it. He summons the wraiths Mummy and Daddy, the owlbear Shadow Plush, and five Shadow Friends at a time.     - When the dome breaks, be kind to Oliver or let Halsin talk. He always agrees to rejoin Thaniel; being cruel costs Halsin approval.
  outcome: 'Thaniel wakes at camp. The land heals only after Ketheric dies and the party takes the road west. Halsin stays as a full companion.'
  lockout: 'Leaving for Act 3 with the quest unresolved: Halsin stays behind for good.'

tips: |-
  Order for the Reithwin run (stops 7 to 10): Tollhouse (Gloves of Battlemage's Power), Mason's Guild (Helmet of Arcane Acuity), House of Healing (Eversight Ring, Battered Lute, Arabella's parents), then the Waning Moon if you want it. Come back for the mausoleum after the friendly Moonrise visit, with Shadowheart in the party.

  The curse here kills without the Pixie Blessing. If the blessing drops on a region change, ring the Moonlantern bell again before crossing a bridge.

  The Sanctuary blessings last until the next long rest. Take them on the day of a hard fight or a big talk: +5 Charisma is +2 or +3 on Bonbon's face checks or on Charles's spell save DC.

  The Moonrise bridge runs south from the square. The Tollhouse pier path to the Moonrise docks is half-ruined but walkable.
