title: Tadpole / Illithid Powers plan
sub: Who eats which parasites, in what order — and who becomes the party's one partial-illithid.
intro: 'Every Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen (a "tadpole") unlocks one Illithid power for one character, so they''re a limited resource you assign deliberately — don''t sprinkle them evenly. This party is a psychic-damage engine (the Resonance Stone makes a target Vulnerable to psychic), so the psychic powers — Concentrated Blast, Psionic Overload, Mind Blast, Cull the Weak — land for DOUBLE. Two rules drive the whole plan: illithid save DCs key off your latest-added class''s casting stat (and stack Arcane Acuity), and only ONE character can ever become a partial-illithid for the elite tier.'
mechanics:
- k: Unlocking the tree
  v: 'Act 1: after the Goblin Camp bridge cutscene, the Dream Guardian visits at the next long rest and starts Embrace Your Potential. From then, any parasite you pick up is fed straight into the Illithid Powers tree instead of sitting in your inventory.'
- k: Tadpoles are limited & assignable
  v: Each parasite unlocks exactly one power for one character. Feed them to the carriers below in the priority order shown — an evenly-spread tree is a wasted one.
- k: Save DC = latest class's casting stat + Arcane Acuity
  v: Save-based illithid powers (Black Hole, Mind Blast, Stage Fright…) use the spellcasting modifier of the LAST class you added, and every spell-save-DC bonus (Arcane Acuity, Arcane Enchantment) applies on top. That's why the Commander — CHA 20 plus his Acuity stack — is the best save-power carrier, and why Gale (WIS after the Tempest dip) and Asterion (INT) are not.
- k: Only ONE partial-illithid
  v: The single Astral-Touched Tadpole (from the Emperor at the very start of Act 3) turns exactly one character into a partial-illithid — the only way to reach the ELITE ring (Black Hole, Mind Blast, Freecast, Mind Sanctuary, Illithid Expertise…). It's offered to the Dark Urge first but can be handed to a companion with a Persuasion check. Everyone else is capped at the inner + middle rings.
- k: Partial-illithid freebies
  v: Becoming partial-illithid instantly grants Concentrated Blast, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Psionic Overload, Transfuse Health and Fly — and refunds any tadpoles already spent on those five inner powers, so early spends on them are effectively free.
- k: Resonance Stone doubles psychic
  v: 'Keep the Resonance Stone on the target and every psychic illithid power hits double: Concentrated Blast (3d6 → 6d6), Psionic Overload''s +1d4/hit, Mind Blast, and Cull the Weak''s AoE.'
- k: The only cost
  v: Partial ceremorphosis is a permanent cosmetic change (dark eyes, black veins; reverts only in the epilogue) and draws a few companion disapproval lines. No mechanical downside — fine for this min-max party.
carrier:
  who: Bonbon (the Bard / Commander)
  why: 'Give the Astral-Touched Tadpole to the Commander. His Arcane Acuity engine (Slashing-Flourish hits → +spell save DC) stacks onto illithid save DCs, and his last class is Bard (CHA 20) — so Black Hole and Mind Blast land at the party''s highest possible DC. Black Hole pulls a whole pack into one cluster (INT save at that sky-high DC) → the rest of the party AoEs the ball: Gale''s Wet-doubled Lightning Bolt, Charles''s cleave, the Commander''s own bonus-action Command/Hold via the Band of the Mystic Scoundrel.'
  alt: 'Thematic alternative: let Charles (the Dark Urge) take it — he''s auto-offered it and also has CHA 20, so his Mind Blast / Black Hole are still strong. But Charles''s #1 power, Luck of the Far Realms, is middle-ring and needs no transformation, so he loses almost nothing by NOT being the carrier. Recommendation stands: the Commander eats it, Charles stays free.'
order:
- step: 1
  act: Act 1
  who: Charles
  power: Favourable Beginnings → Luck of the Far Realms
  why: 'Two tadpoles for the single biggest early spike: once per long rest, turn a hit into a crit → doubled Divine Smite + Savage Attacker rerolls + a GWM bonus swing. Save it for the opening smite.'
- step: 2
  act: Act 1
  who: Asterion
  power: Favourable Beginnings → Luck of the Far Realms
  why: 'Same combo for the Trickster: guarantee a crit on the opening Sneak Attack → doubled sneak dice. Favourable Beginnings also gives +proficiency to that first shot.'
- step: 3
  act: Act 1
  who: Bonbon
  power: Favourable Beginnings
  why: +proficiency to his first ranged attack each fight helps land the opening Slashing Flourish that starts the Acuity ramp. Refunded at his Act-3 transformation, so it costs nothing net.
- step: 4
  act: Act 1–2
  who: Charles & Asterion
  power: Psionic Overload
  why: +1d4 psychic per hit, DOUBLED by the Resonance Stone, across every attack of a multi-attacker. The self 1d4/turn is trivial behind Aura of Protection or a big HP pool.
- step: 5
  act: Act 2
  who: Charles
  power: Concentrated Blast → Cull the Weak
  why: Cull the Weak turns any low-HP enemy into a free kill plus a psychic AoE (doubled by Resonance) — great on the frontline where enemies cluster around him.
- step: 6
  act: Act 2
  who: Bonbon
  power: Luck of the Far Realms; Transfuse Health → Shield of Thralls
  why: Luck sets up Illithid Expertise post-transformation; Transfuse Health → Shield of Thralls is the prerequisite chain for Freecast. Both inner picks are refunded at transformation — bank the tadpoles for the Act-3 elite spend.
- step: 7
  act: Act 3 (start)
  who: Bonbon
  power: Commune with the Astral-Touched Tadpole → partial-illithid
  why: Persuade the party to route the Emperor's tadpole to the Commander. Auto-unlocks the five inner powers + Fly and refunds the tadpoles already spent on them, opening the elite ring.
- step: 8
  act: Act 3
  who: Bonbon
  power: Displace → Black Hole
  why: THE payoff. Force Tunnel is already free from the transformation → one tadpole to Displace, one to Black Hole. Pull the pack into a cluster at his Acuity-boosted INT-save DC.
- step: 9
  act: Act 3
  who: Bonbon
  power: Mind Blast
  why: No prerequisite. A 4d8 + CHA psychic cone that Stuns on a failed INT save at his huge DC — an opening-turn AoE lockdown, and psychic so the Resonance Stone doubles it.
- step: 10
  act: Act 3
  who: Bonbon
  power: Illithid Expertise; Freecast
  why: 'Illithid Expertise (via Luck) grants Expertise in Persuasion / Deception / Intimidation — piled onto the party face. Freecast (via Shield of Thralls) makes his next spell free: a no-slot 6th-level Command (6 targets) or Hold Monster.'
- step: 11
  act: Act 3
  who: Asterion & Charles
  power: Cull the Weak / top-ups
  why: Spend leftover parasites on Cull the Weak for the finishers and any missing inner-ring psychic powers. Gale generally donates his tadpoles here — see his row.
