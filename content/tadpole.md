title: Tadpole / Illithid Powers plan
sub: Transform ALL FOUR into partial-illithids (COMMUNE, never eat). Split into PRE- and POST-Astral-Touched, because transforming refunds the inner-ring tadpoles.
intro: 'Two rules shape everything. (1) COMMUNE with the Emperor''s Astral-Touched Tadpole instead of eating it — communing leaves the tadpole intact so every eligible party member can use it in turn; eating consumes it (one character only). So the whole squad becomes partial-illithids and each one gets Fly + the five inner-ring powers for free. (2) Transforming REFUNDS every tadpole a character spent on those five inner powers (but NOT on the ten middle-ring powers) — which is why the plan splits in two: PRE-transformation (Acts 1–2) you spend freely on inner powers (they come back) and only the middle powers you need now; POST-transformation (Act 3) the refund windfall funds the elite ring. Two damage multipliers drive the party: the Resonance Stone makes targets Vulnerable to Psychic (doubling Psionic Overload / Mind Blast / Concentrated Blast on whoever fights in its aura), and Perilous Stakes makes a chosen boss Vulnerable to ALL damage (doubling the melee''s entire smite/flurry nova). Mind the late-game foes that resist/ignore psychic.'
mechanics:
- k: Transform EVERYONE — commune, never eat
  v: 'When the Emperor hands over the Astral-Touched Tadpole (start of Act 3), choose COMMUNE ("open your mind"), not Eat. Communing transforms the user AND leaves the tadpole for the next party member; eating destroys it (only one transforms). All four commune → all four are partial-illithids, each gaining Fly + Concentrated Blast + Favourable Beginnings + Force Tunnel + Psionic Overload + Transfuse Health for free.'
- k: The refund = why there are two phases
  v: 'Communing REFUNDS any tadpoles a character already spent on the FIVE inner powers (Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health). It does NOT refund the ten middle-ring powers. So PRE-transformation the inner powers are effectively free (grab them early for Act 1–2), while middle/elite are real spends.'
- k: Save DC = NEWEST class''s casting stat + Arcane Acuity
  v: 'Illithid save DCs use the spellcasting-ability modifier of the class you most recently reached LEVEL 1 in — but Fighter and Rogue are IGNORED (no casting stat unless Eldritch Knight / Arcane Trickster). Plus every spell-save-DC bonus (Arcane Acuity). Result: Bonbon = CHA 20 + Acuity → DC ~27 (premier carrier); Charles = CHA 20 (Paladin) → DC ~17; Asterion = WIS (his newest countable class is MONK, since Thief-Rogue is ignored) → DC ~16 at WIS 18 — a REAL INT-save carrier, NOT the dead INT it looks like; Gale = WIS 12 → DC ~13 after the Tempest dip, so keep him to no-save picks.'
- k: Awakened (Zaith''isk, Act 1) → give to BONBON
  v: 'The Crèche Zaith''isk grants ONE character the permanent Awakened passive: all Illithid powers cost a BONUS ACTION instead of an Action (mandatory — you can no longer use them as an Action). Give it to Bonbon: it lets him weapon-attack (stacking Acuity) on his Action and fire Perilous Stakes / a power on his Bonus. Honest cost: it collides with his Band-of-the-Mystic-Scoundrel Command loop, so on a control turn he Commands and on a setup turn he Perilous-Stakes — Mind Sanctuary is his release valve (swap Action/Bonus). NOT Asterion (his bonus action is Flurry/Step, and his best pick Psionic Overload is a passive). Grab it in Act 1 (missable): sit in the Zaith''isk, pass INT DC 12 → WIS DC 15 → CON DC 18 (save-scum in non-Honour; a failed save is −2 to that stat but is cured by consuming any tadpole).'
- k: Eligibility
  v: 'Only tadpole-infected characters can use the Astral-Touched Tadpole. Withers HIRELINGS are NOT eligible — this only works for Bonbon if Bonbon is Alondra''s custom player character, not a Withers hireling (confirm in-game). Halsin/Jaheira are never eligible (not in this party). Companions (Asterion, Gale) may need an approval/persuasion nudge; the player characters just choose.'
- k: Perilous Stakes = the damage multiplier
  v: 'A MIDDLE-ring power (prereq Transfuse Health, refunded → net 1 tadpole, online in Act 1–2). INT save; non-Honour it targets ENEMIES. It makes the target Vulnerable to ALL damage for 3 turns — doubling everything the party does to it (piercing pike, radiant smite, psychic), not just psychic. Downside: the target heals 2d8 when IT attacks — so cast it on a Bard-Held or Monk-Stunned boss that can''t attack, and it becomes pure upside. Put it on Bonbon (DC ~27); Asterion is backup.'
- k: Resonance Stone — psychic-double AND mental-save disadvantage
  v: 'Its Steeped in Bliss aura (9m) makes creatures Vulnerable to Psychic AND gives them DISADVANTAGE on mental saves (INT/WIS/CHA). So on whoever fights in it: psychic doubles (Psionic Overload +1d4/hit, Concentrated Blast 3d6→6d6, Mind Blast, Cull''s AoE) AND Asterion''s INT-save nukes (Black Hole, Mind Blast, Perilous Stakes) land far more often. Asterion (monk) carries it. ⚠ Undead & constructs are IMMUNE to the aura, and the Stone is bugged to sometimes STOP WORKING after Act 2 — so don''t hard-depend on the doubling/disadvantage in Act 3; Perilous Stakes (its own vulnerability) does not need the Stone.'
- k: Watch-outs
  v: 'Psychic is widely resisted/immune late (undead, mind flayers, several Act 3 foes) — toggle Asterion''s Manifestation to Soul (radiant) or Body (necrotic) there and don''t lean on psychic vs them. Cull the Weak is minor (kill threshold = your power count, single-digit HP; AoE only 1d4) and its kills do NOT trigger on-kill gear (Asterion''s Deathstalker invisibility / Elixir of Bloodlust won''t fire) — treat it as flavour, not a plan. Freecast disables if you equip/unequip a RANGED weapon (relevant to Bonbon''s crossbows) — don''t weapon-swap the turn you use it. Middle-ring powers are never refunded, so the pool is finite: prioritise Perilous Stakes, Black Hole, and Luck over filler.'
- k: Fun / niche combos
  v: 'Repulsor (2d6 force + 6m knockback, short rest) + Displace (+1d8 psychic on fall damage you cause) off a ledge = chasm instakills that bypass HP — cheap (Force Tunnel is refunded) and everyone now has Fly to set it up. Black Hole can be recast up to 5 MORE times after the first — repeatedly yank a pack into one kill-zone. Displacer Beast Shape is an 85-HP panic tank (keeps your mental scores) — a real "oh no" button for squishy Gale. Psionic Dominance is a free 1/long-rest counterspell-lite (cancels a spell targeting you of level ≤ your prof bonus).'
- k: Parasite economy
  v: 'Parasites are plentiful — roughly two dozen across the run (Act 1: Grove library, ×3 at the Crèche Zaith''isk, ×1 Grove raid, ×1 Nere; more in Act 2 at Moonrise + the Colony; plus every infected cultist you kill, Gifts from the Absolute if you side with them, and the Mind Flayer Brain from Feed the Mind Flayer). With the inner-ring refund on top, that comfortably transforms all four AND funds Bonbon''s elite tree — but middle-ring spends never come back, so don''t fritter them on filler.'
- k: The tree (reference)
  v: 'INNER (free/refunded on transform): Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health (+ Fly auto). MIDDLE (not refunded): Stage Fright & Ability Drain (←Psionic Overload), Luck of the Far Realms & Charm (←Favourable Beginnings), Displace & Repulsor (←Force Tunnel), Psionic Backlash & Cull the Weak (←Concentrated Blast), Shield of Thralls & Perilous Stakes (←Transfuse Health). ELITE (partial-illithid only): Mind Blast (no prereq), Black Hole (←Displace/Psionic Dominance), Freecast (←Shield of Thralls/Mind Sanctuary), Mind Sanctuary (←Psionic Backlash/Freecast), Illithid Expertise (←Luck/Fracture Psyche), Fracture Psyche (←Ability Drain/Illithid Expertise), Psionic Dominance (←Charm/Black Hole), Absorb Intellect & Displacer Beast Shape.'
