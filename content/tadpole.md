title: Tadpole / Illithid Powers plan
sub: Turn ALL FOUR into partial-illithids — COMMUNE, never eat. Split PRE/POST Astral-Touched, because transforming refunds the inner-ring tadpoles.
intro: 'Two rules. (1) COMMUNE with the Emperor''s Astral-Touched Tadpole — communing leaves it intact for the next member, eating consumes it and only one character transforms. All four commune → all four get Fly + the five inner powers free. (2) Transforming REFUNDS tadpoles spent on those five inner powers, but NOT the ten middle-ring ones — hence PRE (Acts 1–2, buy only inner powers that help now) and POST (Act 3, refunds fund the elite ring). Vulnerability NEVER stacks: Perilous Stakes adds nothing to Stone psychic or Wet lightning/cold, but shines on radiant smites and Asterion''s bludgeoning/force.'
mechanics:
- k: Transform EVERYONE — commune, never eat
  v: 'Act 3 start, the Emperor hands it over: choose COMMUNE ("open your mind"), not Eat. Communing transforms you AND leaves the tadpole for the next member; eating destroys it. All four → Fly + Concentrated Blast + Favourable Beginnings + Force Tunnel + Psionic Overload + Transfuse Health, free.'
- k: The refund = why there are two phases
  v: Communing refunds tadpoles spent on the FIVE inner powers (Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health) — NOT the ten middle-ring ones. Inner powers end up free but tie up specimens until Act 3, so buy only what helps in Acts 1–2. Middle and elite spends are permanent.
- k: Save DC = NEWEST class's casting stat + Arcane Acuity
  v: 'DCs use the casting stat of the class you most recently reached LEVEL 1 in; Fighter and Rogue are IGNORED (no casting stat unless Eldritch Knight / Arcane Trickster). Spell-save-DC bonuses apply. At level 12 — Bonbon CHA 20 → 17, +2 per Acuity hit up to 27 · Charles CHA 20 → 17, or 18 in Helldusk Gloves · Asterion WIS 18 → 17, with Stone disadvantage on nearby enemies · Gale WIS 12 → 13 after the Tempest dip.'
- k: Awakened (Zaith'isk, Act 1) → give to BONBON
  v: 'The Crèche Zaith''isk grants ONE character permanent Awakened — all Illithid powers become a BONUS ACTION instead of an Action (mandatory, not optional). Bonbon: weapon attacks stack Acuity, then his bonus action fires Perilous Stakes or Mind Blast at the party''s best DC. It competes with the Band/Command loop, so pick the control package per encounter; Mind Sanctuary is the team-scale release valve. NOT Asterion — his bonus actions are Flurries/Step. ⚠ MISSABLE: sit in it during Act 1 and pass INT 12 → WIS 15 → CON 18 (save-scum in non-Honour; a failure is −2 to that stat, cured by consuming any tadpole).'
- k: Eligibility
  v: 'Only infected characters can use the Astral-Touched Tadpole. Withers HIRELINGS are not eligible — this works for Bonbon only as Alondra''s custom PC (confirm in-game). Halsin/Jaheira never qualify. Companions (Asterion, Gale) may need an approval nudge; PCs just choose.'
- k: Perilous Stakes = the damage multiplier
  v: 'MIDDLE ring (prereq Transfuse Health, refunded → net 1 tadpole; online Acts 1–2). INT save, and non-Honour it targets ENEMIES. Vulnerable to all damage for 3 turns — but vulnerability does not stack, so nothing for Stone psychic or Wet lightning/cold. It doubles the unmatched types: radiant smites, bludgeoning, force. The target heals 2d8 when IT attacks, so land it with Bonbon''s ~27 DC on a Held/Stunned boss.'
- k: Resonance Stone — psychic-double AND mental-save disadvantage
  v: 'Steeped in Bliss (9m) = Vulnerable to Psychic + DISADVANTAGE on INT/WIS/CHA saves. Asterion carries it to double Manifestation of Mind and Psionic Overload. Black Hole''s pull needs no save; its secondary INT Slow save is rolled at disadvantage inside the aura against his DC 17. Bonbon''s Acuity DC does not depend on it, and Perilous Stakes cannot re-double the psychic. ⚠ Undead and constructs are IMMUNE, and the Stone is bugged to sometimes stop working after Act 2 — Black Hole still pulls without it.'
- k: Watch-outs
  v: 'Psychic is widely resisted or immune late (undead, mind flayers, several Act 3 foes) — switch Asterion''s Manifestation to Soul (radiant) or Body (necrotic). Cull the Weak is good mob cleanup, but its kills do NOT trigger Deathstalker invisibility or Bloodlust — toggle it off when an on-kill proc matters. Freecast disables if you equip/unequip a RANGED weapon (Bonbon''s crossbows). Middle-ring spends never return, so prioritise Bonbon''s Perilous Stakes, Asterion''s Black Hole path, Charles''s Luck, and each caster''s Freecast path.'
- k: Fun / niche combos
  v: 'Repulsor (2d6 force + 6m knockback, short rest) + Displace (+1d8 psychic on fall damage you cause) off a ledge = chasm instakills that bypass HP — cheap, and everyone has Fly to set it up. Black Hole recasts 5 MORE times, so keep yanking a pack into one kill-zone. Displacer Beast Shape is an 85-HP panic tank that keeps your mental scores — the button for squishy Gale. Psionic Dominance is a free 1/long-rest counterspell-lite (spells targeting you of level ≤ your proficiency bonus).'
- k: Parasite economy
  v: 'Roughly two dozen across the run — Act 1: Grove library, ×3 at the Zaith''isk, ×1 Grove raid, ×1 Nere; Act 2: Moonrise + the Colony; plus infected cultists you kill, Gifts from the Absolute, and the Mind Flayer Brain. Transformation itself costs no specimen. Inner-ring refunds fund Bonbon''s elite tree, but middle-ring spends never return — don''t duplicate expensive active powers without an action-economy reason.'
- k: The tree (reference)
  v: 'INNER (free/refunded on transform): Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health (+ Fly auto). MIDDLE (not refunded): Stage Fright & Ability Drain (←Psionic Overload), Luck of the Far Realms & Charm (←Favourable Beginnings), Displace & Repulsor (←Force Tunnel), Psionic Backlash & Cull the Weak (←Concentrated Blast), Shield of Thralls & Perilous Stakes (←Transfuse Health). ELITE (partial-illithid only): Mind Blast (no prereq), Black Hole (←Displace/Psionic Dominance), Freecast (←Shield of Thralls/Mind Sanctuary), Mind Sanctuary (←Psionic Backlash/Freecast), Illithid Expertise (←Luck/Fracture Psyche), Fracture Psyche (←Ability Drain/Illithid Expertise), Psionic Dominance (←Charm/Black Hole), Absorb Intellect & Displacer Beast Shape.'
