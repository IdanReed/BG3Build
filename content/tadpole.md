intro: 'Three rules. (1) COMMUNE with the Emperor''s Astral-Touched Tadpole — communing leaves it intact for the next member, eating consumes it and only one character transforms. All four commune → all four get Fly + the five inner powers free. (2) Transforming REFUNDS tadpoles spent on those five inner powers, but NOT the ten middle-ring ones — hence PRE (Acts 1–2: buy the inner powers that help now, plus the gates for the middle powers you want early) and POST (Act 3: refunds fund the elite ring). (3) Passives, toggles and reactions cost no action, so every S/A one goes to every character who can trigger it; Action-cost powers go to the one character whose bonus action is empty — GALE, who takes Awakened. Vulnerability NEVER stacks: Perilous Stakes adds nothing to Stone psychic or Wet lightning/cold, but doubles radiant smites and Asterion''s bludgeoning/force.'

- k: The refund = why there are two phases
  v: 'Communing REFUNDS any tadpoles a character already spent on the FIVE inner powers (Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health). It does NOT refund the ten middle-ring powers. Inner powers are eventually free but still tie up specimens until Act 3, so in Acts 1–2 buy only the ones that materially help now or gate a middle power you want early; middle/elite powers remain real spends.'
- k: Save DC = NEWEST class''s casting stat + Arcane Acuity
  v: '✅ CONFIRMED on the wiki that gear applies: "All bonuses to spell save DC such as Arcane Acuity or Arcane Enchantment will also apply to illithid powers." That is what puts Gale and Bonbon at DC 27 and makes save-based powers worth buying for both. Illithid save DCs use the spellcasting-ability modifier of the class you most recently reached LEVEL 1 in — but Fighter and Rogue are IGNORED (no casting stat unless Eldritch Knight / Arcane Trickster). At level 12: Gale = CHA 20 → DC 17, because Fiend Warlock at character level 7 is his newest level-1 class — and the Hat of Fire Acuity pushes that to DC 27; Bonbon = CHA 20 → DC 17 base, then +2 per Helmet-of-Arcane-Acuity weapon hit up to DC 27; Charles = CHA 20 → DC 17, about 19–21 in practice on Gloves of Battlemage''s Power stacks; Asterion = WIS 20 because Monk is his newest countable class → DC 17 (8 + proficiency 4 + WIS 5), with nearby enemies disadvantaged by his Resonance Stone.'
- k: Awakened (Zaith''isk, Act 1) → give to GALE
  v: 'The Crèche Zaith''isk grants ONE character the permanent Awakened passive: all Illithid powers cost a BONUS ACTION instead of an Action (mandatory — you can no longer use them as an Action). GALE takes it, because his bonus action is the only empty one in the party — about one Quickened Scorching Ray per fight, never Hasted — while every Action of his is Twinned Haste, Scorching Ray, Fireball or Command. Awakened turns that idle bonus action into Black Hole every turn (six casts per short rest), Perilous Stakes at DC 27 on the boss, Stage Fright when a mob threatens Haste, Force Tunnel when melee reaches him, and a Shield of Thralls recast on Charles. NOT Bonbon: her three Awakened powers were once per long rest and each fought the Band of the Mystic Scoundrel for the same bonus action, so Awakened fired about three times a day. NOT Asterion (bonus actions are Flurries and Step of the Wind). NOT Charles (Hexblade''s Curse and Inquisitor''s Might).'
