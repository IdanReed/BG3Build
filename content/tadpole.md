title: Tadpole / Illithid Powers plan
sub: Transform ALL FOUR into partial-illithids (COMMUNE, never eat). Split into PRE- and POST-Astral-Touched, because transforming refunds the inner-ring tadpoles.
intro: 'Two rules shape everything. (1) COMMUNE with the Emperor''s Astral-Touched Tadpole instead of eating it — communing leaves the tadpole intact so every eligible party member can use it in turn; eating consumes it (one character only). So the whole squad becomes partial-illithids and each one gets Fly + the five inner-ring powers for free. (2) Transforming REFUNDS every tadpole a character spent on those five inner powers (but NOT on the ten middle-ring powers) — which is why the plan splits in two: PRE-transformation (Acts 1–2) take only inner powers that help now; POST-transformation (Act 3) their refunds fund the elite ring. The Resonance Stone makes targets Vulnerable to Psychic, while Perilous Stakes makes a chosen boss Vulnerable to all damage. Vulnerability NEVER stacks: Stakes doubles only damage types that are not already doubled by the Stone or Wet. It is strongest for radiant smites and Asterion''s bludgeoning/force damage, not as another multiplier on psychic or lightning/cold.'
mechanics:
- k: Transform EVERYONE — commune, never eat
  v: 'When the Emperor hands over the Astral-Touched Tadpole (start of Act 3), choose COMMUNE ("open your mind"), not Eat. Communing transforms the user AND leaves the tadpole for the next party member; eating destroys it (only one transforms). All four commune → all four are partial-illithids, each gaining Fly + Concentrated Blast + Favourable Beginnings + Force Tunnel + Psionic Overload + Transfuse Health for free.'
- k: The refund = why there are two phases
  v: 'Communing REFUNDS any tadpoles a character already spent on the FIVE inner powers (Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health). It does NOT refund the ten middle-ring powers. Inner powers are eventually free but still tie up specimens until Act 3, so buy only the ones that materially help during Acts 1–2; middle/elite powers remain real spends.'
- k: Save DC = NEWEST class''s casting stat + Arcane Acuity
  v: 'Illithid save DCs use the spellcasting-ability modifier of the class you most recently reached LEVEL 1 in — but Fighter and Rogue are IGNORED (no casting stat unless Eldritch Knight / Arcane Trickster). Spell-save-DC bonuses also apply. At level 12: Bonbon = CHA 20 → DC 17 base, then +2 per Helmet-of-Arcane-Acuity weapon hit up to DC 27; Charles = CHA 20 → DC 17, or 18 in Helldusk Gloves; Asterion = WIS 18 because Monk is his newest countable class → DC 17, with nearby enemies disadvantaged by his Resonance Stone; Gale = WIS 12 → DC 13 after the Tempest dip.'
- k: Awakened (Zaith''isk, Act 1) → give to BONBON
  v: 'The Crèche Zaith''isk grants ONE character the permanent Awakened passive: all Illithid powers cost a BONUS ACTION instead of an Action (mandatory — you can no longer use them as an Action). Give it to Bonbon: weapon attacks stack Arcane Acuity, then his Bonus Action fires Perilous Stakes or Mind Blast at the party''s best DC. Those powers compete with the Band-of-the-Mystic-Scoundrel Command loop, so choose the right control package for the encounter; Mind Sanctuary is the team-scale release valve. NOT Asterion (his bonus actions are Flurries/Step). Grab it in Act 1 (missable): sit in the Zaith''isk, pass INT DC 12 → WIS DC 15 → CON DC 18 (save-scum in non-Honour; a failed save is −2 to that stat but is cured by consuming any tadpole).'
- k: Eligibility
  v: 'Only tadpole-infected characters can use the Astral-Touched Tadpole. Withers HIRELINGS are NOT eligible — this only works for Bonbon if Bonbon is Alondra''s custom player character, not a Withers hireling (confirm in-game). Halsin/Jaheira are never eligible (not in this party). Companions (Asterion, Gale) may need an approval/persuasion nudge; the player characters just choose.'
- k: Perilous Stakes = the damage multiplier
  v: 'A MIDDLE-ring power (prereq Transfuse Health, refunded → net 1 tadpole, online in Act 1–2). INT save; non-Honour it targets ENEMIES. It makes the target Vulnerable to all damage for 3 turns, but vulnerability does NOT stack: it adds nothing to psychic already covered by the Resonance Stone or lightning/cold already covered by Wet. It still doubles unmatched damage such as radiant smites and Asterion''s bludgeoning/force. The target heals 2d8 when IT attacks, so use Bonbon''s ~27 DC and prefer a Held/Stunned boss that cannot attack.'
- k: Resonance Stone — psychic-double AND mental-save disadvantage
  v: 'Its Steeped in Bliss aura (9m) makes creatures Vulnerable to Psychic AND gives them DISADVANTAGE on mental saves (INT/WIS/CHA). Asterion carries it to double Manifestation of Mind and Psionic Overload across his many hits. His Black Hole pull needs no save; enemies in the aura make the secondary INT save against Slow with disadvantage at his DC 17. Bonbon''s Arcane-Acuity DC does not depend on the Stone. Perilous Stakes does not double the psychic again. ⚠ Undead & constructs are IMMUNE to the aura, and the Stone is bugged to sometimes STOP WORKING after Act 2; Black Hole still pulls without it.'
- k: Watch-outs
  v: 'Psychic is widely resisted/immune late (undead, mind flayers, several Act 3 foes) — toggle Asterion''s Manifestation to Soul (radiant) or Body (necrotic) there and don''t lean on psychic vs them. Cull the Weak scales with the owner''s evolved-power count and is useful for Asterion''s mob cleanup, but its kills do NOT trigger Deathstalker invisibility or Bloodlust; toggle it off when an on-kill proc matters. Freecast disables if you equip/unequip a RANGED weapon (relevant to Bonbon''s crossbows) — don''t weapon-swap the turn you use it. Middle-ring powers are never refunded, so prioritise Bonbon''s Perilous Stakes, Asterion''s Black Hole path, Charles''s Luck, and each caster''s Freecast path.'
- k: Fun / niche combos
  v: 'Repulsor (2d6 force + 6m knockback, short rest) + Displace (+1d8 psychic on fall damage you cause) off a ledge = chasm instakills that bypass HP — cheap (Force Tunnel is refunded) and everyone now has Fly to set it up. Black Hole can be recast up to 5 MORE times after the first — repeatedly yank a pack into one kill-zone. Displacer Beast Shape is an 85-HP panic tank (keeps your mental scores) — a real "oh no" button for squishy Gale. Psionic Dominance is a free 1/long-rest counterspell-lite (cancels a spell targeting you of level ≤ your prof bonus).'
- k: Parasite economy
  v: 'Parasites are plentiful — roughly two dozen across the run (Act 1: Grove library, ×3 at the Crèche Zaith''isk, ×1 Grove raid, ×1 Nere; more in Act 2 at Moonrise + the Colony; plus every infected cultist you kill, Gifts from the Absolute if you side with them, and the Mind Flayer Brain from Feed the Mind Flayer). Transformation itself costs no regular specimen. The inner-ring refunds help fund Bonbon''s elite controller tree, but middle-ring spends never come back, so don''t duplicate expensive active powers without a clear action-economy reason.'
- k: The tree (reference)
  v: 'INNER (free/refunded on transform): Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health (+ Fly auto). MIDDLE (not refunded): Stage Fright & Ability Drain (←Psionic Overload), Luck of the Far Realms & Charm (←Favourable Beginnings), Displace & Repulsor (←Force Tunnel), Psionic Backlash & Cull the Weak (←Concentrated Blast), Shield of Thralls & Perilous Stakes (←Transfuse Health). ELITE (partial-illithid only): Mind Blast (no prereq), Black Hole (←Displace/Psionic Dominance), Freecast (←Shield of Thralls/Mind Sanctuary), Mind Sanctuary (←Psionic Backlash/Freecast), Illithid Expertise (←Luck/Fracture Psyche), Fracture Psyche (←Ability Drain/Illithid Expertise), Psionic Dominance (←Charm/Black Hole), Absorb Intellect & Displacer Beast Shape.'
