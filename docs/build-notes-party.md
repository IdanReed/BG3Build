# Party build notes: loot route and party plan

Written 2026-09-11 while restyling `content/loot.md`, `content/party.md`, `content/meta.md` and `content/tadpole.md` into cheat sheets. Everything below is reasoning that used to live in those files, plus the report's reasoning for the party-wide decisions of `cephalopocalypse-build-review-2026-09-11.md` (the "report"). The content files say WHAT; this file says WHY. Anchors are the loot row `name` or the party.md field, in bold.

Party after the review: Charles (Vengeance Paladin 6 / Hexblade Warlock 6), Asterion (Open Hand Monk 9 / Thief Rogue 3), Gale (Draconic Red Sorcerer 11 / Fiend Warlock 1), Bonbon (Swords Bard 11 / Fighter 1). Non-Honour, Patch 8, modded Hag's Hair.

## 1. Party-wide decisions applied (report IDs)

**T4-V6 Mirror of Loss is not guaranteed.** One DC 25 Religion check per character to pray; a failure locks that character out of the +3 forever. With the +3 the hidden roll is 3–7: 20% useless memory, 20% Patriar's memory, 60% the +2 to a chosen stat. Religion is INT-based and only Charles is proficient, at INT 8. All four end-stat lines (Charles/Bonbon/Gale CHA 20, Asterion WIS 20) assume the +2, so the plan is Enhance Ability from Bonbon, Guidance from the Silver Pendant (the party's only Guidance) and a quicksave before each prayer. Surrendering Forbidden Knowledge (Necromancy of Thay) bypasses the check for one character, at the cost of Danse Macabre. `meta.md` `stat_assumption` carries the clause; every "+2 Mirror" step in the character files gets it too.

**T2-24 Plant Growth under Hunger of Hadar.** Gale's Armour of Landfall grants Plant Growth once per short rest. Under Charles's Hunger of Hadar the enemies are Blinded (cannot jump) and at quarter speed on Plant Growth, so the zone is a multi-turn, no-save lock with Repelling Blast pushing escapers back. Standing rule: no Fireball, Scorching Ray or Heat into the zone, because fire burns Plant Growth away. Gale's fire goes at targets outside; Charles and Bonbon work the inside. Bonbon's own Plant Growth (char 9 pick, T2-5) is cast between the pack and the back line, never on the melee cluster, and layers under the same Hunger of Hadar. Her Silence goes over Hunger of Hadar on non-Holdable fights (T3-B12): her Hold lane is idle exactly then, and Misty Step and Dimension Door are verbal.

**T2-25 Phalar Aluve: always Shriek.** Sing gives allies within 6 m +1d4 to attack rolls and ALL saves for 5 turns (the "mental" tooltip is wrong); Shriek is −1d4 to enemy saves and +1d4 Thunder per damage instance. The report leaned to a per-fight call; the decision is Shriek every time and no Sing text anywhere. Bonbon carries it from the Stone, unchanged.

**T2-21 Arsonist's Oil vulnerability.** Make the target fire-resistant with a thrown Elixir of Fire Resistance, hit it with an Arsonist's-Oil weapon (Bonbon's Flourish covers two targets per attack; the coating lasts ten turns and strips resistance for 2 turns), then throw a second elixir. The resistance overwrite leaves the target Vulnerable to fire: ~175 → ~350 on a 7-ray Scorching Ray. Wiki-confirmed. Two elixirs and a Bonbon bonus action per target; an exploit, kept because this is a non-Honour run. Boss turns only. Arsonist's Oil is crafted from a Sublimate of Frosted Ears + any Ashes (25 gp); no reliable vendor is recorded.

**T3-P1 Stealth opener.** Enemies Surprised by an attack from Stealth skip their first turn, which is a free Hold window. Hide outside vision cones without a roll; release Shift so Shift+Hide does not hide the whole party and drag Charles's Stealth into a check (T3-A8). Gale pre-casts Twinned Haste before initiative when the fight is visible (T3-G1): Hastened lasts 10 turns, so his turn-1 Action goes to a second Scorching Ray. Ambushes keep the old line.

**T3-C6 Radiant Shockwave spares allies.** Luminous Armour's shockwave hits all non-allied creatures, neutrals included. Asterion can stand on the Held target; neutral NPCs in a fight need watching.

**T4-V5 Paralysed auto-crits within 3 m.** Any attack within 3 m of a Paralysed target is an automatic crit, not only melee. party.md now says "within 3 m". Gale within 3 m of a Held target fires 5–7 crits with advantage (~+55 to +80 per cast). Caveat for play: whether ranged spell attacks inside an enemy's reach take Threatened disadvantage (advantage from Paralysed would cancel it). Bhaalist Armour (T2-9) uses the same 3 m.

**T4-V3 Counterspell against a higher-level spell rolls INT.** Live wiki bug; both carriers have INT 8. Charles's L3 pact-slot Counterspell is a 20–30% roll against 4th+ level spells; Gale upcasts from a Sorcerer slot to remove the check. Character-file edits only; it raises the value of Gale's Psionic Dominance.

**T4-V2 Darkness advantage only against enemies without darkvision.** Attacking from Shadows needs the target to lack darkvision (or be beyond its range). Goblins, gnolls, duergar, drow and Shadow-Cursed creatures all see him. The cloud's value is the ranged block both ways, Blinded enemies inside it, and advantage against the darkvision-less minority. From Act 2 the Risky Ring supplies advantage anyway, and Shadow Blade has its own advantage against Obscured targets (T3-C5). The loot rows **Arrows of Darkness**, **Phalar Aluve** and **Gloves of the Growling Underdog** were reworded; the Underdog gloves are now his Act 1 advantage source rather than a Darkness fallback.

**T4-V8 Amulet of the Devout reason.** The old row said the amulet "only works with Channel Divinity". Only the extra charge does; the +2 spell save DC applies to anyone. The skip still stands (see §3), the sentence did not.

**Cross-file facts the party files now carry.** Charles's roster line is Paladin 6 / Hexblade Warlock 6 (T1-3: char 12 is Warlock 6 for Accursed Spectre and a 7th spell known, costing one L2 Paladin slot and Relentless Avenger; Aura of Protection still Paladin 6 = char 11). Asterion's spikes are Fast Hands char 9, Alert char 11, Ki Resonation char 12 (T1-1: every video with this chassis goes Monk 6 → Rogue tail → Monk 7–9; Fast Hands three levels earlier is two more Tavern Brawler punches a turn through the Stone stretch and early Act 3; Alert slips from 9 to 11, Evasion 8 to 10). Aura of Protection must be cast once to activate and is removed by a respec, so char 8 and again char 11 (T3-C2). Hexblade's Curse goes on the nova target during the set-up turn (T3-C4; T4-U1 below). Asterion's racial +2 and Hag's Hair go to WIS (T2-26: DEX 16, 18 under the Cloth; WIS 17 → 18 → 20; AC identical; +1 Psychic per hit from Manifestation of Mind, doubled by the Stone, and +1 per hit from Kushigo; costs −1 initiative, DEX saves, Sleight of Hand and Stealth). Asterion ASCENDS (T2-27). Athletics moves to Asterion at Rogue 1 (T3-A4: +9 to +12 on the elixir, the party's best Shove number; Insight is covered by Gale and Bonbon), so `skills_face` lists him beside Bonbon.

## 2. Bonbon's gear changes that moved loot rows

**T1-2 The Dead Shot in Act 3, Titanstring until then.** Four videos. The Dead Shot is +2 and Keen Attack doubles proficiency on its ranged attacks, so at char 9+ it is +5 to hit over Titanstring, which cancels Sharpshooter's −5 outright, and it crits on 19 (Improved Critical is holder-scoped and stacks). Damage per hit drops ~7 (the Cloud Giant rider). Against AC 18: 75% × 27.5 = 20.6 expected per shot vs 50% × 34.5 = 17.3; on a Hasted 8-projectile turn, 6 hits instead of 4, each an Acuity and Band trigger. Titanstring only wins above ~76% base hit chance, which is the option text ("Bless, Held target"). Keen Attack switches off under disadvantage, so never into Charles's Darkness. One copy exists; Asterion (T3-A2) and Charles (T3-C3) wanted it and lost. The 2026-09-10 §3 decision compared Titanstring only against hand crossbows.

**T2-2 Elixir of Vigilance in Act 3.** Once Titanstring's Strength rider is gone the Giant Strength elixir buys nothing; Vigilance is +5 initiative and Surprise immunity on a d4 initiative roll. With Alert too she is d4+13. This ends the second daily Strength drinker (D17 accepted two), so **Elixir of Cloud Giant Strength** is Asterion's alone and the Bloodlust row no longer says she drinks Giant Strength to the end.

**T2-4 Sharpshooter toggle (Acts 1–2).** The video's "off below 30% displayed" is too low with Titanstring's flat rider: the −5 is worth taking only above ~65% base hit chance in Act 1 (displayed ≥ 40% with it on) and ~85% with Cloud Giant (displayed ≥ 60%); ~10 points lower under advantage. Moot with The Dead Shot.

**T2-1 Alert instead of War Caster (Bard 8, char 9).** Six videos. Her initiative was d4+4, the slowest in the party by 3–8 (Charles d4+7, Gale d4+11, Asterion ~d4+10); Alert makes her d4+9 and Surprise-immune, so Hold Monster lands before the enemy's first turn and she is still Hasted on turn 1 behind Gale. What War Caster protected, a +6 CON save, is mostly covered by Adamantine Splint crit immunity (Acts 1–2), Helldusk's −3 (Act 3), Charles's Aura of Protection: ~85% instead of ~98% on the hard hits. The loot rows that cited "Bonbon has War Caster" (**Cloak of Protection**, **Amulet of Greater Health**) now cite her crit immunity instead.

**T2-3 Sentinel Shield as the default off hand from Moonrise.** The report's lean was "no if Alert", because the party rule is initiative items to the one character without Alert. Accepted anyway: +3 initiative on a d4 roll is near-guaranteed first turn for the Hold Monster carrier, and only one Sentinel Shield exists. Ketheric's Shield (+1 spell save DC, +1 spell attack, DEX-save advantage) becomes the option for fights where +1 DC matters. Both share one caveat: the wiki confirms only that a shield's AC carries over from the inactive melee set, not its other passives (T4-U3). Ketheric's went to Bonbon rather than Charles because the Adamantine Shield's crit immunity is worth more to him than +1 on an Acuity-capped DC. Safeguard Shield is her off hand from the Grove until Moonrise; Fighter 1 gives shield proficiency and a shield needs no Dual Wielder; skipping the Club of Hill Giant Strength is what leaves the hand free.

**T2-8 Snowburst Ring in Act 2 ring 2, Drakethroat set to Cold.** Five videos. Every Cold-damage hit drops a 4.5 m ice circle (2 turns; DEX save vs her spell DC or Prone). Four to eight circles a turn with no slot or concentration, and it works on the undead and constructs that ignore Hold and Command. Cost: ice under the melee cluster is difficult terrain for Charles and Asterion, and Prone ends Charles's Hold Person with no save, so the rule is shoot the back line first. Gale's fire melts it (Ray of Frost re-freezes). Snowburst does not fire on Hunger of Hadar ticks, Armour of Agathys retaliation or thrown weapons. Whether Acuity raises the ice DC is unverified. Act 2 only; Act 3 ring 2 is the Ring of Feywild Sparks. Because Drakethroat is Cold every day, Charles's Shadow Blade also carries +1d4 Cold unless a fight resists it.

**T3-B7 Ring of Feywild Sparks over Caustic Band in Act 3.** Hidden +1 spell save DC (not on the tooltip; `ratings.md` rates it S). +1 DC on Hold Monster beats 8–12 Acid a turn. Auntie Ethel carries it at the Blushing Mermaid. Its Wild Magic clause needs Tides of Chaos, which she lacks. **Caustic Band** stays core for Acts 1–2 (+2 Acid per weapon hit, scaling with Flourish projectiles) and becomes the Act 3 option. The Club of Hill Giant Strength (Arcane Tower stool, STR 19) stays skipped: the Elixir of Hill Giant Strength sets 21 and leaving the Club frees her melee main hand for a shield.

**T2-9 Bhaalist Armour as a bagged nova swap.** Inside 3 m of a Held target every projectile crits and Piercing is doubled (Aura of Murder, 3 m since Patch 8): ≈ 600 vs ≈ 275 on a Hasted 8-projectile turn. ## 3. Questions answered and declines

**Why the Gale dip stays Fiend (T1-5 Hexblade, T1-4 pure Sorcerer, T1-6 Fiend 3, T1-7 early dip).** The dip exists for Command, the party's mass no-concentration disable. Without it, Command would have to come from Charles's Paladin list in Acts 1–2 (costing his Paladin slots and his Acuity build-up) and from Bonbon's Magical Secrets, which only arrive at Bard 10, so Acts 1–2 would have no Command and Act 3 half of it. Hexblade's expanded list is Shield / Wrathful Smite, not Command, and its curse's bonus action collides with Quickened Scorching Ray every turn. Fiend 3 costs Careful Spell, the L6 slot, an L5 slot, two spells known and 2 sorcery points. The char 2 dip slips Scorching Ray, Dual Wielder, Twinned Haste and Elemental Affinity one level each onto the Grymforge and Crèche fights. Vigilance already covers the third feat's best use (Alert). T4-V4 also removed the old worry: Command casts from any Sorcerer slot.

**T2-7's amulet question and T2-30 Amulet of the Devout (declined).** Spellcrux is already Bonbon's Act 3 neck, so casting the Myrmidon first and refunding the slot displaces nothing. The Devout's +2 spell save DC does work on anyone (T4-V8), and it would be live from turn 1 and additive to the Acuity cap; against it, Spellcrux's refunded L6 slot is the Myrmidon refund the plan now depends on, Spineshudder is Gale's neck and Greater Health Charles's, and looting the offering chest curses the looter unless Jaheira wearing Khalid's Gift takes it. Declined; the loot row keeps the sell instruction with the corrected reason.

**Other declines with a party-level footprint.** T2-13 GWM (Savage Attacker's reroll is +120–150 on the nine-swing ceiling; GWM does nothing on a turn with no crit or kill; the bonus action already carries Inquisitor's Might and Hexblade's Curse). T2-15 Great Old One (costs Hexblade's Curse, the heal on kill and Shield). T2-14 Warlock 3 in Act 1. T2-16 Boots of Arcane Bolstering (same slot as the Night Walkers; +2 per damage instance vs Threatened enemies for two turns after a Dash, at Evasive Shoes' +1 AC). T2-20 Alert over Elemental Adept (Vigilance duplicates Alert on Gale). T2-22 Pyroquickness Hat. T2-23 reaction baiting (unverified, T4-U4). T2-29 Luminous Gloves. Asterion Swashbuckler and Monk 8 / Thief 4. T3-A2 / T3-C3 The Dead Shot for Asterion or Charles (one copy, Bonbon's). Tier 5 party items: flat +1 DC pieces lose to items protecting the concentration the DC is cast through; one Strength-elixir drinker is now the case from Act 3; no Cleric level to spare for Guidance / Resistance / Create Water; the "Mirror of Loss cannot be used on both" claim is wrong (per character).

## 4. Loot route rationale by act and area

### Act 1

**Ravaged Beach.** **Silver Pendant**: no Cleric level anywhere in the party, so this is the only Guidance for the whole run and for the Mirror of Loss Religion check; grab it before the Grove.

**Emerald Grove.** **Ring of Protection**: the item guides say lowest AC, which is Gale at AC 15 in Spidersilk against Asterion's 21 unarmoured; the save bonus doubles as Twinned Haste insurance; Asterion takes the Bracing Band at Grymforge and loses nothing. **Safeguard Shield**: not Gale's because Dual Wielder at char 4 fills both hands with staves; Charles takes a plain +2 Shield from any vendor and holds it until the Adamantine Shield is poured. **The Whispering Promise**: applies the same condition as Bless, so it does not stack with Bless and is not enhanced by the Staff of Arcane Blessing; its job is the char 1–3 window; a thrown Potion of Healing blesses everyone it splashes, Bonbon's Healing Word covers one and also fires Broodmother's Revenge; works on a full-HP target. **Hellrider's Pride**: rated #6 of 20 Act 1 items and still a bag item because every glove slot is taken (Bonbon Gloves of Dexterity, Charles Baneful Striking, Asterion Bracers of Defence, Gale Belligerent Skies). **Elixir of Heroism** (T2-12): +1d4 to every attack roll and saving throw until long rest, a distinct condition from Bless so they stack; the tooltip's 10 temp HP is the separate Heroic condition and shares the one-temp-HP-source rule, hence no Armour of Agathys those days. Against a Held target it adds nothing (Bloodlust stays the nova elixir); on undead/construct/crit-immune days +1d4 on every Risky Ring concentration save beats a kill-gated Action. Sold by Mol in the Tiefling Hideout and Talli at Moonrise; crafted from Sublimate of Pegasus Feathers + Ashes.

**Sunlit Wetlands.** **Auntie Ethel's Hair**: vanilla gives one Hair total, the mod one per member; Asterion's goes to WIS now (T2-26). **Get the Hag's Hair** (event): the wiki lists "letting Auntie Ethel get away alive in exchange for her boon" as an Oath of Vengeance break, reported since Patch 8 not to trigger when the Paladin is not the speaker. The old note assumed an Oathbreaker path Charles no longer takes, so the instruction is now that a companion speaks and the player quicksaves. She only bargains in the depths of her lair, not the teahouse, and cannot if Silenced.

**Blighted Village.** **Haste Helm**: not Asterion's because he has Unarmoured Movement plus Step of the Wind and his head is the Circlet of Psionic Revenge; not Gale's because his head is the Shadespell Circlet then the Hat of Fire Acuity; the Ring of Arcane Synergy supplies Charles's damage rider without the head slot.

**Goblin Camp.** **Bow of Awareness**: the old "to land Haste or Wet first" lost the Wet half (T4-V1). **Boots of Striding**: through Act 1 his concentration is only Hex, so Stormy Clamour is the default until Hold Person takes the slot at char 7; Prone ends concentration outright. The old line "after the Resonance respec, self-cast Darkness supplies the Momentum trigger" was dropped: self-cast Darkness evicts Hold Person (Tier 5). **Spidersilk Armour**: costs exactly 1 AC versus unarmoured Draconic Resilience (13 + DEX); Gale has no War Caster and no feat for one, so CON-save advantage is his only concentration protection for two acts; light armour from Human Civil Militia.

**Zhentarim Hideout.** **Titanstring Bow**: the melee set behind it is a passive rack she never swings (Knife + Safeguard Shield, then Phalar Aluve + Sentinel Shield). See §2 for the Dead Shot arithmetic and the Sharpshooter thresholds.

**Underdark.** **Boots of Stormy Clamour**: not Gale's because he has no Reverberation payload until Act 2 and is three times over the 5-stack threshold once he does; Charles converts them properly because Luminous Armour's Radiant Shockwave is a CreateExplosion event that resets the boots' hidden OncePerAttack limit, so Inquisitor's Might Radiant procs Reverberation on every swing. T2-28: Asterion inflicts more conditions per turn than anyone (Stun, Topple Prone, Bleeding from Flawed Helldusk, Ability Drain), each adding 2 turns of Reverberation, so the next Stunning Strike CON save is at −2 to −4; his cost that fight is Night Walkers' Misty Step (Acts 1–2) or Kushigo's +WIS per hit (Act 3, ≈ +20). **Boots of Speed**: #14 of the Act 1 top 20 for Rogue-grade mobility; not Asterion's because Step of the Wind already gives bonus-action Dash and Disengage. **Melf's First Staff**: neither staff is Light, so Dual Wielder is what makes the pair legal; the +1 spell attack applies to every Scorching Ray ray; it does not move to Bonbon in Act 3. **Phalar Aluve**: the old "Darkness Arrows for advantage" went with T4-V2; Shriek's aura sits on the wielder, ends if the sword is unequipped and costs an Action, which is why Bonbon stands 3–6 m out and uses the Haste action. **Staff of Arcane Blessing**: every Bless its wielder casts also applies Mystra's Blessing, a second +1d4 on spell attack rolls only; Asterion is the caster because Charles's concentration is Hold Person and Gale's is Twinned Haste; the free cast is level 1, so three creatures (Charles, Gale, Bonbon). **Elixirs of Bloodlust**: three attacks a turn into Held targets kill something most rounds; the extra Action is another swing or the Hold Person itself. **Ring of Mind-Shielding**: concentration insurance as much as a save.

**Grymforge.** **Disintegrating Night Walkers**: the wiki effect is Misty Step once per short rest and immunity to Enwebbed, Entangled and Ensnared plus no slipping on Grease or Ice. It does NOT list Prone immunity, which the report's T3-G2 line claimed for Gale; the value to Gale is the short-rest Misty Step and never slipping on Snowburst ice, against Evasive Shoes' +1 AC. Night Walker only covers Ensnaring Strike up to level 2, not Grasping Arrow or Black Tentacles. **Adamantine Splint Armour**: crit immunity protects Hold Monster because a concentration save is "DC equal to half the damage taken, or 10, whichever is higher", so a crit roughly doubles the DC, and it stops Hold Person and Sleep from handing attackers automatic crits against her; the second ore is Charles's shield because his melee set is active, unlike a shield parked behind her bow where only the AC carries. **Adamantine Shield**: the Scale Mail this ore used to buy went unworn once Luminous Armour was locked. **Grymskull Helm**: "attackers can't land Critical Hits on the wearer" plus Fire resistance for zero ore. **Bracing Band**: "after shoving an enemy, +1 AC until their next turn", and the wiki names Flurry of Blows: Push explicitly, so on any Push turn the bonus is always up on the party member with no armour.

**Crèche.** **Holy Lance Helm**: Smite the Graceless is 1d4 on a fixed DC 14 DEX save; it matters because Radiant damage fires a Luminous shockwave (spreading Radiating Orb on enemy turns) and the Gloves of Battlemage's Power research confirms it as an Acuity trigger, refilling Acuity between his turns, the direct answer to Acuity decaying by 2 per hit; self-reinforcing because it only fires on a miss and Radiating Orb at −10 makes enemies miss constantly. **Knife of the Undermountain King**: a stat stick she never swings because this party's crits come from Hold, not from threshold; the low-die reroll is melee-only; Organ Rearranger applies to ranged and spell attacks (T3-B11), and whether it fires from the inactive set is T4-U3. **Gloves of Dexterity**: see §2 T2-10. **Circlet of Psionic Revenge**: "Helmets and Gloves marked as Light, Medium or Heavy Armour count as armour, and prevent Unarmoured Defence from working", which rules out the Covert Cowl and the Dark Justiciar Helmet for him; Mask of Soul Perception replaces it in Act 3. **Gloves of Belligerent Skies**: Charles has a claim (Divine Smite is Radiant; Phalar's Shriek Thunder triggers them correctly in Honour Mode specifically) but Gale applies the rider 5–7 times per cast against Charles's two swings.

### Act 2

**Shadow-Cursed Lands.** **Shadow-Cloaked Ring**: the wiki names weapon AND unarmed attacks explicitly, which most riders do not; nearly everything in the Shadow-Cursed Lands is obscured, so roughly +12 across a full Flurry turn.

**Last Light Inn.** **Cloak of Protection**: exactly one exists and it is the only Act 2 cloak that touches saves; Charles carries permanent Risky Ring disadvantage on every save while concentrating in melee and stands in every area attack; Gale has Spidersilk's CON advantage and can be positioned out of danger; Bonbon has crit immunity and AC 18 (the old text said War Caster). **Amulet of the Harpers**: also the Act 2 answer to Hold Person, Fear and Dominate. **Flawed Helldusk Gloves**: across five strikes a turn roughly +12 damage against the +2 AC the Bracers gave, taken while Act 2 enemies are soft. **Cloak of Cunning Brume**: an honest cheap fill; the Act 2 cloak pool is built for melee (Fleshmelter and Thunderskin trigger on being hit) and the one unconditional cloak goes to Charles. **Coruscation Ring**: Radiating Orb makes the target Illuminated, which switches on Callous Glow's +2 radiant, which procs Belligerent Skies' Reverberation; only Gale needs light, so this never conflicts with Charles standing in Darkness. **Covert Cowl**: same redundancy that demoted Risky Ring and Killer's Sweetheart: you cannot improve on a guaranteed crit; requires Light Armour proficiency, illegal on Asterion. **Hat of Fire Acuity**: each Scorching Ray ray deals Fire separately, so one level-4 cast (5 rays) takes Gale from 0 to the cap. **Snowburst Ring**: §2 T2-8. **Evasive Shoes**: Boots of Persistence, Vital Conduit Boots and The Speedy Lightfeet all require Medium Armour proficiency, which Sorcerer 11 / Warlock 1 never grants, and Helldusk Boots go to Charles; T3-G2 makes Night Walkers the Act 3 default. **Potent Robe**: its whole effect is CHA to cantrip damage.

Stacks with Magic Weapon, not with other Elemental Weapon variants.
