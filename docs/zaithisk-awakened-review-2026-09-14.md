# Zaith'isk (Awakened) placement review — 2026-09-14

Question: `content/tadpole.md` gives Bonbon both the Band of the Mystic Scoundrel and
the Zaith'isk's Awakened passive. Both spend the one bonus action. Who should sit in the
Zaith'isk, should Bonbon keep the Band, and is a Thief 3 dip for bonus-action illithid
casting worth it on anyone?

Party: Charles (Vengeance Paladin 6 / Hexblade 6), Asterion (Open Hand Monk 9 / Thief 3),
Gale (Draconic Red Sorcerer 11 / Fiend 1), Bonbon (Swords Bard 11 / Fighter 1). Non-Honour.

Sources: bg3.wiki through `bg3kb` and direct fetches; Cephalopocalypse illithid tier
lists Part 1 (`9af1gYSpXK4`) and Part 2 (`rpVrXn3Rl7k`, elite ring; its summary was
written for this review in `resources/videos/summaries/`); the build and party guides already
summarised in `resources/videos/summaries/`; the four character files, `party.md`,
`tadpole.md` and the build notes. Every mechanics claim below was checked on the wiki.

**Recommendation: Awakened goes to Gale. Bonbon keeps the Band. Nobody takes a Thief
dip. Gale carries Perilous Stakes; Bonbon keeps Mind Blast as an Action cast and drops
Mind Sanctuary.** The power-by-power audit in §8 also adds three zero-action powers the
plan is missing (Ability Drain and Cull the Weak for Bonbon, Psionic Backlash for Bonbon
and Asterion) and drops Psionic Overload from Charles. Details and the alternatives
follow.

## 1. Mechanics that decide it

- **Awakened is mandatory.** "Use of this passive is *not optional*, and makes it
  impossible to use illithid powers as an Action." It does nothing for powers that are
  already a reaction, bonus action or passive. (bg3.wiki/wiki/Awakened)
- **It touches 15 powers**, all the "Class Action" ones: Psionic Overload, Force Tunnel,
  Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health, Stage Fright, Repulsor, Shield of Thralls,
  Perilous Stakes, Fracture Psyche, Black Hole, Mind Blast, Mind Sanctuary, Absorb
  Intellect, Displacer Beast Shape, Survival Instinct. It does nothing for Favourable
  Beginnings, Ability Drain, Luck, Charm, Displace, Psionic Backlash, Cull the Weak,
  Freecast, Illithid Expertise, Psionic Dominance or Fly. (bg3.wiki/wiki/Illithid_powers)
- **Recharges cap the "spam".** Perilous Stakes, Mind Blast and Mind Sanctuary are once
  per long rest. Black Hole, Stage Fright, Force Tunnel, Repulsor, Shield of Thralls,
  Fracture Psyche, Absorb Intellect and Psionic Overload are once per short rest. Black
  Hole grants recasts for 5 turns after the first cast. Only Concentrated Blast and
  Transfuse Health recharge per turn. One bonus action per turn already spends everything
  a short rest provides in a three-turn fight.
- **Perilous Stakes below Honour targets enemies**: INT save, Vulnerable to all damage
  for 3 turns, heals 2d8 when the target attacks. Part 1 @ 45:40: "on tactician mode or
  below Perilous Stakes cast on enemies is a truly nonsensically powerful ability that
  will just instantly win any boss fight." (bg3.wiki/wiki/Perilous_Stakes)
- **Black Hole**: 18 m range, 9 m radius, pulls enemies 6 m with no save, INT save or
  Slowed, short rest plus 5 recasts. Part 2 @ 18:42: "probably the one that benefits the
  most from being turned into a bonus action." Part 2 @ 21:52: with two bonus actions
  "you can cast two black holes in the same turn." (bg3.wiki/wiki/Black_Hole)
- **Illithid DC** = 8 + proficiency + the spellcasting modifier of the newest class whose
  level 1 was taken last, with Fighter and Rogue skipped, plus Arcane Acuity. Confirmed
  on the Illithid powers and Dice rolls pages. A Thief dip on Bonbon or Gale would not
  change their CHA-based DC.
- **Band of the Mystic Scoundrel**: after any weapon-attack hit, Illusion and Enchantment
  spells become bonus actions until the start of the next turn, and cannot be cast as an
  Action that turn. Ranged hits count. (bg3.wiki/wiki/Band_of_the_Mystic_Scoundrel)
- **Fast Hands** (Thief 3): "an extra Bonus Action, which you can use with any of your
  existing Bonus Actions." No cap on illithid casts per turn is documented anywhere.
- **Sorcery points**: Sorcerer 11 has 11 per long rest. Twinned Spell costs 1 per slot
  level (Haste = 3). Quickened Spell costs 3. Create Sorcery Points yields 1 per slot
  level and is itself a bonus action. (bg3.wiki Metamagic pages, Create_Sorcery_Points)
- **Zaith'isk saves**: DC 12 INT, DC 15 WIS, DC 18 (stat varies by class). Each failure
  is a permanent −2 to that stat until the character eats a parasite. The device is
  destroyed after one use. A companion can sit if they are the active speaker and both
  avatars are far away when the dialogue starts. If Lae'zel is in the party and speaks
  first she sits and auto-fails all three saves. Killing Ghustil after talking to her
  bricks it. (bg3.wiki/wiki/Zaith'isk)

## 3. Gale's bonus action is mostly idle

The guide and the video corpus both say Quickened Scorching Ray fills Gale's bonus
action every turn (`gale.md:388`; The BEST SORCERER @ 15:03). The sorcery-point budget
does not support that in this party:

| Per long rest, Sorcerer 11 | SP |
|---|---|
| Pool | 11 |
| Twinned Haste, three fights | −9 |
| Left for Quickened Scorching Ray at 3 each | 2 → **0 casts** |
| With Freecast waiving one Haste (Act 3) | 5 → 1 cast |
| Converting all four L1 and three L2 slots (seven bonus actions spent converting) | +10 → 3–4 casts |

So Gale quickens about once per fight and his bonus action is empty on the other two or
three turns. Every one of those turns is a free Awakened cast. The party never Hastes
Gale, so an Action-cost power on him costs a whole Scorching Ray, the most expensive
Action in the party to lose. Awakened therefore saves him more per cast than it saves
anyone else, and he has more empty bonus actions to spend it on than anyone but Bonbon
in Acts 1–2.

## 4. Options

### A. Status quo: Bonbon keeps Awakened and the Band

- What Awakened buys her: Perilous Stakes, Mind Blast and Mind Sanctuary as bonus
  actions, one each per long rest. Without it each costs one Flourish (she is Hasted, so
  her Action is half her turn's attacks).
- The conflict is real but small. The Band is an Act 3 item, so Acts 1–2 have no
  conflict at all. In Act 3 the three long-rest powers displace three Band casts per
  long rest, and on those turns the power is usually the better cast anyway (a DC 27
  14 m stun cone over one more Hold).
- Verdict: fine, but it wastes the passive. Three saved Flourishes per long rest is the
  smallest payoff of any candidate that can use it at all.

### B. Awakened to Gale (recommended)

- What it buys: Black Hole as a bonus action every turn of every fight from Act 3 (first
  cast plus five recasts per short rest) at DC 27 for the Slow, with the no-save pull
  clustering packs for Fireball, Shriek, Arrows of Many Targets and Radiant Shockwave.
  Stage Fright as a bonus action from Act 2 at DC 27, enemies-only disadvantage on every
  attack for 3 turns, which is direct protection for his Haste concentration. Perilous
  Stakes as a bonus action at DC 27 on the Held boss on turn 2, before Charles's nova.
  Mind Sanctuary as a bonus action (his Freecast already meets the prerequisite).
- What it costs: Quickened Scorching Ray on the turns he has 3 SP, which §3 shows is
  about one turn per fight. Turn 1 in particular improves: Twinned Haste plus a free
  Black Hole instead of Twinned Haste plus a 3 SP Quickened ray.
- Turn script, Act 3: T1 Action Twinned Haste, bonus Black Hole onto the Held target's
  feet (enemies only, allies are never pulled). T2 Action Scorching Ray (Acuity to cap),
  bonus Perilous Stakes on the boss. T3+ Action Fireball or Scorching Ray, bonus Black
  Hole recast or Stage Fright. Quicken only when SP are spare.
- Traps that stay: never press Concentrated Blast, Psionic Overload or Transfuse Health
  (already in `tadpole.md:196-197`). Awakened removes the Action option for these, it
  does not make them safe.
- DC timing: Fire Acuity is 2 turns per fire-damage instance, so a Scorching Ray puts
  him at the cap in one Action. Turn 1 casts are at DC 17 because Haste is the Action.
- Chair logistics: Gale has INT 8, WIS 10, CON 14, the same INT as everyone and worse
  WIS/CON than Bonbon. Save-scum in non-Honour. The Helmet of Autonomy (skeleton at the
  Festering Cove entrance, Underdark) gives WIS save proficiency for the DC 15 save
  (ALL NEW Act 1 Secrets @ 1:06:05). Leave Lae'zel out of the party for the dialogue and
  park Charles and Bonbon out of range so Gale is the speaker.
- Bonus: non-Honour Mind Sanctuary lets each ally in 3 m swap an Action and a bonus
  action once per turn and stacks with Haste (Part 2 @ 30:43). Charles's idle bonus
  action becomes a second Attack action on the nova turn. Test it in game before
  planning on it; the corpus's D rating and bug reports were Honour-specific.

### C. Awakened to Charles

- Reddit's Lockadin guide recommends the Paladin (`resources/reddit/research.md:3446`),
  and his bonus action is free after the two set-up turns.
- What it buys: Perilous Stakes on his own nova target and Shield of Thralls recast per
  short rest as bonus actions. Psionic Overload stays off the table for him: the 1d4 a
  turn strips two turns of Battlemage Acuity and forces Hold Person saves at Risky Ring
  disadvantage, which `tadpole.md:94` currently ignores.
- Why not: his DC is 17 plus one Acuity stack per weapon-spell hit, so 19–21 in
  practice against Gale's 27. He would never Action-cast these powers anyway
  (`tadpole.md:68`), so Awakened adds two casts per rest and nothing repeatable. Stakes
  doubles his smites whoever casts it, and Gale lands it more often.

### D. Awakened to Asterion

- Zero value. His Action is two attacks and a Flurry is two attacks, so Black Hole costs
  the same whether it is an Action or a bonus action. Both bonus actions are Flurries.
  `tadpole.md:13` already refuses him; the corpus agrees (Multiclass Tier List P3 @ 5:46,
  The BEST MONK @ 37:27).

### E. A Thief 3 dip for bonus-action illithid casting

- **Bonbon** Bard 9 / Thief 3 (dropping Fighter 1) or Bard 8 / Fighter 1 / Thief 3.
  Loses Bard 10 Magical Secrets (Command, Conjure Elemental), Bard 11's L6 slot
  (six-target Command, Otto's), and either Fighter 1 (Heavy Armour for the Act 2
  Splint, CON save proficiency, Archery, Intimidation) or Bard 9 (Hold Monster). Gains a
  second bonus action whose only illithid payoff is a second Black Hole per turn, which
  Gale gets from recasts anyway. Already declined in `build-notes-bonbon.md:203`.
- **Gale** Sorcerer 8 / Fiend 1 / Thief 3 loses L5 and L6 slots, Careful Spell and Hold
  Monster for a second bonus action he cannot fill: the Awakened recharges above already
  fit in one bonus action per turn.
- **Charles** cannot spare three levels without losing Paladin 5 Extra Attack or
  Warlock 5 Deepened Pact, which is the three-attack nova.
- **Asterion** already has Fast Hands and spends both bonus actions on Flurries.
- No video in the corpus takes Thief for illithid powers. The one Thief-illithid line is
  the two-Black-Holes aside in Part 2. Verdict: no dip on anyone.

### Should Bonbon keep the Band?

Yes. Flourish then bonus-action Hold Monster or six-target Command is her whole Act 3
turn (`bonbon.md:999`). The only other plausible holder is Charles (Booming Blade hit,
bonus-action Hold Person, auto-crits the same turn), but his set-up-turn bonus action is
already Curse and Might, and the EVIL CLOWN guide @ 34:47 says the Band "may be more
powerful on a sword bard archer if you have one of those in your party." One copy exists.

## 5. Where this departs from the video corpus

The corpus's stated recipient profile is "free bonus action, makes attacks" (Elixir Tier
List @ 53:17) and it names Swords Bard archers and Paladin/Warlocks as the best illithid
users. It rules Gale out because "Quicken already fills his bonus action" and notes that
Quicken cannot apply to illithid powers (The 11 BEST BUILDS @ 24:20). Both points assume
a sorcerer who is not paying 3 SP for Twinned Haste every fight. In this party Gale is,
so his bonus action is empty two turns in three, and the corpus's own top Awakened
beneficiary (Black Hole, Part 2 @ 18:42) is already on his list at the party's best DC.
No video considers Band and Awakened on one character, so the corpus has no verdict on
the conflict itself.

### Is the buff used to its ceiling?

Under the current plan, no. Bonbon's Awakened powers are three long-rest casts, and every
one of them displaces a Band cast in Act 3. Awakened's ceiling on her is capped by the
ring: Black Hole recasts, Stage Fright and Force Tunnel would each fight the Band for
the same bonus action every turn, so the plan rightly never buys them for her, and the
passive ends up firing about three times per long rest.

Under §4B, Gale's Awakened bar per short rest in Act 3 is: Black Hole ×6, Stage Fright,
Force Tunnel, Shield of Thralls (re-cast on Charles), plus Perilous Stakes, Mind
Sanctuary and Displacer Beast Shape once per long rest. That is nine to ten bonus-action
casts per short rest against four to eight combat turns, so every bonus action he owns
is spent at zero sorcery points, and Quicken becomes the thing he does when SP are
spare rather than the other way round. Turn priority: T1 Black Hole (cluster the pack
where Charles and Shriek want it), T2 Perilous Stakes on the boss or Stage Fright when
many attackers threaten Haste, T3+ Black Hole recasts, Force Tunnel when a melee enemy
reaches him or Charles's Held target, Shield of Thralls when Charles's Agathys is gone.
