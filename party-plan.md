# BG3 Party Plan — "Booms, Loot, Shock & Command"

A 4-person **non-Honor** party built to come online early and scale into a moderately-modded late game. Companion doc `party-review.md` holds the adversarial power/patch audit; sources are in `research/research.md`.
## Party at a glance

| # | Build | Role | Played by |
|---|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Oathbreaker Paladin 7 / Hexblade Warlock 5 (Half-Orc) | Melee crit-smite nova | **You** |
| 2 | Arcane Trickster 7 / Abjuration Wizard 5 (Githyanki) | Scout / infiltrator / retribution hyper-tank | **You** |
| 3 | Draconic-Blue Sorcerer 10 / Tempest Cleric 2 | Wet+Lightning nuke + Haste engine | Other |
| 4 | Swords Bard 10 / Fighter 1 / Wizard 1 | Control + damage + face | TBD |

## Party synergies

| Synergy | How it works |
|---|---|
| Haste engine | Sorcerer Twinned-Hastes the Paladin (Haste is single-target, so Twinnable) → a 4th attack |
| Wet → doubled | Sorcerer's Create Water (+ Trickster Mage-Hand water bottles) makes lightning/cold hit double |
| Control → auto-crit | Bard's Hold Monster paralyzes a target → melee auto-crits → doubled crit-smites for the Paladin |
| Shared psychic | Trickster's Resonance Stone aura doubles both its bombs and the Paladin's Shadow Blade |
| Auras | Paladin's Aura of Protection (+CHA saves) + Aura of Hate (+CHA melee damage) buff the melee cluster |

## 1. Oathbreaker Paladin 7 / Hexblade Warlock 5 — "The Three Booms"
*Half-Orc crit-smite frontline. **Played by you.***

**Character block**

| Field | Choice |
|---|---|
| Class | Oathbreaker Paladin 7 / Hexblade Warlock 5 |
| Race | Half-Orc (Savage Attacks — extra weapon die on melee crits; Relentless Endurance) |
| Background | Soldier (Athletics, Intimidation) |
| Starting stats | STR 8 · DEX 14 · CON 16 · INT 8 · WIS 10 · CHA 17 |
| Ability targets | CHA 17 → 18 (Hag's Hair) → 20 (Mirror of Loss); CON stays 16 |
| Feats | Savage Attacker (≈char 6) · **Great Weapon Master** (≈char 9) — GWM's bonus-attack-on-crit works with *any* melee weapon (incl. Shadow Blade); CHA still hits 20 via Hag's Hair + Mirror, so the +2 CHA feat was redundant |
| Invocations | Thirsting Blade (the 3rd attack) · Improved Pact Weapon · Devil's Sight |
| Weapon plan | self-cast Shadow Blade (3d8 at W5) → piercing weapon once Bhaalist Armour drops |

**Leveling** — Warlock 1 → Paladin 2 → Warlock 2–5 → Paladin 3–7 (smite early, Warlock kit mid, auras last; no respec, never runs STR)

| Char | Class | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warlock 1 (Hexblade) | Bind Hexed Weapon (CHA attacks), Hexblade's Curse, medium armour + shield |
| 2 | Paladin 1 | Lay on Hands |
| 3 | Paladin 2 | **Divine Smite** |
| 4 | Warlock 2 | invocation (Devil's Sight) |
| 5 | Warlock 3 | Pact of the Blade + self-cast **Shadow Blade** (2d8) |
| 6 | Warlock 4 | Feat: Savage Attacker |
| 7 | Warlock 5 | **Deepened Pact** → 2 attacks; Shadow Blade → 3d8 |
| 8 | Paladin 3 | pick any oath, break it → **Oathbreaker** |
| 9 | Paladin 4 | Feat: **Great Weapon Master** (7th attack — bonus-action attack on a crit) |
| 10 | Paladin 5 | Extra Attack stacks → **3 attacks** |
| 11 | Paladin 6 | Aura of Protection |
| 12 | Paladin 7 | Aura of Hate |

**Itemization**

| Act | Gear |
|---|---|
| 1 | Everburn Blade / any decent weapon (you mostly smite); medium or heavy armour |
| 2 | **Self-cast Shadow Blade** (main weapon) + shield; **Risky Ring** (always-advantage); fight beside the Trickster to share its **Resonance Stone** aura |
| 3 | **Bhaalist Armour** → switch to a **piercing weapon** (pike, or rapier + shield); **Sarevok's Horned Helmet** (crit range); **Killer's Sweetheart** (guaranteed crit after a kill); a defensive amulet; Helldusk Gloves |

**Description**

Three Charisma-scaled attacks per turn, each a potential crit → doubled Divine Smite. Half-Orc adds a die on crits, Hexblade's Curse drops the crit threshold to 19–20 on the cursed target, and the Risky Ring (or Shadow Blade's dim-light rider) keeps you at advantage so crits land often. **Savage Attacker** rerolls every damage die (weapon *and*, in non-Honour, smite) for ~+16% — it stays; **GWM** adds a bonus-action attack on any crit or kill, so against a Held target (all crits) it's a guaranteed extra swing. Shadow Blade (psychic, one-handed) carries early/mid; once Bhaalist Armour gives piercing vulnerability, switch to a piercing weapon so the whole team's piercing doubles. Rotation: pre-apply Curse the turn before → this turn is attack (3) + Haste (3) + GWM bonus attack (1) = **7 auto-crit swings**, smiting each → short rest to refuel.

> **Nova (Held target, full slot-dump):** ~7 auto-crit attacks smiting from an 8-slot pool (4×L1 + 2×L2 Paladin + 2×L3 pact) ≈ **1,000+ damage** in one turn (~¾ psychic). Caveat: this leans on a **non-Honour** quirk where Savage Attacker also rerolls Divine Smite dice (bg3.wiki: "unknown if bugged or intended") — it fires in-game but would shrink sharply in Honour mode.

**Description**

The arcane hyper-tank the party hides behind — retribution bruiser, bomb-striker, self-sufficient caster, and top-tier thief. Mage Hand scouts (invisible), flanks for Sneak Attack, and throws water bottles to soak clusters → your Sorcerer's lightning then doubles, so you set up the party's nova and run its consumable economy. Wizard 5 makes you self-reliant: a big refreshing Arcane Ward, reactive Counterspell, Glyph, and a 3d8 self-cast Shadow Blade (so the Shadow Blade Ring goes to the Paladin). Arcane Ward + Uncanny Dodge + Adamantine/Flail damage reduction + Blade Ward make you near-unkillable; retribution gear reflects it, and smokepowder bombs (safe via Evasion) nuke clusters. Comes online gently; the tank/bomb ceiling is an Act 3 payoff.

## Watch-outs

| Watch-out | Detail |
|---|---|
| Charisma overload | Paladin/Warlock/Sorc/Bard are all CHA; lean on the Trickster (INT 16 + Expertise) for the DEX/INT/WIS skills |
| Resonance Stone debuff | its aura also makes your own party psychic-vulnerable + disadvantage on mental saves — keep it off your frontline vs psychic/save enemies |
| Deepened Pact (non-Honor) | the 3-attack stack works only outside Honor mode; don't drop Warlock below 5 |
