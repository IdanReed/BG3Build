## Build

**Race** [2:36–4:09]: Halfling (Luck rerolls natural 1s on attack rolls, skill checks and concentration saves). Wood Elf, Half-Wood Elf, Duergar named. Race is "mostly a flavour question" because the dip supplies armour proficiencies.

**Subclass** [4:09–5:43]: Draconic Bloodline for +1 HP per level, unarmoured AC 13 and the fire bonus. Ancestry Brass (Sleep), Gold (Disguise Self, his pick) or Red (Burning Hands).

**Ability scores** [7:17–8:20]: with Ethel's Hair DEX 16 / CON 14 / WIS 10 / CHA 17 → 18. Without it CHA 16, WIS +2, and no Alert. Skills: Persuasion, then Intimidation or Deception [8:20].

**Level order** (character level → class):
- 1 Sorcerer 1 [8:51]: cantrips Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, Minor Illusion, Friends [5:43]; spells Chromatic Orb, Magic Missile [6:46].
- 2 Hexblade Warlock 1 [9:52–13:02]: short-rest slot, Shield and Hex on the Warlock list, Eldritch Blast and Bone Chill, Hexblade's Curse, medium armour and shields. Fiend (Command) and Great Old One (Mortal Reminder) named as alternatives.
- 3 Sorcerer 2 [13:02]: placeholder spell; Twinned plus Distant, or Extended "if you are dipping Fiend".
- 4 Sorcerer 3 [14:34]: Cloud of Daggers and Scorching Ray; Quickened.
- 5 Sorcerer 4 [15:38]: Misty Step; Mage Hand or Light; feat Alert (with the Hair) else ASI.
- 6 Sorcerer 5 [16:40]: Counterspell; Cloud of Daggers → Haste.
- 7 Sorcerer 6 [18:12]: Elemental Affinity; Fireball, conceding Hold Person or Hypnotic Pattern is "a little better".
- 8 Sorcerer 7 [20:16]: Wall of Fire; Chromatic Orb → Hold Person.
- 9 Sorcerer 8 [21:50]: ASI CHA 20; Sleet Storm (or Ice Storm, Hypnotic Pattern, Confusion, Banishment).
- 10 Sorcerer 9 [23:22]: Hold Monster.
- 11 Sorcerer 10 [23:52]: Heightened; Hypnotic Pattern or Cone of Cold.
- 12 Sorcerer 11 [24:55]: Fly; Globe of Invulnerability, or Chain Lightning if Globe is covered elsewhere.

No respec is mentioned.

**Items** [26:21–38:52]: Spellsparkler early, Markoheshkir late (fire attunement). Ketheric's Shield. Risky Ring and Callous Glow Ring. Helldusk Gloves or Spellmight Gloves. Amulet of the Devout, Greater Health or Spineshudder; Spellcrux swapped in for a slot refresh. Robe of the Weave endgame, spare medium armour early (Yuan-Ti Scale Mail), Potent Robe optional. Cloak of the Weave. Night Walkers, Boots of Striding or Boots of Arcane Bolstering. Birthright, Hat of Fire Acuity, Hood of the Weave or Pyroquickness Hat. Deadshot or Hellrider Longbow. Ally-carried: Staff of Arcane Blessing, Phalar Aluve, Arsonist's Oil. Sources are not given.

**Endgame list** [41:19–42:32]: Hat of Fire Acuity, Cloak and Robe of the Weave, Spellmight Gloves, Night Walkers, Amulet of Greater Health, Risky Ring, Callous Glow Ring, Markoheshkir, Ketheric's Shield, Deadshot. AC 20, HP 134.

**Consumables**: Ethel's Hair [7:17], Arsonist's Oil [39:53], Elixir of Fire Resistance thrown at enemies [40:57]. No standing elixir.

**Typical turn** [0:00–1:03]: Haste pre-cast on himself before combat; bonus action Hexblade's Curse; level 5 Scorching Ray; hasted action a level 6 Scorching Ray at "99% chance to hit" from Acuity. Hold Person only when not holding Haste.

**Stated weaknesses**: squishy and rest-dependent before Patch 8 [2:05]; spells known "heavily constrained" [16:40]; Risky Ring save disadvantage threatens concentration [29:03]; Pyroquickness burns the wearer [37:52]; Wet targets resist fire [24:23].

## Mechanics claims

- Hexblade's Curse: proficiency bonus on every damage roll against the target, better crit chance, once per short rest [0:00, 12:01].
- Draconic: +1 HP per level, AC 13 unarmoured [4:09]. Halfling Luck covers concentration saves [3:07].
- The pact slot converts to sorcery points and back [10:24]. Hex adds 1d6 to every ray, "up to 7d6" [11:30]. Hexblade grants medium armour and shields [12:32].
- Twinned Haste: self plus ally, or two allies [13:32]. Distant Spell: 90 ft Scorching Ray [14:02].
- Cloud of Daggers: no save, no attack roll, higher damage than Scorching Ray at level 2 "most of the time" [14:34].
- Elemental Affinity adds CHA to every ray, +12 on a level 2 cast at CHA 18 [18:42].
- Paralysed targets take critical hits from Scorching Ray beams fired within 10 ft [21:18].
- Heightened: 3 sorcery points for save disadvantage [24:23]. Draconic Fly at Sorcerer 11 [24:55]. Globe: damage immunity for 3 turns [25:26].
- Spellsparkler: flat +1 to hit at any charge count, +1 lightning per ray, 1d8 at five [26:59].
- Markoheshkir: Arcane Battery refreshes a level 6 slot; fire attunement adds proficiency bonus per ray; Heat Convergence tooltip broken, applies to one ray only, so discharge it in a Fireball [27:29–28:32].
- Ketheric's Shield: +1 DC and +1 spell attack [29:03]. Risky Ring: advantage on attacks, disadvantage on saves [29:03]. Callous Glow: +2 per ray [29:33].
- Helldusk Gloves: Rays of Fire per short rest [30:05]. Spellmight: −5 to hit for +1d8 "on every single ray" [30:37].
- Amulet of Greater Health: 134 HP [31:08]. Spineshudder: 2 turns of Reverberation per ranged spell hit; one cast maxes it several times [31:38].
- Robe of the Weave + Draconic AC + Ketheric's = AC 20, 25 with Shield [32:41]. Yuan-Ti Scale Mail +1 initiative [33:14]. Potent Robe adds CHA per Eldritch Blast beam [33:44].
- Night Walkers stop Prone, which would end Haste; they replace Misty Step [34:14]. Boots of Striding need medium armour and switch off Draconic AC [34:46, 41:27].
- Boots of Arcane Bolstering: tooltip wrong; +2 spell damage per roll against Threatened enemies for 2 turns after a Dash, including a pre-combat Dash [35:16–36:19].
- Hat of Fire Acuity: 2 turns of Acuity per fire damage, +1 attack and DC per stack [36:50]. Hood of the Weave: +2 spell attack [37:21]. Pyroquickness: extra bonus action once per round on fire damage, burns the wearer per cast [37:21–38:22].
- Staff of Arcane Blessing: Bless becomes +2d4 on spell attacks [38:52]. Phalar Shriek: 1d4 thunder per damage instance, stacks with Curse [39:23].
- Arsonist's Oil strips fire resistance and is "more efficient than taking the Elemental Adept feat" (captioned "elemental affinity") [39:53]. Temporary fire resistance + Arsonist's Oil + resistance expiring = fire Vulnerability: Wet then dry, or a thrown Elixir of Fire Resistance overwritten by another elixir [40:25–40:57].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- "One of the most powerful builds in all of Baldur's Gate 3"; "three turns for every other character's one" [1:34].
- The Hexblade dip makes the build "significantly harder to kill" and less rest-dependent [12:32–13:02].
- Twinned Haste is "one of the best things that you can do as a sorcerer" [17:41].
- Fireball is "a good spell but not an amazing spell" [19:45]. Cloud of Daggers is "generically the best combat spell" at level 2 [14:34].
- Markoheshkir is the best endgame staff for any caster [27:29]; Ketheric's Shield "the best shield by far" for this build [29:03]; Hat of Fire Acuity is "broken" [41:27].
- Alert is "always going to be the feat" when the Hair is available [16:08].

## Relevance to our party

The closest published build to Gale: same Draconic fire chassis, same Scorching Ray engine, same Hat of Fire Acuity, Markoheshkir, Callous Glow and Spellmight endgame, and it names Fiend-for-Command as the alternative dip. It differs on the dip (Hexblade at character 2 for Hexblade's Curse and Shield, against our Fiend at character 7 for Command), the feats (Alert plus ASI against Dual Wielder plus Elemental Adept), a shield instead of a second staff, the Sorcerer 7–10 picks (Wall of Fire, Sleet Storm, Hypnotic Pattern or Cone of Cold, Heightened, Globe against our Daylight, Dimension Door, Telekinesis, Careful, Chain Lightning), and several tactics our file does not record: pre-cast Haste, Boots of Arcane Bolstering, the Heat Convergence discharge, the Arsonist's Oil vulnerability setup, and the claim that Paralysed auto-crits apply to Scorching Ray beams.
