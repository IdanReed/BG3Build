## Mechanics claims

**General**
- Conjure Elemental summons last the adventuring day and are "as if you were adding a whole extra character to your party" [0:31, 16:13].
- Each caster can have one Conjure Elemental summon at a time; casting again, at any slot level, replaces it. Each party member with the spell can have their own [16:43–17:13].
- Conjure Minor Elemental summons are also mutually exclusive per caster; re-summoning kills the current ones and triggers their Death Burst [2:06–2:38, 15:40–16:13].
- Several elemental abilities compute their save DC from the summon's Intelligence, giving DC 8–10, and are therefore near-useless: Air Elemental Primordial Gales [20:53–21:25], Earth Elemental Seismic Strike [25:04], Fire Elemental Erupting Cinder [29:09], Water Myrmidon Explosive Icicles [54:07]. Water Elemental Winter's Breath uses Strength instead, DC 16 [33:51].
- All elementals and myrmidons have Elemental Warp, a bonus-action at-will teleport [21:25, 23:29].
- Elementals other than Air have no jump or fly, so they get stuck on ladders and must be teleported manually [26:05–26:36].

**Conjure Minor Elemental (level 4, from char 7) [2:06–16:13]**
- Azer: 39 HP, AC 20; attack 1d8+3 plus 1d6 fire at +5 to hit; Searing Smite once per battle; Concussive Smash (Dazed), Weakening Strike (STR save, disadvantage on attacks), Backbreaker (Prone); Overheat bonus action, DC 15 CON, Burning Fiercely 1d10 a turn, hits allies too [3:08–8:21].
- Two Ice Mephits: 21 HP, AC 11, fly; Chromatic Orb (cold) at will at +4, prone DC 12; melee +2d6 cold; Death Burst 4d6 cold each, half damage if killed rather than detonated; "kind of like casting Cone of Cold" [8:21–13:07].
- Two Mud Mephits: 18 HP, AC 11; Mud Breath restrains with Muddy (half speed, advantage against, disadvantage on attacks and DEX saves, removes Burning, fire resistance), DEX save DC 12; Death Burst is 4d6 Force despite the tooltip saying Bludgeoning [13:07–15:40].

**Conjure Elemental, level 5 slot [17:44–35:56]**
- Air Elemental: 90 HP, AC 15, fly; resists non-magical B/P/S, Thunder, Lightning; Poison immune. Gushing Air two ranged 2d6+1 Thunder, DEX save for half. Multiattack 2d4+2 slashing + 2d4 lightning twice, doubled against Shocked. Primordial Gales DC 10, so it cannot set up its own Shocked [17:44–22:27].
- Earth Elemental: 126 HP, AC 17. Soil-Clogged Slam 2d6+6 in a small AoE, Prone with no save, hits through Sanctuary, leaves mud. Multiattack doubled against Prone, but enemies stand up on their turn so the combo needs Haste or another prone source [22:27–27:06].
- Fire Elemental: 102 HP, AC 13. Basic attack applies Burning with no save, small AoE. Multiattack doubled against Burning. Erupting Cinder about half a Fireball [27:06–30:11].
- Water Elemental: 114 HP, AC 14; resists non-magical damage and Acid, Fire immune. Slam applies Chilled for 2 turns with no save; Chilled is cold-vulnerable and fire-resistant "just like the wet condition". Multiattack doubled against Chilled, "effectively 8d4 cold twice". Winter's Breath is a cold AoE, DC 16, half on save, and leaves an ice surface [30:11–35:24].

**Conjure Elemental, level 6 slot [35:56–58:15]**
- Air Myrmidon: 117 HP, AC 18, Air Elemental resistances. Invisibility once per short rest, lasts until long rest. Electrified Flail at +11 to hit, 1d8 + 1d10 lightning, CON save DC 13 or Stunned 2 turns, no cooldown. Raging Vortex: 10 turns, 2d8 bludgeoning a turn, no-save Silence, not concentration, ends only on recast or expiry [35:56–41:09].
- Earth Myrmidon: 127 HP, AC 18, 20 with Muck to Metal (costs 17 ft of movement on the turn it is activated only; the tooltip's lasting speed penalty is wrong). Grounded Thunderstrike 9 bludgeoning + 3d10 thunder, CON save DC 13 or Prone. Burrow bonus action 2d8, 40 ft. Sludgy Sling rarely worth an action. No flight [41:09–45:16].
- Fire Myrmidon: 123 HP, AC 18, 20 under its own Haste (Myrmidon's Immolation, 3 turns then Stunned; net five actions over four turns). Fire immune, Poison immune, resists non-magical. Hellish Rebuke at will as a reaction, 2d10. Scorching Strike; Cinderous Swipe cone 4d6+7, twice a turn under Haste [45:16–51:01].
- Water Myrmidon: 127 HP, AC 18, +4 initiative. Hiemal Strike (captions: "Hail of Thorns strike") cold, CON save DC 13 or Chilled; Chilled plus Wet is Frozen. Explosive Icicles: three orbs, once a turn, DC from INT 8. Healing Vapours: 2d8 heal to every creature within 30 ft including enemies, creates a large water surface; the pre-Patch-8 encounter-wide Wet was removed. Myrmidon is immune to Prone [51:01–58:15].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Conjure Elemental is "the sixth level spell that has the most impact in the game" and "the highest priority for spending your sixth level spell slots" [17:13–17:44, 1:01:56].
- Azer is the default Conjure Minor Elemental summon; Ice Mephits are the narrator's favourite; Mud Mephits are niche [7:50–8:21, 8:21–8:54, 13:07].
- Level 5: Earth Elemental "if you are in doubt"; Water Elemental for damage or as the tank for a low-AC party; Air Elemental only if the party applies Shocked (or for flight quality of life); Fire Elemental "the one that you're going to use the least often" [26:05, 29:39–30:11, 34:53–35:56, 58:15–59:19].
- Level 6: "there's no wrong answer"; Air Myrmidon is "generically the one that's going to fit into the most parties", especially against casters and out-of-reach archers; Earth Myrmidon is the best all-round tank; Fire Myrmidon is the mobile AoE option and self-stuns; Water Myrmidon is for wet-and-cold parties [37:30, 59:19–1:01:25].
- Before Patch 8 the Water Myrmidon "was basically the slam dunk of the four myrmidons" because it applied Wet to an entire encounter; that ability is gone [51:01].

## Relevance to our party

Conjure Elemental is Bonbon's second Magical Secret and her listed non-concentration alternative when Hold is invalid. The video confirms the shape of that reasoning (an all-day summon, worth a party member) and settles which mode fits a fire-damage party: the Water Elemental and Water Myrmidon apply Chilled, which is fire resistance, and the Water Myrmidon's encounter-wide Wet is gone since Patch 8, so the reason our file gives for it does not hold. Air Myrmidon (stun rider, no-save Silence on caster packs) or Earth Myrmidon (tank) are the fits, with the Earth Elemental as the level 5 default when the level 6 slot is kept for an upcast Command. The video's "highest priority use of the level 6 slot" claim bears on how Bonbon sequences her single level 6 slot against the Spellcrux Amulet refund.
