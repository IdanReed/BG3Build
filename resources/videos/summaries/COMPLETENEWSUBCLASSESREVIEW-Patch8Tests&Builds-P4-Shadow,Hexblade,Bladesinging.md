## Mechanics claims (tested unless marked "stated")

- Hexblade level 1 adds Shield and Wrathful Smite; Shield costs a Warlock slot, expensive at high slot levels unless multiclass slots exist; Wrathful Smite eats concentration and Booming Blade is usually the better bonus. "The real prize here is Shield." [28:16–29:51] (stated)
- Bind Hexed Weapon is "bind pact weapon at level 1, wildly better"; the weapon uses CHA. [29:51–30:53] (stated)
- Hexblade's Curse: bonus action or applied by a weapon hit; +proficiency damage; crit threshold reduced by 1; on the target's death the Warlock regains Warlock level + CHA modifier HP (tested by lowering CHA). [31:25–32:27]
- Medium armour, shields, martial weapons; "medium armor and shield is the best setup for armor class". [32:58] (stated)
- Hexblade spells by level: 3 Blur, Branding Smite; 5 Elemental Weapon, Blink; 7 Phantasmal Killer, Staggering Smite (4d6 Psychic, Concentration, Staggered = disadvantage on attacks and checks and no reactions, stacks with a Paladin smite); 9 Cone of Cold, Banishing Smite (stacks with a Paladin smite, ranged and melee versions). [35:06, 36:38, 38:48–41:23]
- The Pact of the Blade tooltip says Hexblades do not get Bind Pact Weapon, "presumably to prevent double binding" and a third attack at 5. [35:38] (stated from tooltip)
- At 5 he first reads Deepened Pact as not applying to a hexed weapon [36:08]; in testing, after Bind Hexed Weapon on the shortsword, "now we have extra attack". [44:31–45:03]
- Booming Blade with Extra Attack: two Booming Blades in one turn, "4d8 damage to just a normal attack because you get 2d8 Thunder on each". [44:31–45:03]
- Lifedrinker works with a hexed weapon; "pretty much mandatory". [45:03–45:34]
- Shadow Blade can be bound as the hexed weapon; from a level 5 slot it is 4d8. [45:34–46:05]
- Hexblade's Curse is added once per damage instance: "+4 from hex blades curse psychic damage" on the weapon hit, again on the Booming Blade Thunder, again on Lifedrinker. He calls it a "damage rider bug" but notes the tooltip says "your damage rolls against the target gain a +4 bonus", so "maybe this is working as intended in a weird way". [46:37–48:42]
- Armour of Hexes fires only on enemy attacks (an ally's did not trigger it); on success the attack becomes a critical miss, so riders such as a Steel Watcher's do not go off. [48:42–50:13]
- Accursed Spectre: a Mundane Spectre from a small kill has AC 14, 40 HP, a 60 ft CON-save Devour Soul that heals the Warlock for half and pulls the target; bigger kills give spectres that teleport and later deal AoE. [50:13–51:49]

## Verdicts

- Hexblade "more or less just replaces Pact of the Blade warlock"; melee warlocks will take Hexblade plus a different pact. [29:51–30:22]
- One-level Hexblade dips "are going to be very common"; "one of the best one-level dips in the game for any Charisma class". [28:16, 33:29]
- Armour of Hexes: "a coin flip on taking damage is not what we want to rely on", but a decent use of an idle reaction. [42:27–43:30]
- Accursed Spectre gives "a ton of free hit points all over the battlefield"; the level 6 ability is the standout. [51:17–52:21]
- Hexblade is strong as a dip or to 12 and "probably just going to be the default warlock". [51:49–52:21]

## Relevance to our party

This is the only tested source in the batch for the mechanics Charles's nova rests on. Three tests matter. Deepened Pact's Extra Attack works with a Bind-Hexed weapon, which is what `charles.md` assumes when it binds the Shadow Blade; the wiki's Deepened Pact page agrees. Hexblade's Curse added +4 to each damage instance separately, which makes the file's one-per-swing nova line an undercount if the behaviour survived to release. Two Booming Blades in one turn contradicts the wiki's "one cast per action" and the file's "once per Action"; the video ran on the Patch 8 test build, so the wiki wins. The level 3 and 5 Hexblade spells are all concentration except Blink, already in Charles's alternatives. Accursed Spectre at Warlock 6 is the one feature the Paladin 7 / Warlock 5 split forgoes.
