## Build

**Character and race.** Astarion is the example because his early bonus to hit and his late story damage bonus reward a many-attacks build [1:01]. Custom character: Half-Wood Elf is "by far" the best, for movement and for Shield proficiency, since a shield held in the melee slot still gives its AC while the bows are out [1:32–2:04]. Duergar (invisibility for surprise rounds) and Halfling (reroll 1s) are the other picks [2:04–2:35].

**Level order.** Bard 1–6 (College of Swords at Bard 3) → Druid 1–2 at char 7–8 (Circle of Spores at Druid 2) → Rogue 1–4 at char 9–12 (Thief at Rogue 3). No respec is mentioned. Bard first is for the smoothest start and for Extra Attack at char 6 [2:35–3:06, 15:00–15:31].

**Ability scores.** Graceful Cloth variant (demoed): DEX 16, CHA 16, CON 14, WIS 12 [5:41–6:11]. Ethel's Hair variant: DEX 17, WIS 10 [6:11]. Either way an item is needed to reach DEX 20 with one ASI, because the build wants other feats [5:09].

**Skills.** Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Persuasion, Deception, Perception (race), Intimidation; Urchin or Guild Artisan background [6:11–7:44]. Rogue 1 Expertise: Sleight of Hand and Stealth [21:13].

**Feats.** Sharpshooter at Bard 4 (char 4) [11:23–12:55]. ASI +2 DEX at Rogue 4 (char 12) [23:47–24:50].

**Fighting style.** Two-Weapon Fighting at Swords 3 so the off-hand hand crossbow adds DEX to damage. The narrator notes the build misses Archery and that a Fighter level could supply it, "but you'll see why we don't" [9:50–10:22].

**Spells by level.** Bard 1: Friends, Minor Illusion; Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, Tasha's Hideous Laughter, Longstrider (Disguise Self if Longstrider is elsewhere) [3:06–5:09]. Bard 2: Thunderwave [8:15–8:47]. Bard 3: Cloud of Daggers; swap Thunderwave → Hold Person [10:22–11:23]. Bard 4: Blade Ward cantrip (or Light / Mage Hand); Enhance Ability (or Invisibility / Heat Metal) [12:55–13:26]. Bard 5: Hypnotic Pattern; swap Tasha's → Glyph of Warding [14:28–14:59]. Bard 6: Plant Growth [16:02]. Druid 1: Guidance, Resistance cantrips; prepare Enhance Leap and Fog Cloud [17:05–18:39]. Druid 2: Circle of Spores, Symbiotic Entity [18:39–20:10].

**Items [25:21–30:36].** Hellfire Hand Crossbow + Ne'er Misser (Ne'er Misser is Force; use a generic +X hand crossbow for Piercing with Bhaalist Armour). Special arrows and coatings. Graceful Cloth chest; the Hair variant can wear Armour of Agility plus a shield instead. Gloves of Archery early, then Flawed Helldusk Gloves. Knife of the Undermountain King or Bloodthirst in the melee slot. Helmet of Arcane Acuity + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel. Marksmanship Hat early. Callous Glow Ring + Luminous Gloves for Radiating Orb; Boots of Stormy Clamour for Reverberation. Strange Conduit Ring. Snowburst Ring + Drakethroat Glaive (cold) for ice surfaces. No elixir is named.

**Illithid powers [30:36–32:11].** Favourable Beginnings, Luck of the Far Realms, Ability Drain, Psionic Overload, Cull the Weak.

**Typical turn.** Char 6: two Slashing Flourishes (four shots) plus an off-hand shot is five attacks, "50 damage from Sharpshooter, 15 from Dexterity" before weapon dice [15:31–16:02]. Char 11: six attacks with the Thief bonus action [23:15]. Hits stack Arcane Acuity, then the Band casts Hypnotic Pattern or Hold Person as a bonus action [27:27–28:29].

**Stated weaknesses.** No Archery style [9:50]. Hit chance is the character's "main struggle" [28:29]. Low AC in the Graceful Cloth [25:52]. Survivability issues, part-fixed by Symbiotic Entity temp HP [19:10]. Wrong casting stat for save-based illithid powers [30:36].

## Mechanics claims

- A shield in the melee slot grants its AC while the ranged set is active [1:32–2:04].
- Dissonant Whispers: Frightened targets cannot move; no concentration [3:36–4:08].
- Slashing Flourish turns one attack into two; at Swords 3 with two hand crossbows that is three attacks a round [8:47–9:18].
- Two-Weapon Fighting applies to the off-hand hand crossbow and adds DEX to its damage [9:50].
- Hold Person then a bonus-action hand-crossbow shot is a guaranteed critical hit [10:52].
- Sharpshooter is +10 per attack; toggle it off against low hit chance or low HP [11:23–12:24].
- Font of Inspiration returns Bardic Inspiration on a short rest [13:57].
- Glyph of Warding is non-concentration; the cold version leaves an ice surface [14:59].
- Extra Attack at Swords 6 gives five attacks with two Flourishes plus the off-hand shot [15:31].
- Plant Growth is non-concentration and quarters movement speed [16:02].
- Enhance Leap is a ritual although the tooltip does not say so [18:09].
- Symbiotic Entity spends a Wild Shape charge for temp HP and adds 1d6 to every attack while the temp HP lasts [18:39–19:10].
- Thief's extra bonus action is a sixth attack [23:15].
- A weapon coating applies to both the main-hand and off-hand weapon [25:52].
- Knife of the Undermountain King's crit passive (and Bloodthirst's) applies from the melee slot while the ranged weapons are out [26:56–27:27].
- Helmet of Arcane Acuity stacks on every weapon hit; the Band casts Illusion and Enchantment spells as a bonus action; 16 CHA is enough for the combo [27:27–28:29].
- Callous Glow Ring's +2 is Radiant "even though it's not listed", so it feeds Radiating Orb via the Luminous Gloves; five hits are 10 stacks, −10 to enemy attack rolls [29:01–29:32].
- Boots of Stormy Clamour apply Reverberation whenever the wearer inflicts a condition [29:32].
- Drakethroat Glaive enchants a weapon with +1 and 1d4 cold; cold hits with the Snowburst Ring create ice surfaces [30:04].
- Favourable Beginnings applies to both attacks of a Slashing Flourish and to dialogue checks [31:40, 32:11].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- "Slashing Flourish may be the single most powerful ability in Baldur's Gate 3" [8:47].
- Half-Wood Elf is the best race "by far" [1:32].
- Graceful Cloth is preferred over Ethel's Hair for reaching DEX 20 [5:09].
- Druid 2 gives less up-front burst than the usual Fighter 2 dip for Swords archers, but more sustained damage; "this character will just not run out of juice" [19:40–20:10].
- Thief Rogue is "amazing for any character that can use the off-hand attack" [23:47].
- Hellfire Hand Crossbow is "the best in the game" [25:21].
- One of the best illithid power users in the game, because it makes many attacks [30:36–32:11].

## Relevance to our party

This is Bonbon's chassis on a different chassis: Swords Bard 6 as the core, then Druid and Rogue instead of Bard 7–11 and Fighter 1. It agrees with our Sharpshooter timing, Hold Person, Hypnotic Pattern, Glyph, the Acuity helmet plus Band loop, a shield in the melee set, and stat-stick melee weapons. It differs on the ranged weapon (hand crossbows, which the 2026-09-10 §3 entry already rejected), on Minor Illusion at Bard 1, on Plant Growth as a non-concentration pick, on the Marksmanship Hat early, and on the Snowburst plus Drakethroat cold pairing. Its two dips cost Bard 9–11, which for Bonbon means no Hold Monster, no Magical Secrets and no level 6 slot.
