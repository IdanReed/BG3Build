## Executive Summary

Part 2 ranks the outer-ring ("elite" or astral) illithid powers, which the Astral-Touched Tadpole unlocks at the very end of Act 2 and which are therefore judged against Act 3 encounters, where the party already has its best spells, items, and feats. The headline result is **Black Hole** (S): a 60 ft range, 30 ft radius, enemy-only pull-and-Slow on an Intelligence save that needs no concentration, recharges on a short rest, and can be recast five more times as a bonus action. **Illithid Expertise** (S) and **Fly** (S) are passive or resource-free benefits. **Mind Blast**, **Psionic Dominance**, and **Freecast** are A tier. **Absorb Intellect** and **Displacer Beast Shape** are B. **Fracture Psyche**, **Mind Sanctuary** (Honour Mode, because of bugs), and **Survival Instinct** are D.

Near the start he revises two Part 1 ratings: **Psionic Backlash** moves from B to A and **Concentrated Blast** moves from D to C. He also covers **Survival Instinct**, an Act 1 quest reward he did not rate in Part 1.

As in Part 1, the ranking assumes the character took the Zaith'isk in Crèche Y'llek, so every power that lists an action cost is treated as a bonus action. He calls out where the rating changes without it. The video was recorded before Patch 7; he says he will revisit Mind Sanctuary if Patch 7 fixes it.

## Tier Framework

Same definitions as Part 1:

- **S:** a priority unlock that supplies exceptional, repeatable value.
- **A:** a strong passive or occasional fight-changing effect.
- **B:** consistently useful through the campaign.
- **C:** situational and worth using when its setup appears.
- **D:** generally weak, but still capable of useful edge cases.

He adds a bar for this list: because the powers arrive in Act 3, they "have to be quite powerful in order to be useful", and several would rate higher if they were available earlier. Illithid save DC is again noted as lower than spell save DC for anyone other than a mono-class caster, but most elite powers target Intelligence saves, which many dangerous enemies fail.

## Core Findings

### Commune, Do Not Eat

The Emperor gives the Astral-Touched Tadpole at the end of Act 2. Two choices: eat it, or open your mind to it. Eating grants the outer ring but destroys the tadpole so no one else can use it. Opening your mind grants the same access and the tadpole survives for other companions, if they can be convinced. He states there is no mechanical benefit to eating, so opening your mind is strictly better when the story allows passing it around.

### The Inner-Ring Refund

Communing automatically teaches the five central powers (Psionic Overload, Favourable Beginnings, Force Tunnel, Concentrated Blast, Transfuse Health) and Fly, and refunds any tadpoles already spent on those five. This means a party that has been feeding one character can still give every member the central ring plus Fly for free, and the refunded tadpoles can go straight into outer-ring powers. He calls Favourable Beginnings on the whole party "very powerful by itself".

### Black Hole Is the Destination

Black Hole is the single reason to reach the outer ring. It combines a full-encounter pull, a no-concentration Slow, an Intelligence save target, ally safety, a short-rest recharge, and five bonus-action recasts. He rates it above Mind Blast only because Mind Blast is once per long rest; both would be 4th or 5th level spells if they were spells. Mind Blast has no prerequisite (its adjacent node is Fly), so it can be the first outer-ring pick. Psionic Dominance sits on the path to Black Hole from the Charm side, but most players will reach Black Hole through Displace and then pick up Psionic Dominance afterward.

### Zaith'isk Dependence

Every action-cost power on this list is judged as a bonus action. He is explicit about two cases where the assumption matters most: Fracture Psyche is "a truly terrible spell as an action", and Black Hole would still be worth a full action. Survival Instinct, Mind Blast, Absorb Intellect, and Displacer Beast Shape are all described as bonus actions "with the Zaith'isk". Because an outer-ring character has many strong bonus-action options, weaker bonus actions (Survival Instinct, Fracture Psyche, Absorb Intellect) usually lose the slot to Black Hole or Mind Blast.

### Honour Mode Clauses

Mind Sanctuary and Perilous Stakes both behave differently in Honour Mode. Perilous Stakes cannot target enemies in Honour Mode, which he repeats here as the reason it was "pretty useless" in Part 1. Mind Sanctuary in Honour Mode is only area Haste with a nerfed Haste action; below Honour it allows action and bonus action interchange once per turn. His D rating for Mind Sanctuary is driven by the bugs he hit, not the Honour Mode design.

### Bugs He Reports

- Mind Sanctuary: characters starting a turn inside can receive one or zero actions; leaving can remove the hasted action, the normal action, and sometimes all movement.
- Absorb Intellect: Atrophy death on targets above 5 Intelligence (companion with 8, hireling with 8, city guard with 12, Volo with 15 on one of two tests). Range is 30 ft despite the 60 ft tooltip. The missing "begins combat" tag lets it kill neutrals without starting combat.
- Displacer Beast Shape: the teleport's saving throw does not stop the teleport.
- Fracture Psyche: the tooltip omits the saving-throw penalty.
- Counterspell (and therefore Psionic Dominance): the higher-level ability check always rolls Intelligence; the check result is hidden.

### Prerequisites He Names

- Fracture Psyche: Ability Drain or Illithid Expertise.
- Illithid Expertise: Luck of the Far Realms or Fracture Psyche.
- Psionic Dominance: Charm or Black Hole.
- Black Hole: Displace or Psionic Dominance.
- Mind Blast: none beyond Fly, which communing grants.
- Freecast: Shield of Thralls or Mind Sanctuary.
- Absorb Intellect: Perilous Stakes or Displacer Beast Shape.
- Survival Instinct: quest reward; requires at least one other illithid power to appear.

## Practical Unlock Order

He does not give a numbered list, but the video supports this sequence:

1. Commune (open your mind) to receive the central ring, the refund, and Fly on each character.
2. Mind Blast, because it has no prerequisite and immediately wins one encounter per long rest.
3. Black Hole through Displace, the power he says the astral ring is "what you want to go for".
4. Illithid Expertise through Luck of the Far Realms on the dialogue character.
5. Freecast through Shield of Thralls on spellcasters, especially Sorcerers.
6. Psionic Dominance once Black Hole is known.
7. Absorb Intellect and Displacer Beast Shape as B-tier utility; Fracture Psyche and Survival Instinct only for completion.

His closing recommendation is that "between Freecast, Black Hole and Mind Blast you should be able to have powerful AoE spells that just win every encounter", and that giving several outer-ring powers to the whole party is itself a large gain.

## Conclusion

The outer ring is worth reaching for Black Hole alone, and Mind Blast, Illithid Expertise, and Fly make the trip cheap. Freecast and Psionic Dominance add an extra 6th level slot and an extra Counterspell per day. Absorb Intellect and Displacer Beast Shape are situational healing and buffers. Fracture Psyche and Survival Instinct are prerequisites or pocket tricks, and Mind Sanctuary should be avoided in Honour Mode until its action-loss bugs are fixed. Because communing refunds the central ring and grants Fly, every party member can share the base powers, and the whole party benefits from a single Astral-Touched Tadpole if it is not eaten.
