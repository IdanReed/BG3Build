## Build

**Character and race.** Wyll is the example; Astarion or Minthara also fit [3:38]. Custom: Drow or Half-Drow for extra Darkness casts, Wood Elf for movement, Halfling or Gnome for solo safety [4:09].

**Ability scores** [9:47]: DEX 16, CON 14, CHA 17, WIS 10. Ethel's Hair takes CHA to 18 so one ASI reaches 20 and a feat is freed [9:17]. DEX over CON because Shield and medium armour make concentration checks rare [10:19].

**Skills** [10:19–10:49]: Sleight of Hand and Persuasion; face and lockpicker. Urchin background.

## Mechanics claims

- Bind Hexed Weapon has a fixed, untooltipped 20% chance per hit to apply Hexblade's Curse [7:14].
- Booming Blade was nerfed to once per turn but still pairs with Extra Attack: one Booming Blade, then a normal attack [5:41–6:12].
- Hexblades no longer get Extra Attack without Pact of the Blade [15:28–15:59].
- Scroll, item and illithid spells use the casting stat of the class you most recently took level 1 in; Rogue counts as Intelligence, Swashbuckler included [21:41–22:44].
- Smite spells cost concentration and a bonus action; Divine Smite is a reaction saved for crits; Thunderous Smite and Divine Smite can both land in one turn [24:17–25:18].
- Potent Robe adds CHA to every cantrip, including Booming Blade's on-move damage [34:07].
- The Elixir of Bloodlust action normally cannot Extra Attack [38:18].

## Relevance to our party

A sibling of Charles's split: Hexblade first, Devil's Sight plus Darkness, Booming Blade lead, reaction Divine Smites, Counterspell and Hunger of Hadar at Warlock 5, Command as the prepared standout. The differences are the Rogue 4 chassis (bonus-action attack, initiative, hide in Darkness) in place of deep Paladin, a Crown oath for accuracy, and Warlock 6 as the capstone. Three points bear on charles.md: Warlock 6's Accursed Spectre against Paladin 7's Relentless Avenger, the Act 1 case for a 2d8 Shadow Blade at Warlock 3, and the casting-stat rule for illithid DCs, which the wiki states differently for Rogue. The item list mostly targets slots the guide has locked.
