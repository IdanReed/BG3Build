## Build

**Concept** [1:03–2:04]: a Light Cleric / Open Hand Monk that uses the monk's control side (Stun, Topple, Push, Stagger) and stacks Radiating Orb, Reverberation, Arcane Acuity and Command on one character.

**Race** [2:36]: Shadowheart. Custom: Wood Elf or Half-Wood Elf for movement, Halfling for rerolled 1s on concentration.

**Ability scores** [5:12–5:43]: DEX 17, WIS 16, CON 15, evened out by an ASI at Monk 4. Tavern Brawler variant [5:43, 12:25]: same array, Tavern Brawler at Monk 4 with its +1 into CON, Hag's Hair onto DEX or WIS, a Strength elixir daily.

**Level order** (final Light Cleric 6 / Open Hand Monk 6):

- Char 1 Cleric 1, Light [3:39–4:41]: Wisdom save proficiency, armour, shields. Warding Flare gives the reaction a job and is worth "roughly four points of armour class". Cantrips [6:44]: Guidance, Blade Ward, Produce Flame.
- Char 2–4 Monk 1–3 [7:46–10:53]: Flurry, Dextrous Attacks; armour costs only Unarmoured Defence and Unarmoured Movement [8:49]; Step of the Wind Dash, free jumps; Way of the Open Hand.
- Char 5 Monk 4 [12:25]: ASI, or Tavern Brawler.
- Char 6 Monk 5 [12:58]: Extra Attack, Stunning Strike.
- Char 7 Monk 6 [13:31]: Manifestation of Soul, radiant on every hit; Wholeness of Body for up to six radiant attacks a turn [15:05].
- Char 8 onward [15:36]: Rogue 3–4 (Thief's bonus action "incredibly powerful for this build just like it is for every other monk"), more Monk for Ki Resonation, or the showcased Cleric 2–6.
- Char 8–12 Cleric 2–6 [16:08–21:16]: Radiance of the Dawn; Hold Person, Aid, Spiritual Weapon at Cleric 3; ASI DEX 20 at Cleric 4 (Alert, War Caster, Sentinel, Mage Slayer alternatives); Spirit Guardians and Glyph of Warding at Cleric 5; Improved Warding Flare at Cleric 6. Spells: Healing Word, Bless, Command, Sanctuary, Guiding Bolt, Shield of Faith.

**Items** [22:18–27:31]: Core: Callous Glow Ring + Luminous Armour + Luminous Gloves; from level 11 the Coruscation Ring lets Spirit Guardians stack Orb and the gloves become Gloves of Belligerent Skies for Reverberation on unarmed hits. Boots of Stormy Clamour: "10 stacks of radiating orb, five stacks of reverberation very very quickly" [24:26]. Helmet of Arcane Acuity + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel for a bonus-action Command [24:26–25:27]. Sentinel Shield (+3 initiative) or Adamantine Shield [25:58]. Devotee's Mace: radiant on hit, healing aura, attacks with DEX as a monk weapon [26:29]. Elixir of Hill Giant Strength if Tavern Brawler. Haste Helm early for movement [27:00]. Unarmoured alternative: Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo + Vest of Soul Rejuvenation [27:31].

**Typical turn** [0:00–1:03, 24:56]: Spirit Guardians up, Stunning Strike; target prone (Reverberation), stunned, covered in Orbs. Second Stun or Flurry for more Orb, bonus-action Command through the Band on the rest, Wholeness of Body for another Flurry.

**Stated weaknesses**: armoured monks lose Unarmoured Movement [27:00]; Spirit Guardians arrives late at level 3 [20:46]; Acuity does not build from Flurry [17:41]; competes for the Sentinel Shield [25:58].

## Mechanics claims

- [3:39] Cleric first gives Wisdom save proficiency; Dexterity saves mostly guard against damage.
- [4:41] Warding Flare's disadvantage is worth about +4 AC.
- [8:49] Armour costs a monk only Unarmoured Defence and Unarmoured Movement.
- [9:51] Step of the Wind Dash doubles movement; jumping costs no bonus action.
- [13:31, 23:54] Manifestation of Soul adds WIS + 1d4 radiant to every unarmed strike.
- [14:03] Radiating Orb lowers attacks; Reverberation lowers saves and knocks prone.
- [14:35] Command through the Helmet of Arcane Acuity works with one Cleric level.
- [17:41] Arcane Acuity triggers only off weapon attacks, not Flurry.
- [20:15] Bonus-action Dash spreads Spirit Guardians across every enemy.
- [22:51] Callous Glow Ring's tooltip omits the damage type; it is radiant, against illuminated enemies.
- [23:22] Luminous Armour and Luminous Gloves apply Orb when the wearer deals radiant; Coruscation Ring applies it from Spirit Guardians.
- [23:54] Gloves of Belligerent Skies apply Reverberation from radiant unarmed strikes.
- [24:26] Arcane Acuity raises spell save DC, so the Band's bonus-action Command lands.
- [25:27] Luminous Armour is medium; with a shield about AC 20; at Orb −10 enemies hit only on crits.
- [26:29] Cleric grants mace proficiency; a mace is a monk weapon.

## Verdicts and comparisons

- [0:00] "Render any enemy in the game completely and utterly helpless."
- [4:11] Light Cleric "solves a lot of the problems that monks have".
- [11:53] Tavern Brawler monks "kill enemies too quickly" for control to matter.
- [12:58] Stunning Strike "one of the most powerful control options in the entire game".
- [13:31] "One of the absolute fastest at stacking up radiating orb in the entire game."
- [15:36] Thief's extra bonus action "incredibly powerful... just like it is for every other monk".
- [23:22] Core combination: Callous Glow Ring + Luminous Armour + Luminous Gloves.
- [25:58] Sentinel Shield "the best shield in the game"; initiative preferred over crit immunity.

## Relevance to our party

This is our party's mechanics list on one body. Every engine the video puts on the monk already has a home here: Luminous Armour on Charles (fed by Inquisitor's Might and Smite), Gloves of Belligerent Skies, Callous Glow and Coruscation on Gale, Boots of Stormy Clamour on Charles as a flex, Helmet of Arcane Acuity and Band of the Mystic Scoundrel on Bonbon, Haste Helm on Charles, Kushigo boots and the Vest on Asterion. The interaction claims agree with ours: Orb fires on the wearer's own radiant, Reverberation feeds Stunning Strike and Topple, Acuity comes off weapon attacks. The two monk items nobody here carries are the Luminous Gloves and the Devotee's Mace. The Cleric half does not apply to Astarion.
