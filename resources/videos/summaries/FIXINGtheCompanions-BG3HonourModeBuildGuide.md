- Scope: live respec of five companions (Shadowheart, Astarion, Lae'zel, Wyll, Gale) at character level 3 in an Honour Mode save, when Withers first offers respecs. Premises: stay close to each companion's starting class, need no specific items, no Hag's Hair [1:01]–[1:33]. Early recording (Fog Cloud bug, 100g respec cost).

## Premises stated

- Builds must work with no magic items and few resources; consistency over peak power [4:09].
- Every build gets 16 Constitution [12:59]. Even ability scores only; odd scores "never matter" except in specific cases [5:10].
- Every turn offers five resources: action, bonus action, movement, concentration, reaction; good builds use all five [18:14].
- Every party wants Mage Hand and Minor Illusion somewhere, one or two Guidance sources, Misty Step on everyone who can get it, and a way to leave combat [7:15], [21:24], [34:57].

## Per companion

**Shadowheart → Light Cleric** [2:35]–[11:57]. Trickery is the weakest domain; Light (Warding Flare, off-turn defence) or Tempest instead [3:38]. WIS 16, CON 16, then DEX, leftover in STR for jumps [5:10]–[6:12]. Cantrips Guidance, Sacred Flame, Blade Ward over Resistance [7:15]–[8:16]. Prepared: Bless, Spiritual Weapon, Healing Word, Command, Guiding Bolt; open with Bless plus Spiritual Weapon, or Flaming Sphere plus Spiritual Weapon [10:52]. Skip Aid for now; Command over Hold Person because Hold allows two saves before a turn is lost [11:26].

**Astarion → Assassin archer** [11:57]–[15:05]. Stats: DEX max, CON 16 ("it's bad to be MAD and rad to be SAD"), WIS 14 as the most important save, STR 12 for jump mobility, 14 if wanted [12:29]–[13:29]. Build as an archer per the presenter's "best archer" guide [13:29]. Expertise in Sleight of Hand and Stealth; keep Perception; no conversation skills [13:59]–[14:35]. Rogue 3: Assassin "the best by default, especially for an archer" (two actions if you open combat); Thief "also good if you have a specific build in mind"; Arcane Trickster "the weakest by a fairly wide margin" [14:35]–[15:05].

**Lae'zel → Battle Master** [15:05]–[18:46]. Great Weapon Fighting; Battle Master over Eldritch Knight; Trip Attack, Precision Attack, Riposte [15:37]–[17:43]. Works with no magic gear [18:46].

**Wyll → Great Old One Warlock** [20:20]–[27:09]. GOO over Fiend for free fear on crits [21:55]; Eldritch Blast plus Hex is "the baseline damage-dealing number in the game" [22:26]; Armour of Agathys; INT dumped, CON 16, DEX 14, WIS 12 [22:56]; Expeditious Retreat → Misty Step at level 3; Agonising Blast and Devil's Sight, Darkness at 3 ("100% immunity to enemy ranged attacks") [25:04]–[25:35]. Pact: Chain strongest at level 3, Tome best long-term, Blade for melee [26:07]–[27:09].

**Gale → Divination (or Abjuration) Wizard** [27:40]–[36:00]. Cantrips: Mage Hand or Minor Illusion (whichever Wyll lacks), Fire Bolt [28:11]. Spells: Shield, Magic Missile ("you need magic missile"), Fog Cloud (bugged), Longstrider, Ice Knife, Grease; no Mage Armour because Gale is Human and already has light armour [28:44]–[29:47]. Never learn on level-up a spell you hold a scroll for [29:47]. Stats INT 16, CON 16, WIS 12, DEX 14 [30:17]. Not Evocation: wizards win with control and buffs (Fear, Hypnotic Pattern, Haste), and friendly fire is solved by positioning [31:20]–[32:20]. Divination's Portent is the anti-randomness tool [32:51]–[33:55]. Level 3: Flaming Sphere and Misty Step; Scorching Ray, Web, Invisibility, Cloud of Daggers also good; Hold Person optional because Shadowheart's Command overlaps [34:57]–[36:00].

## Mechanics claims

- D20 bonuses rise only on even scores [5:10].
- Light Domain gets medium armour only [5:40]. Warding Flare has unlimited uses [10:21].
- Radiance of the Dawn deals radiant damage with half on a successful save (dice garbled in captions) [9:20].
- Hold Person allows two saves before the enemy loses a turn; Command allows one [11:26].
- Assassin gets two actions when it begins combat [15:05].
- Great Old One's fear on critical hit is free crowd control [21:55].
- Darkness gives 100% immunity to enemy ranged attacks [25:04].
- Warlocks always hold their highest slot after a short rest, so Misty Step's slot level costs nothing [25:35].
- Fog Cloud is bugged: enemies still attack you inside it [28:44].
- Gale as a Human is proficient in light armour, so Mage Armour is redundant [28:44].
- An enemy that slips on ice loses its whole turn; Grease plus cold makes a bigger ice surface than Ice Knife [29:15].
- Portent substitutes a stored d20 for any roll you see, ally or enemy [32:51].
- The Pact of the Chain quasit turns invisible and can threaten a target for Sneak Attack [26:38].

## Verdicts

Trickery is the weakest Cleric domain; Light and Tempest the best; Life good but item-dependent [3:06]–[4:39]. Arcane Trickster the weakest Rogue subclass; Assassin the best at level 3 [14:35]. Battle Master the best default Fighter subclass [16:41]. Great Old One "stronger than Fiend" [21:24]. Evocation weaker than Abjuration or Divination on Honour Mode [31:20]. Eldritch Blast plus Hex is the damage benchmark [22:26].

## Relevance to our party

Our Asterion and Gale both leave their starting classes, which the video avoids by design, so most of it is orientation rather than contradiction. On Astarion the video's stat priorities (max DEX, 16 CON, WIS next, expertise in Sleight of Hand and Stealth, Perception kept) match asterion.md, and its Thief exception ("if you have a specific build in mind") covers our Monk/Thief; its STR 12 for jumping is moot under a daily Giant Strength elixir. On Gale the transferable points are the shared premises: Human light armour makes Mage Armour redundant (gale.md says the same), Magic Missile as a party requirement (gale.md drops it at Sorcerer 6), and CON 16 over DEX 16 on a concentrator, which gale.md resolves the other way with Spidersilk Armour's advantage on Constitution saves.
