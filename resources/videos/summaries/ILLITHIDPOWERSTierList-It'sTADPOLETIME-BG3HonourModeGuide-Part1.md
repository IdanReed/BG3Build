## Executive Summary

Part 1 ranks the central and middle-ring illithid powers. Because these abilities sit on top of a character’s normal class progression, even D-tier powers can be useful. The highest priorities are **Favourable Beginnings**, **Cull the Weak**, and, for characters who obtain bonus-action illithid casting from the Zaith'isk, **Psionic Overload**. **Shield of Thralls** is also S tier because it can be precast and convert a tank’s temporary hit points into an area stun.

The ranking assumes the character successfully obtains the Zaith'isk enhancement in Crèche Y'llek, allowing powers that normally cost an action to use a bonus action. That assumption radically changes several ratings. The guide also evaluates **Perilous Stakes under Honour Mode rules**; below Honour Mode, casting it on enemies to make them vulnerable to all damage is vastly stronger.

## Tier Framework

- **S:** a priority unlock that supplies exceptional, repeatable value.
- **A:** a strong passive or occasional fight-changing effect.
- **B:** consistently useful through the campaign.
- **C:** situational and worth using when its setup appears.
- **D:** generally weak, but still capable of useful edge cases.

Illithid save DC uses mental ability scores and proficiency. Heavily multiclassed characters with modest mental stats may therefore struggle to land powers that allow saves, while passives and no-save effects remain reliable.

## Core Findings

### The Zaith'isk Changes the Economy

The guide assumes players investing heavily in tadpoles will prepare for the Zaith'isk checks. Turning powers into bonus actions makes Psionic Overload worth activating before a sequence of attacks, lets Force Tunnel combine movement and knockback without giving up the main action, and makes Stage Fright a plausible encounter-wide debuff. Without the enhancement, these powers often drop roughly a tier because an action is the most valuable resource in combat.

Psionic Overload is the clearest beneficiary. The caster takes psychic damage each turn, but every attack gains 1d4 psychic damage. Eldritch Blast rays, Scorching Ray, Flurry of Blows, and other high-volume attacks multiply that rider far beyond its self-damage. Killing an enemy sooner also prevents more damage than the power inflicts.

### Passives Produce Campaign-Long Value

Favourable Beginnings is powerful precisely because it is subtle and free. The first check against an NPC or first attack against an enemy effectively receives expertise-like proficiency. It raises the reliability of important conversations and high-impact opening attacks without consuming any action.

Ability Drain is similarly automatic. Weapon attacks reduce Strength or Dexterity according to the attacker’s weapon, while spell attacks reduce the caster’s spellcasting ability. The penalty accumulates in long fights and counts as applying a condition, supporting gear that reacts to conditions.

### Build Toward Cull the Weak

Concentrated Blast itself is D tier, but unlocking it is still a priority because it opens Psionic Backlash and Cull the Weak. Cull the Weak executes enemies below a threshold equal to the character’s number of evolved powers; with only Illithid Persuasion, Concentrated Blast, and Cull the Weak, the threshold already begins at three.

The execution bypasses many defensive effects and triggers a nearby psychic explosion. As more powers are unlocked, routine attacks effectively gain an expanding damage threshold. One caveat is that an execution may not trigger effects that require the character to deal the formal killing blow, such as some Bloodlust interactions.

### Shield of Thralls Is Precast Control

Shield of Thralls grants temporary hit points on a short-rest cooldown. When the shield breaks, nearby creatures make an Intelligence save or become stunned. Putting it on a frontliner before combat creates a nearly free interrupt: if an enemy’s first attack breaks the shield, the stun can erase the remainder of that enemy’s turn and catch its neighbors. Intelligence is also a favorable save to target.

## Practical Unlock Order

For attack-focused characters, the guide generally favors:

1. Favourable Beginnings.
2. Concentrated Blast as the required path to Cull the Weak.
3. Cull the Weak.
4. Luck of the Far Realms.
5. Psionic Overload, especially after securing bonus-action powers.
6. Shield of Thralls and the remaining passives or reactions.

For a defensive caster or utility character, Favourable Beginnings into Transfuse Health and Shield of Thralls is a plausible alternate path, though Cull the Weak remains the strongest long-term destination.

## Caveats and Context

Illithid powers involve unavoidable story spoilers and role-playing consequences. The rankings judge mechanical power, not whether a character should accept tadpoles. The fallback transcript repeats rolling-caption phrases, which have been deduplicated. “Shield of THS” and “cold the weak” in the captions clearly refer to Shield of Thralls and Cull the Weak.

## Conclusion

The strongest illithid powers either add value without spending actions or turn a bonus action into an effect normally worthy of a full turn. Prioritize Favourable Beginnings and the path to Cull the Weak, exploit Psionic Overload only on attack-dense builds, and treat Shield of Thralls as precast crowd control. Even weak prerequisite powers can be worthwhile when they unlock an S-tier destination.
