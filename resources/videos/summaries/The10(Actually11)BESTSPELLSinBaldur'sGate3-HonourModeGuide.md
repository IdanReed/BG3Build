# The 10 (Actually 11) Best Spells in Baldur's Gate 3

- Playlist index: 21
- Video ID: `xLE9DCUHM9I`
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xLE9DCUHM9I
- Scope: Honour Mode impact, including a Patch 8 spell

## Executive Summary

This ranking asks which spells most change an Honour Mode run, rather than which merely deal the most damage. Power matters, but so do reliability, availability, action economy, synergy, and how much the game feels worse when a party lacks the spell. Longstrider ranks first because it grants the entire party extra movement all day at no resource cost. Guidance follows because it improves the countless ability checks that shape dialogue, exploration, and progression. Command places third as scalable, non-concentration crowd control, while Counterspell and Shield complete the top five through exceptional reaction economy.

Hold Person receives an unnumbered honorable mention because its ceiling is arguably the highest in the game—paralysis can instantly decide an encounter—but its value depends heavily on targets, save DC, and party construction.

## Evaluation Criteria

The list weighs:

- Raw effect: damage, control, defense, or utility.
- Game-changing capability: whether the spell enables strategies unavailable without it.
- Reliability: no-save effects and guaranteed value receive special credit.
- Action and resource economy: reactions, bonus actions, rituals, and persistent effects are prized.
- Breadth and longevity: spells useful across many acts and parties rank higher.
- Absence cost: the best spells are those the narrator most misses when a party cannot use them.

## Ranked Findings

| Rank | Spell | Central Case |
|---:|---|---|
| HM | Hold Person | Paralysis removes a humanoid from combat and makes attacks from within 10 feet automatic critical hits. With Arcane Acuity, Band of the Mystic Scoundrel, and a burst party, it can be the strongest spell in the game; without that investment it is less consistent. |
| 10 | Spirit Guardians | Persistent, action-efficient area damage that dominates many Act 2 encounters. Radiant damage also applies Radiating Orb and Reverberation gear effects, combining offense and defense. |
| 9 | Globe of Invulnerability | Makes creatures inside immune to damage for three turns. It arrives late, but reduces many Act 3 bosses to damage races the party can safely win. |
| 8 | Cloud of Daggers | One of the most reliable damage spells: no attack roll, no save, strong upcasting, and damage both on entry/cast and at the start of a target's turn. Especially decisive against Act 1 groups. |
| 7 | Sanctuary | A bonus action, non-concentration protection that stops enemies from targeting a creature until it acts offensively. It rescues exposed allies, protects buffers and summoners, and can waste many enemy turns through positioning. |
| 6 | Booming Blade | Patch 8's melee cantrip adds scaling thunder damage to a weapon attack and further damage if the target moves. It also triggers cantrip, thunder, damage-rider, and Reverberation synergies at almost no opportunity cost. |
| 5 | Shield | A reaction for +5 AC when that bonus will convert an incoming hit to a miss. It can negate multiple attacks for one level-one slot, protect concentration, and defeat on-hit effects. |
| 4 | Counterspell | Trades a reaction for an enemy's spell, often negating their whole turn and a scarce high-level resource. It provides unique insurance against encounter-ending magic. |
| 3 | Command | Level-one, non-concentration control that scales by adding targets when upcast. Prone, Drop, and other commands can remove turns, create advantage, or obtain the cambion's weapon in the prologue. Arcane Acuity and Mystic Scoundrel make it encounter-winning. |
| 2 | Guidance | A free cantrip adding 1d4 to ability checks. It improves dialogue, locks, exploration, and story outcomes throughout the whole game, stacks with Shapeshifter's Boon, and can activate concentration-based item effects. |
| 1 | Longstrider | A ritual granting 10 feet of movement for the day to every party member and summon. It costs no slot or concentration and repeatedly turns otherwise wasted turns into attacks while enabling kiting and retreat. |

## Core Strategic Lessons

Action economy is the unifying theme. Shield and Counterspell exchange a reaction for substantial enemy actions. Sanctuary can trade one bonus action for several wasted enemy turns. Spirit Guardians deals damage through movement after the initial cast. Longstrider improves whether both allies and enemies can take relevant turns at all.

The ranking also favors effects that “just work.” Cloud of Daggers has no attack roll or saving throw. Globe of Invulnerability simply prevents damage. Guidance and Longstrider impose no meaningful resource cost. The narrator repeatedly argues that free value need not be flashy to be dominant; when the underlying effect is also strong, as with Longstrider and Booming Blade, it becomes exceptional.

Several spells become much stronger with deliberate construction. Hold Person rises to number-one caliber when save DC and critical-hit burst are maximized. Command becomes a bonus-action mass disable with Mystic Scoundrel. Spirit Guardians turns into both damage and attack suppression through Luminous Armour and related gear. Sanctuary has edge-case combinations, including moving Moonbeam without breaking protection.

## Practical Recommendations

- Cast Longstrider on all allies and summons after every long rest.
- Keep Guidance available on two characters when possible so it can stack with other check reactions.
- Give important casters Shield and Counterspell; use them selectively on attacks or spells that would truly change the fight.
- Upcast Command when disabling several enemies is better than spending the same slot on damage.
- Cast Spirit Guardians before combat where possible, then use movement to tag as many enemies as safely possible.
- Use Cloud of Daggers when reliable damage or a chokepoint matters more than a larger but uncertain blast.
- Treat Globe of Invulnerability as an Act 3 boss solution, while remembering enemies may also benefit if they enter it.
- Build Hold Person parties around save DC, Arcane Acuity, close-range critical attacks, and the actual presence of humanoid targets.

## Caveats and Context

This is intentionally an oversimplified “top ten” discussion, not a replacement for the creator's full tier list. Party composition and play style can radically change rankings. Hold Person does not affect every creature, Command does not work on undead, and late-game spells have less total campaign impact. Booming Blade was newly added in Patch 8, so its placement reflects that ruleset. Some Sanctuary interactions may be unintended.

## Conclusion

The list's most important insight is that the best spell is often the one that quietly creates extra turns. Longstrider and Guidance dominate because they provide broad, repeatable value for free; Command, Counterspell, Shield, and Sanctuary dominate because they deny enemy actions at favorable cost. Damage remains important, but the strongest magic changes who gets to participate in the encounter at all.
