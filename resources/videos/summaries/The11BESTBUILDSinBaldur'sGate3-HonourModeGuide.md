## Criteria (2:05–4:43)

"Best" means easiest to win Honour Mode with. Three criteria: power (damage, control, AC, saves), resiliency (recovery from bad rolls, bugs, mistakes) and ease of play (few decisions, no forced story choices or party requirements). Early-game strength is weighted heavily because that is where runs die (8:51).

## Chapter: Lockadin, #11 (4:47–10:53)

- Concept: Warlock short-rest slots fuel Divine Smite, so smites land far more often than on a pure Paladin (4:47). Level 4–5 pact slots on smites give the burst (5:45).
- Everything on Charisma: weapon attacks, Command, Evard's Black Tentacles (5:45–6:15). Great Old One recommended for free Frightens on critical hits, and crits are frequent because Darkness gives advantage (6:15–6:45).
- Eldritch Blast as a full ranged fallback (6:45). Oath features are named as extras: Vengeance's advantage, Ancients' healing, Oathbreaker's Charisma damage; deep Paladin adds the saving-throw aura (7:17–7:48). Hunger of Hadar gets a special mention (7:48).
- Weaknesses: melee (8:20); slow start, since Pact of the Blade needs Warlock 3, Extra Attack Warlock 5 and smites Paladin 2, so "not until level seven that this character fully comes online" (8:20–8:51); still rest dependent (9:21–9:51).
- Broken tools: Darkness abuse, Arcane Acuity plus upcast Command from Warlock slots to disable "five enemies", Charisma stacking (10:22–10:53). Dialogue skills as a bonus strength.

## Chapter: Fire Sorcerer, #8 (21:42–26:54)

- Draconic Sorcerer adds Charisma to fire damage per instance; a 6th-level Scorching Ray is seven beams, so Charisma applies seven times (22:14). The narrator says you can cast Scorching Ray and then "twin another Scorching Ray" in one turn (22:46).
- Full sorcerer toolkit on top: control spells, Constitution-save proficiency, Twinned Haste when fire is immune or slots are precious (22:46–23:18). Claims up to five spells in one turn through Quickened, Haste and Twinned (23:50); best user of scrolls and Illithid powers because they combine with Quickened casts (23:50–24:20).
- Weaknesses: needs 5th/6th-level slots, rest dependent (24:51); lowest hit points, robes, squishy (25:22). Later caveat (34:41): one-shot casters are not actually more rest dependent than other casters, because they end fights with one slot.
- Broken tools: Arsonist's Oil strips fire resistance, and Wet then oil then Wet expiring is claimed to produce fire vulnerability (25:52); Hat of Fire Acuity is the easiest Acuity stacking in the game (26:23); Charisma stacking.

## Chapter: Open Hand Monk, #3 (45:09–50:20)

- Monk plus Thief Rogue; Open Hand's extra bonus actions feed Flurry of Blows: "eight attacks in a turn" with Monk 9 / Rogue 3, ten with the Fighter variant (45:40–46:11). Tavern Brawler means attacks almost never miss and Open Hand manifestations plus items add flat damage (46:11).
- Free control: Topple, Push and Stunning Strike several times a turn for one ki each (46:43). "The best melee character in the game by far" (47:13); only stun-immune enemies force you to "play the game" (47:43).
- Weaknesses: melee positioning; low AC and HP for a melee body, so it can go down to focused attention (48:15); Alert delayed because Tavern Brawler is the level-4 feat, so Alert arrives at level 8 at the earliest and "probably not until level 9 when you can have Monk 5 and Rogue 4" (48:45); four of six attributes matter, so daily Giant Strength elixirs are "basically mandatory" for the 9/3 and 6/4/2 splits (49:16–49:48).
- Broken tools: Tavern Brawler, strength elixirs, and Stunning Strike using Strength or Dexterity for its DC in BG3, higher than tabletop (49:48–50:20).

## Chapter: Swords Bard Archer, #1 (54:29–59:41)

- Ranged Slashing Flourish doubles ranged attacks; Bardic Inspiration fuels burst; a Fighter 2 dip adds more opening-round attacks (55:02–55:33). Band of the Mystic Scoundrel and Helmet of Arcane Acuity land bonus-action control spells at "infinitely high" DCs (56:03–56:35, 58:37–59:08). Full Bard levels give skills, spells and Magical Secrets at level 10 (57:05).
- Turn: stack Acuity with Slashing Flourishes or an Arrow of Many Targets, then bonus-action Hypnotic Pattern; always wins initiative (58:37–59:08). Command comes from Magical Secrets at Bard 10 or a single Warlock level; "either way works" because both keep Charisma casting (59:08).
- Weaknesses: squishy Bard hit points, mitigated by medium armour, Dexterity AC, an optional shield, and Shield from a one-level Sorcerer or Wizard dip (57:35); wants Sharpshooter, so it needs the Gloves of Dexterity or Sharpshooter plus a Dexterity ASI plus Ethel's Hair to reach 20 (58:07); Alert delayed a little, less costly on a Dexterity character (58:37).

## Mechanics claims stated as fact

- Lockadin online at level 7: Warlock 3 for Pact of the Blade, Warlock 5 for Extra Attack, Paladin 2 for Smite (8:20–8:51).
- Great Old One gives Frightens on critical hits (6:15).
- Darkness gives advantage to you, disadvantage to enemies and shuts down enemy ranged attacks with no save (10:22).
- Command upcast from Warlock slots hits multiple enemies (10:22).
- Tavern Brawler in Honour Mode doubles Strength to hit but not to damage in Wild Shape (15:30).
- Assassin plus Gloomstalker: extra attack on entering combat, action refresh on round one, automatic crits on surprised enemies (16:30–17:33). Battle Master maneuvers out-damage Champion crits (17:02).
- A 6th-level Scorching Ray is seven beams (22:14). Scorching Ray can be twinned (22:46; contradicts the Twinned Spell rule, which needs a single-target spell). Up to five spells in one turn (23:50).
- Wet, then Arsonist's Oil, then Wet expiring leaves fire vulnerability (25:52).
- Tempest Cleric maximised 6th-level Witch Bolt on a Wet target is a "guaranteed 300 damage" (31:33).
- Arcane Ward reduces incoming damage and refills on abjuration casts (36:18).
- Thrown weapons apply Tavern Brawler and full weapon effects at range (40:27). Thrown weapons against the final boss often miss and vanish (44:38).
- Open Hand Monk 9 / Rogue 3 makes eight attacks a turn by default (46:11). Stunning Strike DC uses Strength or Dexterity (49:48).
- Eldritch Knight thrower: seven throws on turn one at level 12 (three, three from Action Surge, one from War Cleric) vs five for the Berserker (51:22–51:52). Fighter's level-6 feat lets it fit Tavern Brawler, Alert and a Strength cap (54:29).
- Slashing Flourish doubles ranged attacks; Arrow of Many Targets stacks Acuity instantly (55:02, 58:37).

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Lockadin is last because it is melee, comes online at level 7 and stays rest dependent (8:20–9:51).
- Fire Sorcerer is "the character to take if you just want to kill things stone dead" but sits at #8 for squishiness and slot dependence (26:23–26:54).
- Top five are so close that "you could reorder these and I'd be happy with pretty much any order" (45:40). Monk is #3 because it can die to focused attention where the Eldritch Knight (AC in the 30s, heavy armour, Shield) cannot (48:15, 53:23–53:56); the Eldritch Knight beats the Berserker on opening-round attacks (51:52).
- Swords Bard is #1: "better archers than archers, better casters than casters" (56:03), positioning barely matters, and it "will solo the game" (56:35).
