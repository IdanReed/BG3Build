## Mechanics claims

Honourable mention, Gloom Stalker Dread Ambusher [2:04–3:37]
- The tooltip omits that the first-turn 1d8 rider comes with a whole extra attack that costs no action. With Extra Attack and Action Surge that is three attacks in the opening round.

#5 Staff of Arcane Blessing [3:37–7:19]
- Tooltip: Bless grants an additional 1d4 to saving throws and weapon attack rolls and an additional 2d4 to spell attack rolls, on top of normal Bless. Actual: the normal 1d4 to attack rolls and saving throws, and 2d4 in total to spell attack rolls. The extra applies to spell attacks only [4:38–5:09].
- Demo: weapon attack chance 50% to 60%, Eldritch Blast 50% to 70% after casting Bless with the staff [5:41–6:49]. Two buff icons appear, Bless and the staff's blessing, which makes the tooltip look additive [5:41].
- The presenter says he gave incorrect information about this item in early videos [4:07].

#4 Heavily Obscured, Fog Cloud and Darkness [7:19–14:05]
- Tooltip says you are "hiding" and undetected. Actual: Heavily Obscured only means the area is unlit. Standing in Fog Cloud does not make you hidden; you must still use Hide [7:50–8:21].
- Two effects. (a) You can Hide without a Stealth check, unless an enemy with darkvision can see into the area. (b) Attacking from Shadows: advantage on attacks against enemies without darkvision [8:21–8:52].
- Fog Cloud Blinds everyone inside and does not show a Heavily Obscured icon [9:24].
- Demo: from inside Fog Cloud, no advantage against enemies with darkvision; advantage against a target without darkvision [9:56]. Hiding against a darkvision enemy requires a Stealth check [10:28]. Hidden plus Attacking from Shadows both apply against a non-darkvision target [10:28].
- Darkvision has a range. Beyond the Oathbreaker Knight's 40 ft darkvision the Attacking from Shadows buff appears [10:58].
- Darkness does the same as Fog Cloud: heavily obscures and blinds [11:28]. A Warlock with Devil's Sight standing in his own Darkness is still seen by an enemy with ordinary darkvision, gets no Unseen or Attacking from Shadows buff against her, and still rolls Stealth to hide from her [11:59–12:29]. Backing out past her 60 ft darkvision range restores the buff [12:29–13:01].
- "Darkness and Fog Cloud, when it comes to attacking without stealthing, do exactly the same thing" [13:01]. Whether the enemy can "see through magical darkness" is not what is tested; only whether the area is lit and whether the enemy has darkvision in range [13:33].

#3 Confusion [14:05–20:21]
- Tooltip says "creatures". Actual: enemies and neutrals only; allies are never affected. Same for Cleric's Destructive Wrath [15:06–15:38].
- The tooltip and the condition give no odds. Tabletop d10 table quoted [17:42]. In-game demo: four failed-save turns gave three normal actions and one Confused Stupor; the presenter says the in-game odds are unknown [18:15–20:21].

#2 Wild Heart Tiger's Bloodlust [20:21–25:30]
- Tooltip shows a 7 ft radius; it is a 7 ft cone. The displayed formula (1d8 + 18) / 2 is wrong: the weapon die, STR and enchantment are halved, the Great Weapon Master +10 is not, and the rage bonus shows +2 where it is +3 at level 12 [22:55–25:30].

#1 Counterspell [25:30–27:34]
- Now fixed: the tooltip correctly says that countering a spell of higher level than the slot used needs a check with your spellcasting ability against DC 10 + spell level. The old tooltip said "upcasting has no effect".
- Bug at time of recording: every character rolls that check with INTELLIGENCE regardless of casting stat [26:32].

## Verdicts

Ranked worst to least bad: #1 Counterspell (fixed), #2 Tiger's Bloodlust, #3 Confusion, #4 Heavily Obscured, #5 Staff of Arcane Blessing, honourable mention Dread Ambusher. The staff is "still very powerful if you have characters with spell attacks" but "significantly worse than the tooltip makes it look" [6:49]. The two Heavily Obscured effects "combined are very powerful" [8:52]. Overall tooltip quality in the game is called very good [25:30].

## Relevance to our party

The staff is Asterion's Bless source and our text already describes Mystra's Blessing as +1d4 to spell attack rolls only, which is what the video demonstrates. Asterion's Eversight Ring is his way to fight inside that cloud. Confusion is on Bonbon's list and our tier note already says enemies only.
