## Build

**Demo [0:00–1:41].** The bard kills three and a half companions in one round of combat "with almost no items on this character", including two misses. The narrator notes he could not Sneak Attack because he forgot to start from stealth.

**Premise [2:12–2:44].** Bard as a damage dealer: "tied for the most ranged attacks that you can make in a single round of combat". Also pitched as a main character (skills, utility spells) and as one of the strongest Tactician solo characters because it fights from stealth at range.

**Race [2:44–3:47].** Wood Half-Elf: bonus movement for a ranged attacker who wants to stay out of melee, and shield proficiency, because a shield's AC applies while two ranged weapons or a two-handed ranged weapon are equipped.

### Version 1: item-agnostic (Swords Bard 8 / Thief Rogue 4)

**Ability scores [3:47–4:49].** DEX 16 / CON 16 / CHA 14, remaining points into WIS. Charisma is kept low on purpose; the build avoids every spell with a save DC. With Ethel's +1: DEX 17 / WIS 10, later 18 to save a feat.

**Skills and cantrips [4:49–6:53].** Stealth is the only essential skill; move Performance into Sleight of Hand. Cantrips: Minor Illusion (group enemies, pull them out of light) and Mage Hand. No Vicious Mockery (low DC, and every action is a shot).

**Damage [23:38].** Six attacks with Sharpshooter and DEX 20 is a minimum of 90 before dice; average 160–180 a round.
**AC [24:11].** Medium armour with full DEX plus a shield: 24.

### Version 2: Gloves of Dexterity (Swords Bard 6 / Thief Rogue 3 / Battle Master Fighter 3)

[24:44–30:26]. Respec when the gloves arrive: CHA 16 / WIS 14 / STR 12, DEX left low because the gloves set it to 18 and add +1 attack, "the same attack bonus as having 20 dexterity". Bard 1–6 as above with Sharpshooter at Bard 4 as the only feat. Then Rogue 1–3 (Thief) immediately, then Fighter 1 (Archery, offsetting the lower DEX and Sharpshooter), Fighter 2 (Action Surge), Fighter 3 Battle Master (Precision Attack, Menacing Attack, Disarming Attack) or Champion. Loses Greater Invisibility and Freedom of Movement, gains far more attacks. AC 23.

**Items [31:03–31:33].** "Very item independent." Gloves of Dexterity for version 2; otherwise "find the best hand crossbows that you can". Potions of Speed, an ally's Haste, and Elixir of Bloodlust are singled out because every extra Action is two to four attacks.

**Stated weaknesses.** Sharpshooter's −5 hurts at level 4 without Bless [13:10]; "landing attacks is something that this build can actually struggle with" [18:23]; hand crossbows have lower range than bows [15:48]; version 2 empties its four Inspiration dice in one round [29:24].

## HOW MANY ATTACKS?? [28:20–32:03]

- Version 1: **6 a turn** = Extra Attack's two Attack rolls, each a Slashing Flourish (4 projectiles) + two Thief bonus actions, each an off-hand hand-crossbow shot (2).
- Version 2: **10 a turn** once per short rest = the above 4 + Action Surge's second Action (4 more Flourish projectiles) + 2 off-hand shots. "100 Sharpshooter damage plus an additional 40 damage just from your dexterity" before weapon and Flourish dice. Needs four Inspiration dice for eight Flourish projectiles, then the dice are gone until a short rest.
- Theoretical maximum: narrator says **16** with Haste and Bloodlust, itemised as 8 Flourish + 2 off-hand + 2 Haste-action + 2 Bloodlust-action, which sums to 14; he flags "my math on that might be slightly off".

## Mechanics claims

- [3:17] Shield AC applies while wielding two ranged weapons or a two-handed ranged weapon. (Still current.)
- [7:25] Longstrider is a ritual, costs no slot, +10 ft all day. (Still current.)
- [8:59] Song of Rest is a third short rest each day; Bardic Inspiration returns on short rest from Bard 5. (Still current.)
- [10:01] Off-hand hand-crossbow shots add DEX to damage only with Two-Weapon Fighting; the old bug that added it without the style was fixed. (Still current: wiki Two-Weapon Fighting.)
- [10:31] Slashing Flourish can fire both projectiles at the same enemy despite the tooltip. (Still current: wiki "Targets: 2 targets or 1 target twice".)
- [11:33] Flourish adds the Inspiration die to damage.
- [16:50] Extra Attack lets each Attack roll be a Flourish: two Flourishes = four projectiles, plus one off-hand = five attacks.
- [22:36] The two Flourish projectiles resolve simultaneously, so being hidden for the first gives advantage on both. (Unverified.)
- [26:47] Gloves of Dexterity: DEX set to 18 plus +1 attack equals 20-DEX accuracy.
- [28:20] Action Surge is a second Action, so two more Flourishes. (Still current in every mode.)
- [29:24] Four Inspiration dice fund eight Flourish projectiles; then out until short rest. (Changed: the wiki now lists the Inspiration as "Cost on hit", so misses do not spend a die. The video budgets dice per use.)
- [29:56] A Battle Master manoeuvre and a Flourish cannot be used on the same attack, except Precision Attack.
- [31:33–32:03] A Haste action and a Bloodlust action each give two attacks. (Still true on Tactician and below; Honour Mode later removed Extra Attack from Hastened and Bloodlust actions. Wiki Extra Attack: "Only Action Surge works in honour mode.")
- [0:32] Sneak Attack was unavailable because stealth was not started. (Changed: Sneak Attack now also triggers "after landing any other ranged weapon attack as a post-strike reaction" that costs no Reaction, so the separate action is no longer required; the advantage or adjacent-ally condition still is.)
- [28:54] "Four inspiration dice" at Bard 6 with CHA 16. (Bardic Inspiration uses equal the Charisma modifier, which is 3 at CHA 16; not verified here.)

## Verdicts and comparisons

- [9:29] College of Lore is the best college in general; Swords is taken here only because the build is damage.
- [10:31] Swords Bard is one of very few characters with multiple main-hand attacks from level 3; only Berserker and War Cleric compare.
- [13:41] Sharpshooter early only with Bless or reliable advantage; otherwise ASI is "a reasonable default for most players".
- [18:55] Crossbow Expert is not worth a feat; walk away and take the opportunity attack, or Disengage.
- [23:38] 160–180 average damage a round puts this "comfortably over top of almost every other build in the game".
- [24:11] Medium armour plus shield at DEX 20 beats heavy-armour characters on AC.
- [29:56] Battle Master over Champion for Precision Attack and Menacing Attack.
- [31:03] Version 2 is "a worthy tradeoff" of 1 AC for many more attacks.
- [32:03] "Nothing is going to survive that long. 16 attacks is way more than you're going to need."

## Relevance to our party

Bonbon (content/characters/bonbon.md) shares the chassis (Swords Bard, Sharpshooter, Slashing Flourish, Extra Attack, shield behind ranged weapons) and none of the premise: she is CHA-primary with the Helmet of Arcane Acuity and Band of the Mystic Scoundrel, Bard 11 / Fighter 1, Titanstring on a daily Giant Strength elixir, and her bonus action is Healing Word then a bonus-action Command. The video's per-Action arithmetic (two Flourishes = four projectiles) is exactly the count docs/itemization-changelog.md 2026-09-10 §3 uses, and its attacks five through ten come from Thief bonus actions and Action Surge, class levels that changelog and `creation.notes` already weigh against Bard 10–11. Because our run is non-Honour, the video's Haste-action arithmetic applies to Bonbon under Gale's Twinned Haste where the Honour-mode videos' does not. Its Tactician-era claims mostly still hold; the two that changed are Sneak Attack's free post-strike trigger and Flourish dice being spent only on a hit.
