## Build

**Race.** Astarion as the example [3:09]. Custom: Duergar, Halfling, Wood Elf / Half Wood Elf, Githyanki; a race with shield or longbow proficiency smooths the early game [3:39–4:10].

**Class split and level order.** Final Swords Bard 10 / Fighter 2. Bard 1–6 at char 1–6 (College of Swords at Bard 3) [8:49, 9:50, 18:05]; Fighter 1 at char 7 for medium armour, shields, martial weapons and Archery [19:39]; Fighter 2 at char 8 for Action Surge [20:10]; Bard 7–10 at char 9–12 [21:12–25:53]. Alternatives at char 7: Assassin 3, Gloom Stalker 3, Wizard 1, or Fighter 1 + Wizard 1 [19:06, 21:12]. One respec when the Gloves of Dexterity arrive at the end of Act 1 [4:10].

**Ability scores.** Start STR 8, DEX 16, CON 14, INT 8, WIS 12, CHA 16 (or CHA 14 / CON 16) [4:41–5:12]. After the Gloves: STR 10, DEX 8, CON 16, INT 8, WIS 14, CHA 17, reaching CHA 20 with Ethel's Hair and the Mirror of Loss [5:12–6:14].

**Skills.** Dialogue skills plus Sleight of Hand, Stealth optional [6:14–6:44]. Expertise: Sleight of Hand + Persuasion at Bard 3 [12:24], Intimidation + Deception at Bard 10 [25:53].

**Feats.** Bard 4 (char 4): Sharpshooter; turn it off at 30% hit chance or lower; offset with advantage, hiding, high ground, Bless [13:25–14:27]. Bard 8 (char 10): Alert, +9 initiative with DEX 18; no ASI needed because the Gloves set DEX [22:46–23:49].

**Cantrips.** Minor Illusion + Friends at Bard 1 [6:44–7:46]; Vicious Mockery at Bard 4 as an Enchantment cantrip for the Band's bonus action [14:27]; True Strike at Bard 10 as a joke [26:25].

**Spells.** Bard 1: Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, Longstrider, Disguise Self [7:46–8:49]. Bard 2: Tasha's Hideous Laughter [9:19]. Bard 3: Cloud of Daggers; fighting style Two-Weapon Fighting as a hand-crossbow contingency [11:22–11:52]. Bard 4: Hold Person [15:29]. Bard 5: Hypnotic Pattern; swap Cloud of Daggers for Glyph of Warding or Plant Growth [16:29–17:33]. Bard 6: Extra Attack, four attacks a round; Enhance Ability [18:05–18:36]. Bard 7: Confusion, or Greater Invisibility for stealth archery [21:42–22:46]. Bard 8: Freedom of Movement, Plant Growth, Dimension Door or Invisibility [24:20]. Bard 9: Hold Monster [24:52]. Bard 10: a utility spell plus Magical Secrets Counterspell + Command; Banishing Smite and Sleet Storm considered [26:14–28:29].

**Items** [28:29–33:42]. Gloves of Dexterity, "the only mandatory item" [28:29]. Longbow over hand crossbows; Titanstring with a Giant Strength elixir or the Club of Hill Giant Strength, or his preference The Dead Shot for accuracy [29:01–29:32]. Armour of Agility or Elegant Studded Leather; AC "not that important" [29:32–30:04]. Melee set as passives: Knife of the Undermountain King for crit chance on ranged attacks, or Sentinel Shield [30:04–31:06]. Helmet of Arcane Acuity + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel: hit once or twice, then bonus-action Hold Person or Hold Monster [31:06–31:38]. Gloves of Archery before the Gloves of Dexterity [31:38–32:10]. Elixir of Bloodlust as the standing elixir [32:10–33:12]. A Misty Step item, then +attack, AC or saves [33:12–33:42].

**Typical turn** [33:42–35:15]. Bardic Inspiration → Slashing Flourish on the most threatening enemy (Brace first if not moving) → more Flourishes → bonus-action Hold Person or Command, or Hypnotic Pattern or Confusion → keep shooting → Action Surge if anything is left.

**Stated weaknesses.** Sharpshooter's −5 hurts early [13:25]; wants a Bless ally [13:55]; no innate Misty Step [33:12].

## Mechanics claims

- Slashing Flourish makes two attacks per attack and scales with Extra Attack, Haste, Potion of Speed and Action Surge [1:03–1:33]; Sharpshooter's +10 applies to both [13:25].
- Mobile Flourish pushes 20 ft with no save, then teleports the caster to the target [10:21–10:52].
- Cloud of Daggers has no saving throw [11:52].
- Hold Person gives automatic critical hits against the target [15:29].
- Action Surge's action allows a full Extra Attack in Honour Mode; Haste and Bloodlust actions do not [20:10–20:42].
- The highest enemy initiative bonus is about +10 [23:16–23:49].
- Hold Monster works on anything except Undead or CC-immune targets [25:22].
- Magical Secrets stops at 5th-level slots, so Counterspell cannot stop a 6th-level spell [27:27].
- Knife of the Undermountain King's crit reduction applies to ranged attacks while held [30:04–30:36].
- A shield gives its AC while a ranged weapon is wielded [30:04].
- Helmet of Arcane Acuity: 2 turns of Acuity per damaging weapon attack [31:06].
- Bloodlust's extra action cannot Extra Attack in Honour Mode but can cast a spell; with the Band a kill yields two spells [32:10–32:40].
- Friends turns the target hostile afterwards; fast travel away [6:44–7:16].

## Relevance to our party

This is Bonbon's build with the split reversed: Swords Bard 10 / Fighter 2 taken Bard-first, against our Fighter 1 / Bard 11 taken Fighter-first. The Action Surge argument is Honour-specific and the run is non-Honour. The concrete differences are the second feat (Alert, not War Caster), a DEX 8 respec once the Gloves of Dexterity arrive, The Dead Shot over Titanstring, Bloodlust over Giant Strength, Minor Illusion at Bard 1, and a Sharpshooter toggle threshold. Items, the Acuity + Band loop, Sharpshooter timing and the Hold Person / Hypnotic Pattern / Confusion / Hold Monster / Command spine match ours.
