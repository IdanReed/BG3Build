## Control philosophy

- **Neutralise attack rolls first.** Radiating Orb to −10 plus Warding Flare's disadvantage means attack-roll enemies "almost never hit" [1:32], [16:06]; Reverberation to five stacks knocks them prone and lowers saves [16:37]. High AC on the melee is offence: enemies then target allies and eat Sentinel attacks; if they attack you they miss and eat Riposte [24:58]–[26:32].
- **Lock the field.** Sentinel plus Polearm Master stops movement inside Spirit Guardians [28:36]; Spike Growth and Hunger of Hadar go under a banished enemy's return point [1:04:54]; Guardian of Faith blocks doors and takes no damage from debuffed enemies [13:01]; Cloak of Cunning Brume's fog plus Sentinel keeps a blinded enemy in place [37:56].
- **Rated control spells.** Command "probably the single best control tool in the game" [8:50]; Hold Person; Glyph of Warding cast under an enemy detonates at once [11:26]; Spike Growth "still the best thing you can do" in late Act 2 and early Act 3 [11:58]; Moonbeam + Sanctuary; Hold Monster, Hunger of Hadar, Confusion; Plant Growth is non-concentration and "always going to be extremely relevant" [49:17]; Freedom of Movement on two casters so a stunned ally can always be freed [13:31], [51:23]; Banishing Arrow as "a level 4 spell that we get to cast as an attack" on a Charisma save [1:04:24]; Cutting Words to make enemies fail saves [46:08].
- **Sequence.** Control before damage, then damage from consistent sources (Arcane Archer, glaive) once enemies cannot answer; the archer's job is killing mages, "the only thing we're missing" [1:06:28]. Cleric slot budget: top slot to Spirit Guardians or Destructive Wave, level 4 to Aid [14:35].
- **Initiative.** Everyone acts before the enemy. Alert on the cleric (char 6), fighter (char 12) and archer (char 12); the Omnicaster instead carries all the initiative gear, Hellrider Longbow (+3, "doesn't matter that we're not proficient") and Sentinel Shield (+3) for +9 total; "the lowest initiative in this party is +8" [3:37], [33:17], [55:31], [1:11:06].
- **Stated weakness.** Counterspell arrives only at char 8 via Magical Secrets [49:48].

## Mechanics claims

- Coruscation Ring applies Radiating Orb on spell damage while the wearer is illuminated; the Light cantrip keeps you illuminated [16:06]. Callous Glow adds 2 radiant to all damage sources, not shown in its tooltip [16:37].
- Moonbeam damage comes from a hidden summon and does not apply the caster's radiant riders [12:29].
- The cleric multiclass bug grants two extra skill proficiencies [7:47], [43:01].
- Wizard scroll scribing is limited by total caster level, not wizard level [43:32].
- Ring of Feywild Sparks gives +1 spell save DC, missing from the tooltip [57:36]. Amulet of the Devout gives +2 spell save DC [57:36].
- Hellrider Longbow's +3 initiative works without longbow proficiency [55:31].
- Seeking Arrow always hits but does not work with Sharpshooter [1:05:57]. Arcane Acuity applies to Arcane Archer shot DCs [1:15:14]. Snowburst ice saves use the wearer's spell save DC [1:14:44].
- Knife of the Undermountain King, Bloodthirst and Deadshot together crit on 16–17+ [1:18:24].
- Elixir of Battlemage's Power gives three permanent Arcane Acuity stacks for the long rest [59:42].
- Friends: leave the area on Honour Mode before it expires [39:28]. Aid raises Guardian of Faith's HP [13:01]. Sand Toss uses a CON save keyed to the Swashbuckler's Charisma [31:43].

## Verdicts

Arcane Archer "heavily underrated" and "one of the strongest characters out there" [1:02:20]; Precision Attack "almost always correct" and Trip Attack "probably the best control effect" [26:00]; Sentinel over GWM when on Strength elixirs [27:34]; Deadshot usually better than Gontr Mael for the archer [1:13:41]; Amulet of the Devout, Cloak and Robe of the Weave, Ring of Feywild Sparks as the caster DC set [56:34]–[57:36]; Boots of Striding for a permanent concentrator [58:39]; Helm of Balduran "really great" on the frontliner [37:26].

## Relevance to our party

None of the four builds is ours, but the philosophy overlaps: Bonbon is our concentration controller (Hold Monster, Command through the Band, Glyph, Confusion, Hypnotic Pattern, Conjure Elemental), Gale the non-concentration one (Extended Command), and Charles already carries Helm of Balduran, Boots of Striding and the Radiating Orb engine. Three video points test our plan. It insists every member, above all the controller, out-rolls the enemy on initiative, and Bonbon is our slowest body with no Alert. It puts a +2-DC Amulet of the Devout and Boots of Striding on the caster. It builds Snowburst Ring plus a cold Drakethroat enchant into a prone check on every arrow, an item chain nobody in our party carries. It also rates Plant Growth as an always-relevant non-concentration layer under Hunger of Hadar, which charles.md recommends but nobody in the party learns.
