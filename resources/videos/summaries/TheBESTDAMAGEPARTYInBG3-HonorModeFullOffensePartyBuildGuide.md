## Party frame (0:00–2:05, 54:37–56:10)

Dead is the best crowd control. The party averages "something like a thousand damage" in round one and can reach 2,000 (1:15:18). The Monk is the strategic core the rest is built to enable (3:08, 54:37); the Lockadin is the tactical anchor the others play behind, with Darkness plus Devil's Sight as a safe bubble (55:08–56:10). Only one member drinks elixirs (5:42).

## Build 1: Tavern Brawler Open Hand Monk 9 / Thief 3 (2:18–19:17)

- Race: Karlach for Soul Coins; custom picks are Duergar (Invisibility, Enlarge) or Wood Elf (speed) (3:38–4:08).
- Order: Monk 1–6, then Rogue 1–3, then Monk 7–9 (4:40, 12:32–17:11). Monk first because skills come from another character.
- Scores: dump Strength (elixir) and Charisma; CON 15, WIS 16, DEX 17 if Ethel's Hair is going here ("the character that benefits most"), else DEX 16 and two spare points into INT (5:42–6:45). Tavern Brawler's +1 goes to CON for 16 (8:17).
- Levels: Open Hand at Monk 3 (7:15). Tavern Brawler at Monk 4; with STR 20 it is "+10 to attacks and damage" and three attacks a round (7:46–8:48). Monk 5 Extra Attack and Stunning Strike, two stun attempts a round (8:48–9:20). Monk 6 Manifestations, which are OFF by default in the passives tab (10:56); Wholeness of Body's extra bonus action makes six attacks, around 120 damage a round (12:00–12:32). Rogue 1 at char 7: Expertise Athletics and Perception (13:02). Rogue 2 Cunning Action (13:33). Thief at char 9: eight attacks (14:37). Monk 7 (16:10), Monk 8 Alert at char 11: "you could even consider delaying the Rogue levels to take your monk levels earlier to get Alert sooner", since the early build has only +3 initiative; Alert makes it +8, or +9 with the Hair (16:10–16:41). Monk 9 Ki Resonation at char 12 (17:11).
- Items: only two required, strength elixirs (Hill, then Cloud) and Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo (18:14); any unarmed damage riders; Radiant Orb gear works through Manifestation of Soul (18:45). 300 damage a round, peaking around 400 with Haste or speed potions (19:17).
- Turn: reach any target with Cunning Action and Step of the Wind, stun or topple, flurry twice.

## Build 2: Draconic Fire Sorcerer 12 (19:40–39:02)

- Party face. Gold ancestry for Disguise Self (22:24); dump nothing important: CHA 17 for Actor, CON 16, DEX 14, WIS 10 (23:57).
- Cantrips: Fire Bolt, Minor Illusion, Light (nobody else has a light source), Friends (22:55). Ray of Frost or Bone Chill at Sorc 4 (26:03–26:33); True Strike at 10 as a joke (34:26).
- Spells by level: Chromatic Orb and Shield at 1 (23:26); Magic Missile at 2 with Twinned and Distant metamagic (24:29–25:01); Quickened and Cloud of Daggers at 3, explicitly not Scorching Ray, because without Elemental Affinity "cloud of daggers is actually still better" and the party lacks AoE (25:01–26:03); Actor and Misty Step at 4 (26:03, 27:05); Counterspell at 5 (27:36); Elemental Affinity at 6, take Scorching Ray, replace Chromatic Orb with Fireball (28:08–28:39); Wall of Fire at 7 and swap Cloud of Daggers for Haste (29:09–29:40); Alert at 8 ("too important to pass up", though +2 CHA is acceptable) and Greater Invisibility for the archer (30:13–32:21); Hold Monster at 9, guaranteed with the Hat of Fire Acuity from killing the Strange Ox in Act 2 (32:21–33:56); a utility spell such as Daylight or Banishment and Heightened Spell at 10 (34:26–35:28); Fly and Globe of Invulnerability at 11 (35:59–36:30); Charisma ASI and Disintegrate at 12 (36:30–37:00).
- Items: every spell save DC item in the party goes here, since it is the only save-based caster (33:23, 38:31); Hat of Fire Acuity as the default head (a 7-ray Scorching Ray caps it); Pyroquickness Hat as the damage alternative, a 6th-level Scorching Ray plus two Quickened 5th-level ones per turn, with Wet on yourself to negate the self-Burning (37:30–38:31); Markoheshkir (38:31); initiative gear.
- Play patterns: open from stealth with Wall of Fire so enemies are Surprised and stuck in it (29:09); Twinned Haste in fire-immune fights (29:40); Arsonist's Oil on the archer's arrows for fire-resistant enemies (29:40, 39:33).

## Build 3: Gloomstalker 5 / Assassin 3 / Battle Master 4 archer (39:33–54:06)

Rogue 1 first for Expertise (Sleight of Hand, Stealth), Ranger 1–5 (Archery, Longstrider, Hunter's Mark, Gloomstalker, Sharpshooter at Ranger 4, Extra Attack and Misty Step), Rogue 2–3 (Assassin), Fighter 1–4 (Defence, Action Surge, Battle Master with Precision, Menacing and Trip, Alert). Gloves of Dexterity let it dump Dexterity after the Crèche (40:35). Initiative +12 with no gear (52:32). Bow: The Dead Shot, "because that prevents you from having to use strength elixir to maximise the Titanstring, although you could do the Titanstring plus the Club of Hill Giant Strength" if you do not want two elixir users (53:34). Supplies Arrows of Darkness for the Lockadin (39:33), Longstrider, Guidance is the one thing the party lacks (find the amulet, 1:00:19).

## Build 4: Lockadin, Devotion Paladin 3 / Great Old One Blade Warlock 9 (54:37–1:14:46)

- Oath of Devotion purely for Sanctuary, wanted when the Sorcerer is concentrating on Twinned Haste (56:10–56:40, 1:01:51, 1:04:26). Scores: STR 8, DEX 14, CON 16, WIS 12, CHA 16 (56:40).
- Order: Paladin 1, Warlock 1 (Great Old One; Eldritch Blast, Blade Ward, Dissonant Whispers, Hex) (57:44–58:46), Warlock 2 (Agonizing Blast, Devil's Sight, Armour of Agathys; Drow for extra Darkness) (59:18), Warlock 3 (Pact of the Blade, Darkness) (59:48–1:00:49), Paladin 2 (Divine Smite, Great Weapon Fighting, Command, Bless, Thunderous Smite) (1:00:49–1:02:53), Warlock 4 (Bone Chill, Misty Step, +2 CHA) (1:02:53), Warlock 5 (Extra Attack, Hunger of Hadar, Hex swapped for Counterspell, Repelling Blast) (1:03:23–1:04:26), Paladin 3 (Sanctuary, Sacred Weapon) (1:04:26), Warlock 6 (Hold Person for the Helmet of Arcane Acuity plus Band of the Mystic Scoundrel loop) (1:05:58–1:07:03), Warlock 7 (Hypnotic Pattern or Evard's Black Tentacles on top of Wall of Fire; Slow, with Confusion via the Band as the alternative) (1:07:03–1:09:05), Warlock 8 (+2 CHA to 20, Banishment) (1:09:05), level 12 either Paladin 4 for Alert or Warlock 9 for Hold Monster and the Conjure Elemental invocation; Warlock 9 preferred (1:09:37–1:11:41).
- Tactician note: Paladin and Warlock Extra Attack stack below Honour, so "get two more Paladin levels" for three attacks (1:05:27).
- Items: Helmet of Arcane Acuity, Band of the Mystic Scoundrel, best heavy armour, two-hander or sword and board ("probably spend more time as sword and board") (1:12:13–1:13:14); Birthright to CHA 22 because "you won't be able to use the mirror on both this character and the Sorcerer" (1:13:14); Killer's Sweetheart for a guaranteed critical smite; Ring of Arcane Synergy stacked by Eldritch Blast's three beams, fired with the Haste action before the weapon attacks and the Band's bonus-action spell (1:13:44–1:14:46).

## Party-level allocation and turn order

Monk: strength elixirs, Ethel's Hair, monk clothing, Kushigo boots. Sorcerer: every spell save DC item, both Acuity hats, Markoheshkir, robes. Archer: Gloves of Dexterity, the best bow, crit gear, coated and Darkness arrows. Lockadin: Helmet of Arcane Acuity, Band, heavy armour, Birthright, Killer's Sweetheart, Ring of Arcane Synergy. Concentration: the Sorcerer holds Wall of Fire, Greater Invisibility or Hold Monster, and Twinned Haste only when fire is immune; the Lockadin holds Darkness or Hunger of Hadar. Bless from the Paladin goes on the Sharpshooter archer (1:02:21). Two Counterspells (Sorcerer, Lockadin). The archer usually acts first because it starts combat from stealth and rolls in at +12 (51:59–52:32).

## Mechanics claims stated as fact

- Tavern Brawler at STR 20 is +10 to attack and damage; Stunning Strike allows two attempts a round at Monk 5 (7:46–9:20). Manifestations are off by default (10:56). Wholeness of Body grants an extra bonus action (12:00).
- Cloud of Daggers beats Scorching Ray for single-target damage before Elemental Affinity (25:33).
- Wall of Fire from stealth Surprises enemies (29:09). Arsonist's Oil applies fire vulnerability to resistant enemies (29:40).
- Hat of Fire Acuity: seven rays stack Acuity to the cap (37:30). Pyroquickness Hat allows three Scorching Rays a turn; Wet makes the wearer immune to Burning (38:00–38:31).
- Initiative is rolled on a d4, so +12 is unbeatable (52:32). Champion needs about 20 attacks to catch Battle Master's damage (50:28).
- Paladin and Pact Extra Attack stack on Tactician, not Honour (1:05:27). Great Weapon Fighting "multiplies our Smite damage" (1:02:21). Blade Ward multiplies Armour of Agathys hit points (58:46).
- The Mirror of Loss cannot be used on two characters (1:13:14; the wiki says each party member may pray and offer, and the lock is per character).
- Eldritch Blast's three beams are three Arcane Synergy triggers (1:13:44).

## Verdicts

The Monk is the highest and most consistent damage in the game and the reason to start there (2:18). The Fire Sorcerer is the highest-damage sorcerer variant and the party's utility and face (19:40, 21:21). The Lockadin spot is "pretty flexible" and could be a Light Cleric, Moon Druid or Battle Master (54:37, 56:10, 1:11:41); its one weakness, coming online at 5 and 7, is tolerable because the rest of the party carries the early game (55:08).

## Relevance to our party

Three of four builds map to ours. The Monk chapter uses our stats and feats but a different level order (Thief at char 9, Alert at char 11) and puts Athletics Expertise where Asterion puts Stealth. The Sorcerer is pure Sorcerer 12 with Alert at 8 and no Elemental Adept, delays Scorching Ray to Sorc 6 behind Cloud of Daggers, and holds Wall of Fire or Hold Monster rather than permanent Twinned Haste; it also names the Pyroquickness Hat that Gale's file omits. The Lockadin runs Devotion / Great Old One at Paladin 3 / Warlock 9 with the Acuity Helmet and Band on itself, all already compared in `docs/ceph-video-comparison.md`, and its Mirror of Loss claim is contradicted by the wiki. The Dead Shot remark is the only new note on the two-elixir cost already accepted in deviation D17.
