## Party concept

Darkness is symmetric, so the party breaks the symmetry: everyone who stands in a cloud has Devil's Sight (Warlock 2) or a blind-immunity item [0:32]–[2:38]. Inside a cloud the party is immune to enemy ranged attacks, attacks with advantage, and enemy melee attacks at disadvantage [1:03]. Enemy casters are answered by an Oath of the Ancients paladin: Aura of Protection (+CHA to saves) and Aura of Warding (half spell damage) [3:42]–[4:12], [15:08]. Only one blind-immunity item is reliably early (Eversight Ring, Act 2), so one character wears it and the other three take Warlock levels [2:38]–[3:09]. The party wants many Darkness casts spread over the field, not one cloud [2:06].

## Build 1 — Lockadin: Oath of the Ancients Paladin 7 / Great Old One Warlock 5 [3:09]–[17:12]

- Race Drow for the extra Darkness cast [4:43]. Stats STR 8, DEX 14, CON 16, WIS 12, CHA 16; swap CON and DEX for initiative, because the build never gets Alert [4:43]–[5:14].
- Level order: Paladin 1 → Warlock 1 (Great Old One over Fiend, because Command already comes from Paladin and the build crits often; Eldritch Blast, Minor Illusion; Dissonant Whispers, Hex) [5:45]–[6:47] → Warlock 2 (Agonising Blast, Devil's Sight; Armour of Agathys) [7:19]–[7:50] → Warlock 3 (Pact of the Blade; Darkness; Dissonant Whispers → Misty Step) [7:50]–[9:24] → Paladin 2 at char 5 (Divine Smite, Great Weapon Fighting; Command, Bless, Thunderous Smite, Shield of Faith, Protection from Evil and Good) [9:24]–[10:26] → Warlock 4 (feat +2 CHA; Bone Chill; Hold Person) [10:57]–[11:29] → Warlock 5 at char 7 (pact Extra Attack; Hunger of Hadar; Repelling Blast; Hex → Counterspell, or Hypnotic Pattern for a Band cast) [11:29]–[13:02] → Paladin 3–7 at chars 8–12 (Paladin 4 +2 CHA; Paladin 5 Misty Step free from Ancients, so respec the Warlock pick away; Paladin 6 Aura of Protection; Paladin 7 Aura of Warding) [13:33]–[15:08]. Smoother alternative start: Warlock 1–3 first, respec to Paladin 1 / Warlock 3 at char 4 [5:45].
- Items: initiative first, since the build has no Alert: Gloves of Dexterity (DEX 18), Soulbreaker Greatsword (+2 Initiative), Yuan-ti Scale Mail; then crit gear; Helmet of Arcane Acuity plus Band of the Mystic Scoundrel for a bonus-action Command or Hold Person after a weapon attack, with the remark "you don't even need the Arcane Acuity helm honestly" [15:39]–[17:12]. The Deadshot rides in the ranged slot for crit chance [54:07].
- Typical turn: stand in Darkness, attack with advantage, smite on crits; a hasted action is often a double Eldritch Blast [7:19]; bonus-action Command or Hold via the Band [17:12]. Hex is dropped at Warlock 5 because a smite out of that slot is more damage [12:32].
- Stated weakness: initiative [15:39].

## Mechanics claims

- Inside magical Darkness: immune to enemy ranged attacks; advantage on your attacks; enemy melee at disadvantage [1:03].
- Blind immunity comes from Devil's Sight or four items; Eversight Ring is the only reliable early one [2:38].
- The Lockadin "can hang out in darkness and fire off Eldritch Blasts, forcing enemies to come to you" [9:54]; hasted double Eldritch Blast is a good use of the extra action [7:19].
- Non-Honour: Paladin 5 Extra Attack and Warlock 5 pact Extra Attack stack for a third attack [14:37].
- Aura of Protection is +5 to party saves at CHA 20; Aura of Warding halves incoming spell damage [15:08].
- Band of the Mystic Scoundrel: weapon attack, then Command or Hold Person as a bonus action; Hypnotic Pattern qualifies as an Illusion [11:29], [13:02].
- Warlock spell slots can be converted into sorcery points and exceed the sorcerer's maximum; metamagic applies to Warlock spells [26:37].
- Extended Spell doubles Command's duration: two turns prone [27:10].
- Darkness is roughly −4 to a melee attacker's hit chance; the Shield spell is −5 [36:31].
- Confusion affects enemies only despite saying "creatures" [31:52]. Banishment targets a Charisma save that late bosses tend to fail [32:23].
- Eversight Ring does not let you fire into a Darkness cloud you are not standing in [38:37], [54:38]; Helldusk Helmet lets you see into and shoot into clouds [54:38].
- With Extra Attack, fire the Arrow of Darkness as the second attack, or the target becomes untargetable [45:23].
- Dire Raven casts Darkness at its landing point with its movement and is immune to its own blind at Ranger 11 [52:05]–[53:07]. Other beasts are not blind-immune [43:19].
- Rangers share the Fighter hit die [41:12]. Point buy cannot reach three 16s [40:10].
- Spiritual Weapon is immune to Blind; Grasping Vine ignores blindness [1:01:20], [1:05:32].
- Callous Glow Ring adds nothing against enemies standing in darkness [1:11:44].
- Heroes' Feast poison immunity lets the party stand in its own Stinking Cloud or Cloudkill [1:08:39].
- A Sorcerer "gets to use scrolls better than any other character" (context: highest Charisma plus DC gear) [35:30].

## Verdicts

- Great Old One "probably the most optimal" patron for a crit-heavy Lockadin whose Command comes from Paladin; Archfey also reasonable [6:16].
- Oath of the Ancients "just awesome for this party" because it is tightly grouped [4:12].
- Hunger of Hadar is the Sorlock's main offence; "two hungers can cover the entire battlefield" [25:05].
- Command on as many characters as possible [21:57], [1:00:48].
- Alert on the controller "as early as possible"; this party carries fewer Alerts than most [24:35], [1:01:51].
- Titanstring + elixir over the Deadshot for the archer; Deadshot to the paladin [54:07].
- Staff of Arcane Blessing "insanely powerful" with two Warlocks [1:12:48].
- Mono-class Ranger is competitive on Honour Mode [51:35].

## Relevance to our party

Charles already runs the video's Lockadin core (Devil's Sight, Darkness clouds, Counterspell, Hunger of Hadar plus Repelling Blast, Hold Person and Misty Step free from the oath), but on Vengeance/Hexblade with Alert, with arrows instead of the spell, and with the target kept outside the cloud. Gale is a one-level Fiend dip taken, as the video says, for Command plus Extended Spell; his concentration is Twinned Haste rather than Hunger of Hadar, and he stands outside the clouds. Asterion is the party's Eversight Ring carrier, exactly the video's prescription for its one non-warlock melee. Bonbon, like the video's Ranger, is the Titanstring-plus-elixir archer who fires the Darkness arrows. The claims that bear on us are the Eldritch-Blast-from-inside-darkness play (bg3.wiki's Darkness text says ranged attacks cannot leave the cloud), Great Old One's Mortal Reminder on an auto-crit build, and the arrow-sequencing rule for an archer with no blind immunity.
