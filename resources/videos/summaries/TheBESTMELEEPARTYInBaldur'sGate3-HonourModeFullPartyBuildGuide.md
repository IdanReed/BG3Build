## Party overview

Four melee characters, no shared items, no story requirements [1:33]. The design rests on three pieces: a Wolf Heart Barbarian that grants advantage on every melee attack against enemies near it [2:38], two Sentinel + Protection carriers flanking the group so every attack on the tank draws a reaction attack [27:00], and an Oath of the Ancients Paladin whose auras let the party stand grouped without dying to AoE [22:17].

1. **Wild Heart (Wolf) Barbarian 8 / Battle Master Fighter 4** [3:40–21:47]. STR 16, DEX 14, CON 16, WIS 12. Reckless Attack on purpose to draw the AI [5:45]. Great Weapon Master at 4, +2 STR at 8 and 12. Aspect of the Beast: Elk for +5 party movement [8:49]. Feral Instinct at 7 [10:54]. Fighter at char 9–12: Great Weapon Fighting, Action Surge, Riposte / Precision / Disarming, ASI. Items: Soulbreaker Greatsword early, Sword of Chaos late, Adamantine Scale Mail, Ring of Regeneration, Amulet of the Harpers [19:13–21:16].
2. **Oath of the Ancients Paladin 12, Dexterity-based** [21:47–38:23]. STR 8, DEX 16, CHA 16, CON 14, WIS 12; respec at 6 to DEX 17 / CON 15 / WIS 8 once the aura covers saves [24:21]. Protection fighting style [25:26]; Sentinel at 4 [27:00]; DEX/CON at 8; DEX 20 at 12. Spells: Bless, Thunderous Smite, Command, Aid, Plant Growth, Warden of Vitality, Elemental Weapon, Lesser Restoration [26:27–33:14]. Items: Hellrider's Pride + Whispering Promise triggered by Healing Radiance, Phalar Aluve used for Sing, Armour of Agility, Sentinel Shield, every Charisma bonus (Mirror, Birthright) [35:48–38:23].
3. **Rogue 4 / Gloomstalker Ranger 8, dual daggers** [38:23–51:51]. DEX 17, CON 16, WIS 15. Rogue 1 → Ranger 1–5 (Two-Weapon Fighting, Sentinel at Ranger 4, Extra Attack, Misty Step) → Rogue 2–4 (Thief, ASI) → Ranger 6–8 (Iron Mind, Land's Stride, ASI). Items: highest-AC light armour, crit-range and damage-dice items such as the Knife of the Undermountain King and Dark Justiciar Gauntlets [50:51–51:51].
4. **Open Hand Monk 9 / Thief Rogue 3** — below.

## Monk / Thief chapter [52:24–1:06:29]

- Role: the problem solver. Stun the most dangerous enemy and operate outside the pod when needed [52:24]. Alternatives named: Lockadin, Sorcadin, Paladin 2 / Swords Bard 10; Way of Shadow is "pretty good" for the bonus-action teleport but Open Hand is "hard to pass up" [56:03].
- Ability scores: the elixir spread. STR 8, DEX 16, CON 15, WIS 16. Two spare points into INT (best for saves) or CHA, or DEX 17 plus Ethel's Hair for 18 "and then you get an extra feat out of the deal", called the most powerful version [53:27–54:29]. Athletics is the only skill that matters [53:57].
- Level order: Monk 1–6 at char 1–6 → Rogue 1–3 at char 7–9 (Thief at 9) → Monk 7–9 at char 10–12 [59:08–1:03:21]. Feats: Tavern Brawler at Monk 4 = char 4 [56:34]; Alert at Monk 8 = char 11 [1:02:48]. Rogue 1 is taken for Athletics Expertise [59:08]; Rogue 2 so dashes stop costing Ki [59:40]; Thief for the second Flurry, six attacks a round [1:00:12–1:00:42].
- Daily elixir: Hill Giant until Cloud Giant appears, "the only hard item requirement in this entire party build" [53:27, 1:03:53].
- Items [1:03:53–1:06:29]: Flawed Helldusk Gloves for unarmed damage, or Gloves of Dexterity to move points into WIS and use Ethel's Hair for WIS 18 (base AC 20); aim for AC 21–22 with Cloak of Protection; Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo best in slot; Vest of Soul Rejuvenation for +AC, heal on a successful save, and the reaction unarmed strike when an attacker misses.
- Typical turn: Stunning Strike the priority target, then Flurry. Topple is less valuable than usual because the party already has advantage; Stagger stops reactions while moving past melee enemies; Push knocks enemies off ledges into the party below [55:31–56:34].
- Stated weaknesses: squishier if built without elixir stats [53:57]; lower AC than the two Sentinel characters, so it draws attacks sometimes [1:05:56].

## Strategy and positioning [1:06:29–1:08:02]

Barbarian front and centre. Paladin front-side and Rogue on the far side, so each Sentinel covers both the Barbarian and the Monk and the two Sentinel zones do not overlap. Monk at the back. To bait the Vest of Soul Rejuvenation counter, move the Monk to the front line and drop the Barbarian back. The Monk may also roam outside the pod to hit enemies the pod cannot reach.

## Mechanics claims

- Wolf Heart's aura is applied to the enemy, so allies do not need to be within 7 ft of the Barbarian [6:46].
- The AI prioritises the target it is most likely to hit; Reckless Attack steers aggro to the Barbarian [5:45].
- Shared initiative: once the whole party out-rolls the enemies, party turns can be interleaved in any order [11:25].
- Sentinel does not trigger when an enemy attacks another Sentinel carrier [27:31].
- Aura of Protection's tooltip shows +0 but adds the CHA modifier [29:05].
- Most AoE damage spells target DEX; a pass halves, Aura of Warding quarters [29:36].
- Amulet of the Harpers grants advantage on WIS saving throws although the tooltip says checks [21:16].
- Dual-wielding makes both a main-hand and off-hand attack on Sentinel and opportunity reactions [44:04]. An off-turn Sneak Attack consumes the next turn's Sneak Attack [44:36].
- Tavern Brawler adds the STR modifier a second time to unarmed attack rolls and damage [56:34]. Stunning Strike's DC uses the higher of STR or DEX [57:35–58:06].
- Manifestations add WIS + 1d4 of the chosen type [58:36]. Wholeness of Body grants an extra bonus action [58:36].
- Rogue 1 taken mid-build still grants Expertise [59:08]; Rogue 2 gives bonus-action Hide, Dash and Disengage without Ki [59:40].
- Evasion halves damage on a failed DEX save and zeroes it on a success [1:01:12].
- Stillness of Mind casts automatically and costs the Action; fear immunity from the Paladin removes that downside [1:02:16].
- Step of the Wind makes jumping free [1:03:21].
- Vest of Soul Rejuvenation: heal on a successful save, +AC, reaction unarmed strike against an attacker that misses [1:05:25].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Four ranged characters were the easier Honour party; this is "a bit of a challenge run" that ends stronger than it looks [0:30].
- Great Weapon Master over Alert at Barbarian 4 unless initiative gear is missing [7:16].
- Battle Master over Champion because crits are not the focus [15:34].
- Ancients is "one way in the game" to keep a grouped party alive [22:17]; Aura of Warding "is what allows this party to stand close together" [30:41].
- Only one strength-elixir drinker per party, for stockpile sanity [3:40, 18:41].
- Open Hand over Shadow [56:34]. Tavern Brawler is "an absurdly broken feat" [57:05].
- Radiant Manifestation for Radiating Orb is "overkill" [58:36].
- The DEX 17 + Ethel's Hair monk is "probably the most powerful way to build this character" [54:29].

## Relevance to our party

The Monk chapter is the same chassis as Asterion: elixir Tavern Brawler, Open Hand, Thief, Alert, Flawed Helldusk Gloves → Kushigo boots → Vest of Soul Rejuvenation. The differences are level order (Thief at char 9 instead of 12), the Athletics Expertise pick, and the Gloves of Dexterity stat shuffle. From the Paladin chapter, Phalar Aluve's Sing mode and the Sentinel Shield route are the item calls that transfer to Bonbon and Charles. The Barbarian and Sentinel formation has no counterpart here, but the AI-targeting, shared-initiative and Stillness of Mind claims bear on how Charles and Asterion stand next to each other and to Gale.
