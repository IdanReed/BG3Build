## Build

**Race / character** [4:10–5:43]. Lae'zel is the demo. The narrator states Astarion is "almost certainly the most powerful monk" because his story abilities raise accuracy and per-attack damage; Karlach's Soul Coin mechanic also pays on multi-hit. For custom characters he recommends a race with armour proficiency (Githyanki medium, wood half-elf light + shields) because only Unarmoured Defence cares about armour at levels 1–2, or Duergar for Enlarge and out-of-combat invisibility.

**Class and level order.** Demo is Open Hand Monk 12. The multiclass section [35:23–38:29] builds Monk 9 / Thief Rogue 3: Monk 1–6 first (Open Hand at 3, Tavern Brawler at 4, Extra Attack + Stunning Strike at 5, Manifestation + Wholeness of Body at 6), then Rogue 1–3 at character levels 7–9 "because we want to reach the very powerful Rogue options as quickly as we can" [36:25], then Monk 7–9 at 10–12. Alternative finish is Monk 8 / Rogue 4 for a third feat; with the Strength elixir he says take 9/3 for Ki Resonation [38:29]. No respec beyond the level-4 attribute swap on the no-elixir build.

**Ability scores** [6:13–8:48, 17:42–18:12]. Three routes: a DEX monk (separate guide), item-replaced stats (Elixir of Hill Giant Strength sets STR 21; Gloves of Dexterity), or "muscle through". Demo no-elixir array: start 12 STR / 16 DEX / 16 CON / 14 WIS, respec at level 4 to STR 17, DEX 14, CON 14, WIS 12, INT 10. Elixir array: STR 8, DEX 16, CON 15, WIS 16, with Tavern Brawler's +1 into CON for 16/16/16 and STR 21 from the elixir. Late game on the elixir build he suggests respeccing DEX into WIS, since Alert covers initiative and WIS feeds Manifestation and the Kushigo boots [33:17].

**Skills** [8:48–9:19]. Athletics is the only check a monk makes ("kick an enemy off a cliff").

**Feats.** Tavern Brawler at Monk 4 [15:37–18:12]. Alert at Monk 8 [23:52]. Monk 12: +2 STR without the elixir; with it, Lucky, with Mobile, Mage Slayer and Tough as alternatives [28:04–29:36].

**Features by level.** 1 Flurry of Blows and Martial Arts bonus strike [9:19–10:23]. 2 Ki, Unarmoured Movement, Step of the Wind, Patient Defense as "a last resort" [10:56–13:02]. 3 Open Hand Flurry variants [13:34–15:37]. 5 Extra Attack, Stunning Strike [18:12–20:48]. 6 Manifestation, Wholeness of Body [20:48–22:20]. 7 Evasion, Stillness of Mind [22:20–23:52]. 9 Resonating punches, +20 ft jump, difficult terrain ignored [24:55–25:59]. 10 poison immunity [26:29]. 11 Sanctuary after long rest [27:32].

**Items** [30:08–35:23]. No locations are given. Graceful Cloth early, Vest of Soul Rejuvenation late (AC and a reaction attack). Bracers of Defence early, then damage gloves such as Flawed Helldusk Gloves. Gloves of Dexterity option: DEX to 8, points into WIS, 18 DEX from the gloves, +9 initiative, AC 19 with WIS 16. Cloak of Protection. Callous Glow Ring (+2 radiant vs illuminated targets; cast Light on the monk). Luminous Gloves with Manifestation of Soul for Radiating Orb on bosses. Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo. Bow passives work while punching: Bow of Awareness (+1 initiative) early, The Dead Shot (Improved Critical) late. Medium armour for defensive fights if proficient.

**Consumables** [7:14, 33:17]. Elixir of Hill Giant Strength daily from Act 1 vendors; Cloud Giant later, or for a single hard day.

**Typical turn** [11:27, 14:05, 18:44, 21:19, 37:27]. Hit-and-run: reach the priority target, spend one of the two Attack-action strikes on Stunning Strike, Flurry (Topple for advantage, Stagger against reactions, Push into hazards), kill, leave. Six attacks a turn under Wholeness of Body; eight with Fast Hands and Wholeness, before Haste or Bloodlust.

**Stated weaknesses** [2:34–3:39, 22:50–23:21, 23:52]. Lower AC and HP than other melee classes; +2 initiative on the STR array before Alert; Stillness of Mind auto-fires and eats a turn; late items are less powerful than other classes' late items.

## Mechanics claims

- Tavern Brawler adds the STR modifier a second time to unarmed attack rolls and damage [16:09–17:09], and gives +1 STR or CON [17:42].
- Elixir of Hill Giant Strength sets STR to 21 for the day regardless of base [7:14].
- Stunning Strike consumes one attack, not the whole Action [18:44]. Its DC keys off STR or DEX, whichever is higher, not WIS [19:47]. Few enemies are immune [20:18].
- A target knocked Prone by Flurry: Topple loses concentration with no saving throw [15:07]. Topple grants advantage on later attacks [14:05]. Stagger blocks reactions, including bosses' legendary reactions [14:36].
- Manifestation adds WIS modifier + 1d4 per attack; the d4 doubles on a crit [20:48].
- Wholeness of Body gives an extra bonus action for three turns [21:19].
- One Ki per monk level, refreshing on short rest [10:56].
- Evasion: half damage on a failed DEX save against damaging spells, none on a success [22:20].
- Stillness of Mind auto-casts against Charmed/Frightened and consumes the turn [22:50].
- Ki Resonation blast is 3d6 Force and chains between marked targets [25:28].
- Monk 10 poison immunity lets a monk stand inside an ally's Cloudkill [26:29].
- Callous Glow's +2 is Radiant even though the tooltip does not say [32:13].
- Bow passives (initiative, Improved Critical) apply to unarmed attacks while the bow is equipped [33:50–34:20].
- Thief's second bonus action is an extra Flurry every turn, about +50% damage [35:26–37:27]. Rogue 2 Cunning Action replaces Ki spent on Dash and Disengage [36:57].
- At levels 1–2 only Unarmoured Defence cares whether armour is worn [5:12].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Open Hand is "by far" the best monk subclass on damage [13:34].
- Tavern Brawler "completely breaks" bounded accuracy [16:39].
- Rogue is the monk multiclass that "stands head and shoulders above all of the others"; the Open Hand / Thief build was third on the narrator's top-11 builds list [35:23, 37:58].
- Elixir build: 9/3 split; no elixir: 8/4 [38:29]. Third feat on the elixir build: Lucky [29:36].
- Astarion is the strongest monk companion, Karlach next [4:10].
- Fighter 2 (Action Surge), Barbarian (Rage), one Monk level on a Paladin are minor alternatives [39:00].

## Relevance to our party

This is Asterion's build almost line for line: Open Hand 9 / Thief 3, Tavern Brawler on Giant Strength elixirs with the +1 into CON, Alert, Graceful Cloth then the Vest, Bracers then Flawed Helldusk Gloves, the Kushigo boots. The differences are the order of the Rogue levels (the video takes all three at character 7–9, so Fast Hands is online from 9; we take Rogue 1 at creation and 2–3 at 11–12), the late-game shift of DEX into WIS, Athletics as the monk skill, three items we assign elsewhere or not at all (Callous Glow Ring, Luminous Gloves, The Dead Shot as a bow-passive stat stick), and the statement that Astarion's story abilities are a damage source, which our file does not record.
