## Build

**Race** [2:36–3:37]: does not matter. Named: Wood Elf / Half-Wood Elf (movement), Githyanki (Misty Step, best two-handers), Gnome (advantage on mental saves).

**Ability scores** [4:09–8:21]: balanced route (shown): STR 16, DEX 14, CON 14, INT 8, WIS 8, CHA 14, one odd point. Elixir route: STR 8, DEX 16, CON 16, WIS 12, rest to CHA, with Elixir of Hill Giant Strength daily. Finesse route: DEX weapons, no two-handers. DEX is for initiative, not AC [7:17]. After Alert, respec DEX to about 10 and move the points to CON or CHA [19:50–20:21].

**Skills** [8:21–8:54]: Athletics, Persuasion.

**Oath** [9:25–10:26]: Vengeance, for Inquisitor's Might at 1, the spell progression, Vow of Enmity, and an oath that is easier to keep.

**Items** [31:35–35:54]: priorities in order: hit chance, crit chance and guaranteed crits, initiative, defence. Named: Oil of Accuracy, Diluted Oil of Sharpness, +1-attack items (Gloves of Dexterity), Killer's Sweetheart, Knife of the Undermountain King ("typically you won't be able to equip this one"), multi-die two-handers (Soulbreaker Greatsword, Unseen Menace early, biggest number later), Elixir of Hill Giant Strength, the Gloves of Dexterity respec trick (DEX 8 at creation, 18 from the gloves) opening uncapped medium armour such as Yuan-Ti Scale Mail, Elixir of Heroism if not drinking Strength (else Viciousness or Bloodlust), highest-AC heavy armour.

**Typical turn**: Vow of Enmity or Inquisitor's Might as the bonus action, then attacks and smites; up to three smites a turn from level 5 (Thunderous plus two Divine) [20:53].

**Weaknesses**: mobility [2:36], multi-attribute dependence [3:37–4:41], defences "relatively low for a melee build" [32:17].

**Multiclass** [35:54–37:26]: Paladin 2 or 5 is the floor. Sorcerer (Shield, higher slots), Warlock (short-rest slots for smites, "check out my lockadin guide"), Bard (Swords flourishes smite several targets), Fighter 2 (Action Surge). "Paladins won't want to dip one level of other classes."

## Mechanics claims

- Paladins have the highest burst damage; a crit doubles smite dice as well as weapon dice [1:02, 11:27].
- Inquisitor's Might: bonus action, +CHA weapon damage for two turns, Daze [9:55–10:26]. Lay on Hands healing rises by four per Paladin level [10:56]. Divine Sense gives two turns of advantage against certain enemy types [11:27].
- Great Weapon Fighting's reroll applies to smite dice [12:32].
- Thunderous Smite: 2d6, push 10 ft, prone; stacks with a Divine Smite on the same attack [13:36–14:07].
- Vow of Enmity: bonus action, Channel Oath, advantage on one enemy for 10 turns; bugged so self-casting gives advantage on every attack [16:12–16:45]. Abjure Enemy is a Charisma save [17:15].
- Alert +5 initiative [18:18]. Extra Attack and oath Misty Step at 5 [20:53–21:24].
- Aura of Protection: tooltip shows +0 but the bonus is CHA; 10 ft radius; must be activated after levelling and then stays on; applies to concentration saves [22:57–23:29, 26:04].
- Self-cast Haste that loses concentration costs the next turn [25:32]. Elemental Weapon: +1 to hit, +1d4 damage, until long rest [27:38]. Improved Divine Smite: +1d8 Radiant per attack [28:41].
- Killer's Sweetheart guarantees a critical, doubling the smite [32:49]. Gloves of Dexterity give +1 to attack rolls [32:17].
- Elixir of Heroism is "a permanent bless that stacks with normal sources of bless" [34:53]. Uncapped medium armour plus Gloves of Dexterity can beat heavy armour AC [34:53–35:24].

## Verdicts

- Vengeance is the best oath for damage [9:25]; Oathbreaker acknowledged, not chosen [8:54].
- Great Weapon Fighting over Defence [12:00]. Alert over every level-4 alternative [18:18–19:20]. Savage Attacker over STR ASI at 12 [30:13–30:44].
- Improved Divine Smite at 11 is the reason to stay mono-class [28:41]. Command is not worth preparing at CHA 14 [14:39].

## Relevance to our party

Charles shares the oath and the first seven Paladin levels, so rows 1–7 compare directly: Inquisitor's Might as the opener, the Vow of Enmity self-cast bug, Alert first, oath Misty Step at 5, Aura of Protection at 6 and a dismissive view of Relentless Avenger all match `charles.md`. The Strength frame, Great Weapon Fighting, STR ASI and Improved Divine Smite do not apply to a Charisma Hexblade behind a shield who stops at Paladin 7. Three items are new relative to our file: Divine Sense as a bonus-action advantage source against undead and fiends, the Aura of Protection activation step, and Elixir of Heroism stacking with Bless. The item priorities match the file's reasoning, with the caveat that against a Held target Charles auto-hits and auto-crits, so the first two priorities collapse for him.
