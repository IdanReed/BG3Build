## Build 1: Eldritch Knight thrower, Fighter 11 / War Cleric 1 [3:09–17:47]

Two stat splits: STR 8 / CON 17 with a daily Strength elixir, or STR 17 / DEX 14 / CON 16 without [5:12–6:16]. Defence style ("unselect and reselect, it's bugged") [6:16]. Fighter 2 Action Surge; Fighter 3 Eldritch Knight with Weapon Bond for a returning thrown weapon, cantrips Blade Ward + Minor Illusion, spells Shield, Magic Missile, Longstrider ("every party wants Longstrider mandatorily") [7:21–8:22]. Fighter 4 Tavern Brawler (STR, or CON on the elixir split) [8:22]. Fighter 5 Extra Attack, Enhance Leap [8:55]; Fighter 6 +2 STR (Alert if on the elixir) [9:27–9:59]. Char 7 War Cleric 1: three bonus-action attacks per day including thrown, Bless as the concentration spell [9:59–12:34]. Fighter 7–8: Misty Step, Alert at Fighter 8 ("I like getting this on every character for Honour Mode") [13:05]. Respec at char 11 to Fighter 11 / Cleric 0 for the third attack a level early, re-add Cleric at 12 [14:39]. Result: seven attacks in the opening round, 250–300 nova, AC ~25 with shield and plate, Nyrulna best in slot, Ring of Flinging [15:43–16:46]. "Maybe my number one pick in total for an honor mode build" [17:16].

## Build 3: Light Cleric 12 [31:18–42:13]

## Build 4: Swords Bard archer, Swords Bard 10 / Fighter 2 [42:13–55:41]

- Why Swords: the fourth slot is open; the Bard covers skills. Lore Bard "fits extremely well" defensively (Cutting Words, earlier Counterspell); Swords chosen because "swords bards are incredibly broken and give you a lot of damage" [42:13–42:44].
- Ability scores: archery split, high DEX and CON, CHA 14 only ("still gives us great dialogue skills because we're a Bard, we're going to get expertise"). Later respec once the Gloves of Dexterity arrive: DEX 8, CHA 16, WIS 14, "probably the optimal play" but optional [42:44–44:48].
- Skills: Sleight of Hand and Persuasion ("the two most important skills in the entire game"), Perception; Guild Artisan (Insight, Persuasion) "probably the best background for a party face". Astarion suggested as the origin for this build [43:15–44:17].

## Party wrap [55:41–58:49]

Lowest AC 19 (the Wizard, who wants to be hit). Two Counterspells, multiple Sanctuaries, Misty Step on everyone. Stated weaknesses: no guaranteed single-target deletion like a lightning build, and no repeated crowd control from a Tavern Brawler Monk [57:46].

- Tavern Brawler doubles the STR modifier on thrown attack and damage [8:22]. Defence style must be unselected and reselected [6:16]. War Cleric 1 gives three bonus-action attacks per day, thrown included [10:31]. Fighter 11 gives a third attack [14:39].
- Counterspell and Glyph of Warding are Abjuration and stack Arcane Ward [24:30–25:00]. Projected Ward blocks up to 22 damage [32:19]. Banishment targets Charisma saves [26:36]. 15/16/17 is the highest three-stat point-buy [33:53].
- Slashing Flourish doubles attacks and adds an inspiration die [46:22]. Helmet of Arcane Acuity stacks per arrow hit; Band of the Mystic Scoundrel makes Illusion and Enchantment spells bonus actions after a weapon attack, Vicious Mockery included [44:48, 53:37]. Hold Monster and Hold Person give automatic critical hits [51:34].
- A shield can be worn while wielding a bow [54:08]. Knife of the Undermountain King's passive applies to bow attacks [54:39]. Alert + DEX 18 = +9 initiative [51:04]. Greater Invisibility self-cast before combat gives free attacks and Acuity [51:34]. Friends is safe if you leave the area afterwards [45:20].

The other three builds share nothing structural with ours, but the party wrap names Tavern Brawler Monk crowd control as the thing this party lacks, which is Asterion's job in ours.
