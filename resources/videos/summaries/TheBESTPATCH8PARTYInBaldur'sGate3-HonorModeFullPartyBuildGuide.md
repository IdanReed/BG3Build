It is a darkness party: the Shadow Sorcerer casts Darkness with sorcery points so it can see inside, the Hexblade sees through it with Devil's Sight, and the other two need a blind-immunity item [4:10, 31:06, 40:52]. The presenter used it for his first Patch 8 Honour run [1:13:34].

## How the party uses Arcane Acuity

The Hexblade builds Acuity from the Gloves of Battlemage's Power and spends it through the Band of the Mystic Scoundrel [15:02–15:34]. The Paladin builds it from the Helmet of Arcane Acuity, two weapon attacks for four stacks, then casts Command with the Haste action [34:11]. The bard takes every flat save-DC item instead of Acuity [1:03:10]. The sorcerer needs no DC items because its save spells are damage spells [44:31, 49:40].

## Allocation rules stated

No overlapping items [0:30]. Ethel's Hair to the character a single ASI takes to 20 [3:08, 19:11]. Alert on the characters that can afford the feat; initiative gear to the one that cannot [5:43, 23:21, 32:39]. Save-DC gear concentrated on the bard [1:03:10]. Spell-slot elixirs to the Paladin [24:23]. Amulet of Greater Health to the sorcerer [50:42]. Resonance Stone on the Hexblade, its mental-save cost offset by Aura of Protection and Aura of Courage [14:30, 28:01]. Caustic Band on whoever has a free ring, for the acid omen [43:28].

## Mechanics claims

- Shadow Blade is a spell, so every attack with it triggers the gloves [15:02]. It carries no enhancement bonus, so it hits less often than an enchanted weapon [18:08].
- Warlock slots auto-upgrade, so a Warlock's Counterspell always beats spells up to level 5 [7:18].
- Steeped in Bliss doubles psychic damage and gives disadvantage on mental saves [13:28–14:30].
- Aura of Protection's tooltip now shows the CHA bonus [25:25].
- Divine Allegiance's self-damage interacts with the paladin's own radiating-orb gear [25:56].
- Thunderous Smite can be used in the same turn as Divine Smite but costs the bonus action [22:50].
- The Helmet of Arcane Acuity kept working after Patch 8 [34:11].
- Longstrider and Enhance Leap are rituals, free outside combat [55:23].
- Mantle of Inspiration's charm and Mantle of Majesty have no saving throw [58:29, 1:04:41].
- Warden of Vitality does not retrigger Chalice healing [1:09:22].
- Ring of Feywild Sparks grants an unlisted +1 spell save DC [1:10:55].
- Boots of Striding prevent being knocked prone [1:10:55].
- Plant Growth + Hunger of Hadar: enemies cannot jump out and move at quarter speed [1:05:12].
- Two Cone of Cold casts on Wet enemies end encounters [45:32].
- A Wizard level placed at char 1 costs CON save proficiency; Amulet of Greater Health compensates [51:14].
- Mirror of Loss offers "the full plus three benefit" [3:08].

## Relevance to our party

Charles and the video's two Hexblade characters share Shadow Blade, the Gloves of Battlemage's Power, Booming Blade, the Ring of Arcane Synergy, Luminous Armour, Boots of Stormy Clamour, Alert, Counterspell, Hunger of Hadar and Repelling Blast. The differences are the off-hand (Knife then Rhapsody instead of a shield), the late chest (Potent Robe), the Band on the gloves carrier, The Dead Shot in the ranged slot, and Sentinel Shield + Hellrider Longbow on the character without Alert. Bonbon matches the bard as the flat-DC carrier (Ketheric's Shield, a DC helmet) but has no Alert and no Ring of Feywild Sparks. The Mirror of Loss aside, the Longstrider and Enhance Leap remark, and the Sorcerer / Wizard split are party-level notes.
