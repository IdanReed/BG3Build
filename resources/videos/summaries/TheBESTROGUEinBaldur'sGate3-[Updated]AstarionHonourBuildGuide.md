## Build

**Race / character** [3:38–5:42]. Astarion is the demo and, the narrator says, "the best rogue and indeed the best companion for any martial class" because of his story abilities: an early bonus to hit chance and skill checks, and a late option to add damage to every attack. For custom characters: wood half-elf (Darkvision, shields, +5 ft) first; Githyanki (medium armour, Astral Knowledge) and Duergar (invisibility) next.

**Class and level order.** Thief Rogue 12, single class, dual hand crossbows. Subclass at 3 [15:33–17:37]; feats at 4, 8, 10, 12 (Rogue gets four) [17:37]. No respec other than optionally evening odd scores.

**Ability scores** [6:13–8:20]. Party face: DEX 16, CON 16, CHA 14, WIS 12. Not the face, standard: add STR 12 and WIS 14. Not the face, greedy: DEX 17, CON 16, WIS 15, evened out by later ASIs. CHA drops to 8 when someone else talks.

**Skills** [8:20–9:51, 22:48]. Always proficiency plus Expertise in Sleight of Hand. Stealth proficiency, with the second Expertise in Stealth (Persuasion instead if the face). Acrobatics against shoves. Investigation as the only other skill rolled outside dialogue. A spare point in Persuasion in case the scout is dragged into a conversation. Rogue 6 Expertise: Perception on Astarion (racial proficiency).

**Feats.** 4 Sharpshooter, left on unless hit chance is 30% or lower, or when a missed Sneak Attack must be secured [18:08–19:41]. 8 ASI (DEX 18, WIS 16), or Moderately Armoured on Astarion for medium armour and shields [24:53–25:54]. 10 ASI to DEX 20 [27:29]. 12 Lucky recommended; Alert less needed on a DEX build with initiative gear; Crossbow Expert called a waste; Mobile and Tough named as options [29:36–33:15].

**Features by level.** 1 Sneak Attack [10:24]. 2 Cunning Action [12:59]. 3 Thief Fast Hands [16:04]. 5 Uncanny Dodge [20:44]. 6 Expertise [22:18]. 7 Evasion [23:50]. 9 Supreme Sneak [26:26]. 10 bonus feat [26:58]. 11 Reliable Talent [28:32].

**Items** [33:15–37:53]. No act or vendor named. Two hand crossbows with the better one in the off-hand, because it fires more often; the enchantment number is the only weapon stat that matters. Hit-chance gear (a "Marksmanship hat"). Crit-threshold gear: Shade-Slayer Cloak; Knife of the Undermountain King equipped in the melee set applies while shooting. Damage-dice riders (Flawed Helldusk Gloves) over flat riders (Gloves of Archery), because dice double on crits. AC and save items, a shield if proficient, Misty Step items. Elegant Studded Leather for +initiative and the Shield spell.

**Typical turn** [13:30–14:31, 39:29–41:02]. From darkness or after a bonus-action Hide, shoot with Sharpshooter; hold Sneak Attack for a crit via the Ask prompt; off-hand shots with the second bonus action; Hide or Dash away. Tank one attack a turn with Uncanny Dodge; drink potions with spare bonus actions.

**Stated weaknesses** [2:36, 21:14, 41:33]. No Extra Attack, so fewer attacks than other martials; lower damage than dedicated damage dealers; low HP, offset by AC, Uncanny Dodge and Evasion.

## Mechanics claims

- Sneak Attack: once per turn, +1d6 at each odd Rogue level, doubled on a crit; triggers with advantage (darkness against a target without Darkvision, Hidden, Invisible, Blinded target, Prone target) or with an ally adjacent to the target and no disadvantage [10:24–12:28].
- Cunning Action: Dash, Disengage, Hide as bonus actions; three Dashes a turn [12:59–13:30].
- Thief's second bonus action works for off-hand attacks, Cunning Actions, potions, item and story bonus actions [16:04–17:37].
- Sharpshooter is about +30 damage a turn on three attacks [18:08].
- Uncanny Dodge fires on the first incoming attack each round with no reaction prompt [20:44].
- Evasion: zero damage on a successful DEX save, half on a failure [23:50].
- Reliable Talent: minimum 10 on proficient skill checks; removes natural-1 failures [28:32].
- Shield AC applies while attacking with ranged weapons [4:40, 37:22].
- Passives of a melee weapon in the melee set apply while attacking with the ranged set [35:49].
- Damage dice riders double on crits; flat bonuses do not [36:20–36:51].
- Two-Weapon Fighting style adds DEX to off-hand damage, about +10 a turn with two off-hand shots [42:35].
- Hiding outside every red vision cone needs no Stealth roll; hold Shift to see cones; Shift+Hide hides the whole party [38:26–38:58].
- The Sneak Attack reaction set to "Ask" lets you decline it on the first hit and use it on a later crit [39:29–40:30].
- The dual-wield toggle spends the bonus action on an automatic off-hand attack after every main-hand attack [40:30–41:02].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Rogue 12 is viable, but the class is front-loaded into levels 1–3 [0:31–1:01].
- Thief over Assassin and Arcane Trickster: "the rogiest Rogue" and the most options [16:04].
- Wood half-elf is the best rogue race [5:12].
- Sharpshooter is "the correct feat" at 4 [18:08]; Crossbow Expert not worth a feat [30:08]; Lucky the default at 12 [32:43].
- Best multiclasses: Fighter 5 or Ranger 5 for Extra Attack and a fighting style; Ranger 5 / Fighter 3 for Battle Master maneuvers; Spore Druid 2 (Symbiotic Entity); Bard 1 for skills; "monks or paladins work extremely well" as partners [41:33–44:09].

## Relevance to our party

Asterion takes Rogue 1 at creation and Thief 3 at the end, so only the dip matters: Fast Hands as the reason for the tail, Expertise in Sleight of Hand and Stealth, Acrobatics and Investigation as the other picks, and three interface points (Sneak Attack on Ask, dual-wield toggle off, hide outside vision cones without a roll). The video never proposes Rogue 4 for a feat on a dip character. Its item advice needs a held melee weapon or armour and does not apply to an unarmoured, empty-handed monk, except the statement that Sneak Attack rides on ranged attacks, which his fallback hand crossbows satisfy. Astarion's story abilities are named as the reason he is the best martial companion; our file does not record them.
