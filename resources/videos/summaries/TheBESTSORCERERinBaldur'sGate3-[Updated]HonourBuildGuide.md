## Build

**Race** [2:36–4:40]: Halfling hireling; Luck rerolls natural 1s on saves, including concentration. Half-Wood Elf (shield and light armour, 35 ft move), Duergar, Githyanki named.

**Ability scores** [4:40–5:42]: CHA 16, CON 16, DEX 14, WIS 12, INT 8. Alternative CHA 17 / WIS 10 with the Actor feat planned. Skills: Persuasion and other dialogue skills only.

**Subclass** [5:42–8:47]: Storm Sorcery. Draconic "does the most damage of any of the three but requires you to focus on a single element" (he points to his fire sorcerer video); Wild Magic discounted. Tempestuous Magic (bonus-action flight after a level 1+ spell, no opportunity attacks) is "one of the top five or so class abilities in the entire game" [7:44].

**Level order** (all Sorcerer, no respec):
- 1: Ray of Frost, Minor Illusion, Mage Hand, Friends [8:47–11:58]; Chromatic Orb, Shield [11:58].
- 2: Twinned ("absolutely mandatory") plus Distant or Extended [13:00]; Magic Missile [14:02].
- 3: Quickened, "the only choice that makes sense"; Cloud of Daggers [15:03].
- 4: Alert over an ASI [16:35–18:07]; Bone Chill [18:07]; Disguise Self as a ritual to trigger flight out of combat, instead of Misty Step [19:10].
- 5: Haste; Counterspell deferred [20:44].
- 6: Storm free spells (Create Water, Sleet Storm, Thunderwave); Lightning Bolt; Cloud of Daggers → Counterspell [22:16].
- 7: Wall of Fire, else Confusion [23:51].
- 8: ASI CHA 18; Hold Person [25:22].
- 9: Hold Monster [26:55].
- 10: Heightened; Mage Armour or utility [27:57].
- 11–12: Chain Lightning and Globe of Invulnerability in either order; ASI CHA 20 at 12 [29:31–32:04].

**Items** [32:04–37:16]: "almost completely item independent"; wants spell save DC and initiative only. Fistbreaker Helm; Boots of Striding; a save-DC robe or Elven Chain; Ketheric's Shield or Sentinel Shield if shield-proficient; Bracers of Defence early; Snowburst Ring; Markoheshkir best in slot, set to lightning for a free Chain Lightning per short rest.

**Consumables** [33:40–35:12]: collect scrolls, because metamagic applies to them. No elixir stated.

**Typical turn**: Haste cast before combat starts [17:41]; Quicken plus Twin for three spells in a turn [15:03]; Quickened Create Water then a lightning spell [23:20]; Heightened Hold Person or Hold Monster [28:29].

**Stated weaknesses**: low hit points [2:05]; Storm's Fury at level 11 "not very good" [29:31]; level 5 Sorcerer spells "a little bit disappointing" [26:55].

## Mechanics claims

- CON save proficiency means concentration usually fails only on a natural 1 or 2 [1:35, 3:07]. Halfling Luck rerolls those 1s [2:36].
- One sorcery point per Sorcerer level; spell slots convert into more [13:00]. Quicken costs 3 points; Quicken plus Twin is three spells in one turn [15:03].
- Cloud of Daggers: 4d4 on cast and 4d4 at the start of the enemy's turn, no save, no attack roll [16:05].
- Bone Chill stops healing and gives Undead disadvantage on attacks with no save [18:07].
- Ritual spells trigger Tempestuous flight at will out of combat [19:42].
- Haste on Honour gives martials one extra attack, not Extra Attack [21:15].
- Chain Lightning cannot be Twinned since Patch 6 but can be Quickened [30:01]; Markoheshkir's Chain Lightning could still be Twinned at recording [36:14].
- Globe of Invulnerability lets you detonate explosive barrels safely [30:33].
- A spellcaster who falls Prone loses concentration with no save; Boots of Striding remove that fail case [33:08–33:40].
- Metamagic applies to spells cast from scrolls and items (Twinned Otto's Irresistible Dance or Disintegrate scrolls) [33:40–35:12].
- Item and scroll save DC uses the ability of the class whose level 1 was taken most recently; Sorcerer 1 / Cleric 2 / Sorcerer 3+ makes items use Wisdom [38:49–39:19].
- Wizards learn scrolls up to total caster level, so a Wizard 1 dip on a Sorcerer can learn every Wizard spell [40:21–40:53].
- Fiend Warlock 1 gives Command; Quickened or Extended Command "synergise extremely well" with Sorcerer [37:47–38:18].
- Fighter 1 at character level 1 gives heavy armour and shields and keeps CON saves; Fighter 2 gives Action Surge [39:50].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Sorcerer is "the single strongest character class in Baldur's Gate 3" because it multiplies actions [0:33].
- Draconic is the highest-damage subclass; Storm is the best overall [6:43–7:14].
- Twinned Spell is "one of the most powerful features in the entire game" [13:00]. Minor Illusion "may even be the single best spell in the game" [10:22]. Magic Missile is "absolutely mandatory for any caster that can pick it up" [14:02].
- Cloud of Daggers is the best level 2 spell for both AoE and single target [16:05].
- Markoheshkir is "one of the best items in the game" and the Sorcerer is its best user [36:45].
- Recommended one-level dips: Warlock (Fiend) for Command, Wizard for spell access, Cleric for armour and utility, Fighter for armour and Action Surge [41:25].

## Relevance to our party

Different subclass, so Tempestuous Magic, the free lightning spells and the Create Water combos do not transfer. What does: the Sorcerer turn structure (Quicken plus Twin, Haste cast before combat), the metamagic order (Twinned, Extended or Distant, Quickened, Heightened), the Alert-first argument our file already answers with the Elixir of Vigilance, the Prone-ends-concentration rule behind the Boots of Striding and Night Walkers advice, the statement that metamagic applies to scrolls, and the multiclass section, which endorses our Fiend 1 dip for Command and describes a Wizard 1 scroll-learning dip that Gale's INT 8 cannot use. The item-DC ordering rule does not bite our Sorcerer-then-Warlock order because both cast from Charisma.
