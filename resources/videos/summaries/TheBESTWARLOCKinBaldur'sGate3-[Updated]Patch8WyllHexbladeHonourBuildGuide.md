## Mechanics claims

- Bind Hexed Weapon: CHA attacks; 20% chance per hit to apply Hexblade's Curse; bug at recording: each re-bind adds a separate 20% roll, so 20–30 re-binds give a near-certain curse [8:48–10:19].
- Hexblade's Curse adds proficiency bonus to "every single instance of damage": each Eldritch Blast beam, each Extra Attack, damage riders, "even on honor mode"; raises crit chance; once per short rest as a bonus action [9:18–10:19].
- 15 AC medium armour plus a shield is AC 19 at level 1 [10:51].
- Booming Blade: 1d8 on movement at 1; 1d8 on hit plus 2d8 on movement at 5; 2d8 plus 3d8 at level 10 [12:24–12:55].
- Shield is "unique to hexblade warlocks"; Hex stacks with the Curse [13:26–13:56]. Warlock 2 grants two spells in Patch 8, "probably a bug" [14:28].
- Darkness: enemies who cannot see through it cannot make ranged attacks at you; you attack with advantage; their melee inside is at disadvantage [17:05–17:35].
- Pact of the Blade folds into the hexed weapon and is taken for Extra Attack at 5 [18:06–18:37]. Warlock spells always cast at the highest slot, so Shadow Blade is always max dice [19:09, 20:11].
- Shadow Blade: 2d8 Psychic, one-handed, lasts until long rest, re-summon and re-bind daily; advantage against targets in dim light or darkness even if they see through the darkness [19:40–20:41].
- Counterspell almost always succeeds off the top slot [23:47]. Hunger of Hadar blinds with no save, Cold and Acid each turn, difficult terrain [24:19–24:50]. Repelling Blast pushes 15 ft [25:20].
- Accursed Spectre: reaction summon when a cursed enemy dies, a second Hexblade's curses count, strength scales with the corpse's size [25:52–26:24].
- Banishment targets Charisma saves; Phantasmal Killer is Hexblade-only; Staggering Smite is Concentration; Confusion spares allies [27:26–29:30].
- Banishing Smite: 5d10 Force, banishes at 50 HP or less, 10d10 on a crit, Concentration [31:02]. Cone of Cold 8d8, doubled on Wet [32:04]. Minions of Chaos last all day [32:34].
- Armour of Hexes: 50% to negate, the attack becomes a critical miss so riders do not fire [33:34–34:04].
- Hold Person from a level 5 slot targets four; Hold Monster one [34:36]. Warlock 11: Mystic Arcanum and a third short-rest slot [35:08–36:39]. Lifedrinker adds CHA to weapon damage again [37:11].
- A bow's holder passive (The Dead Shot) applies in melee [39:47]. Potent Robe adds CHA to Booming Blade [40:50]. With Arcane Synergy, Lifedrinker, Potent Robe and Booming Blade, CHA applies "up to six times" [41:51].
- Arcane Acuity stacks "up to a maximum of seven"; the Band makes an illusion or enchantment spell a bonus action after a weapon attack [42:23–42:54].
- Resonance Stone gives every nearby creature, allies included, disadvantage on mental saves and Psychic vulnerability [43:58]. Booming Blade is once per Action [45:29].
