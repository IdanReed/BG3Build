## Build

**Premise [0:00–2:36].** Tavern Brawler adds Strength to unarmed attack and damage and "increases your hit chance to well over what the game is designed to support", so nearly every published monk is Strength-based. A monk can instead run entirely on Dexterity by not taking the feat. The narrator states up front this is weaker than a Tavern Brawler monk but more flexible, single-attribute, and far less item-dependent.

**Race [2:36–4:09].** Astarion is the example and is called "the best monk in the game thanks to his unique story abilities giving him a ton of additional damage especially in the late game" (the ability is not named). For a custom character: Duergar (out-of-combat invisibility, Enlarge), Wood Elf or Wood Half-Elf (movement speed stacking with monk speed and Rogue Dash), Githyanki (once-a-day Misty Step).

**Ability scores [5:41–7:15].** DEX 17 / CON 16 / WIS 15, called "an extremely greedy attribute split" that a Strength monk cannot afford. Alternative face split 16/16/14 with CHA 12 and Persuasion expertise. ASI at character 5 evens this to 18/16/16 (AC 17 from stats alone) [12:26–12:57]. DEX 20 by character 12 [22:49].

**Level order.** Final split: Open Hand Monk 8 / Thief Rogue 4. No respec.

**Feats.** Character 5: ASI. Character 10: one of ASI (DEX 20), Alert ("extremely powerful on honor mode"), Sharpshooter (the narrator's pick, with two hand crossbows), or Sentinel (works because this monk holds a weapon) [19:12–21:47]. Character 12: ASI to DEX 20, or another of the above.

**Items [27:23–29:59].** No acts, vendors or locations are given. "Almost completely item independent." The one item called necessary is the Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo (Wisdom modifier added to unarmed strikes, "effectively +3"). Also named: a pair of hand crossbows or a bow (two hand crossbows if Sharpshooter is taken); Flawed Helldusk Gloves (captions say "flawed husk gloves"; guessed from context) for damage on both unarmed and weapon attacks; Bracers of Defence (20 AC shown with no clothing bonus; 22–23 reachable); whatever the best one-handed weapon found is; Dueller's Prerogative for the extra reaction, always active because there is no shield or off-hand weapon [29:27]. Then the generic list: initiative, non-armour AC, and unarmed or weapon damage.

**Consumables.** None mentioned. Elixirs of Giant Strength never come up.

**Typical turn [24:21–27:23].** Movement speed is the stat that matters: 45 ft at level 12 (46 shown). End the turn 45 ft from melee enemies so they cannot reach; Dash to 90 ft to stay outside most ranged attacks and still close next turn. Hand crossbows add 30 ft of reach without spending a bonus action on Disengage. Sometimes take the opportunity attack rather than spend a Ki on it. Hide as a bonus action in obscurement. On the attack: open with Stunning Strike or Topple, then Flurry of Blows.

**Stated weaknesses.** Less damage than Tavern Brawler [1:02]. AC falls behind once late armours appear [13:28]. Stillness of Mind auto-casts and eats the action when Charmed or Frightened, so look for immunities [21:47]. Every Cunning Action costs a Flurry [16:35].

## Mechanics claims

- [0:00] Tavern Brawler adds Strength to unarmed attack rolls and damage; it is Strength-based.
- [1:02] Monk unarmed strikes and monk-weapon attacks use Dexterity.
- [4:40] Rogue 1 grants light armour, hand crossbow and rapier proficiency.
- [7:46] Monk 1 makes three attacks on a Ki turn (Attack + Flurry), two otherwise.
- [9:19] Step of the Wind's jump does not cost a bonus action.
- [13:28] Monk 5: "four attacks per round with whatever weapon we've found plus our unarmed attack".
- [13:59] Stunning Strike's DC uses the higher of Strength or Dexterity; it only costs an attack; a failed attempt can simply be retried.
- [15:34] Monk 6: Wholeness of Body is once a day and grants an additional bonus action plus healing and Ki; Ki-Empowered Strikes overcome damage "immunities" (captions; likely resistances).
- [17:39] Thief's Fast Hands allows six attacks a turn, or three Dashes.
- [18:10] The second bonus action allows a second off-hand attack with a second equipped weapon.
- [20:46] Sentinel only triggers on weapon attacks, so a monk must hold a weapon to use it.
- [21:47] Stillness of Mind costs an action and casts automatically.
- [24:21] 45 ft movement at character 12; Cunning Action Dash doubles it to 90 ft.
- [27:54] Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo add the Wisdom modifier to unarmed strikes.
- [28:24] Sharpshooter on four hand-crossbow attacks is +40 damage.
- [28:57] 20 AC from stats plus Bracers of Defence alone; 22–23 with clothing bonuses.
- [29:27] Dueller's Prerogative's extra reaction requires no shield and no off-hand weapon; Rogue gives rapier proficiency.

## Verdicts and comparisons

- [0:30] Tavern Brawler is "broken", "breaks the game wide open"; a Dex monk "won't" match it [1:02].
- [1:32] Dex monk advantages over Tavern Brawler: flexible, single-attribute, much less item dependent, more strategic options.
- [2:36] Astarion is the best monk in the game.
- [10:52] Open Hand is the strongest of the three subclasses.
- [15:03] Monk 6 is worth taking before any second Rogue level, even though a Dex monk with a weapon "won't be getting full value" from Manifestation.
- [18:41] A third feat via Rogue 4 beats Monk 9's AoE.
- [21:16] Sharpshooter is the strongest third-feat option; Sentinel and Alert are the alternatives.
- [27:23] The character is almost completely item independent and "won't conflict with the rest of your party in any way".

## Relevance to our party

Asterion (content/characters/asterion.md) is the Tavern Brawler version of this exact chassis: Rogue 1 first, Open Hand, Thief tail, Bracers, Flawed Helldusk Gloves, Kushigo boots, Stun-or-Topple-then-Flurry. The video therefore answers what our build becomes on a day without a Giant Strength elixir (a DEX 20 monk, which the narrator rates as still very strong), and it differs from ours in level order (Thief at character 9, not 12) and endpoint (Monk 8 / Thief 4 for a third feat, which our traps already weigh). Its weapon-dependent items (Dueller's Prerogative, Sharpshooter hand crossbows, Sentinel) do not transfer to an empty-handed puncher. Its Astarion remark points at Vampire Ascendant, which our file never mentions.
