## Build

**Character and race.** Astarion, for the early hit bonus and the late story damage bonus [1:03–1:34]. Custom: Duergar is "probably" strongest (invisibility for surprise rounds), then Halfling, then Wood Elf / Half-Wood Elf; any race with longbow proficiency widens early itemization [1:34–2:35].

**Level order.** Bard 1–6 (Swords at Bard 3) → Ranger 1–3 at char 7–9 (Gloom Stalker at Ranger 3) → Rogue 1–3 at char 10–12 (Assassin at Rogue 3). Ranger before Rogue is recommended for hit chance and initiative; Rogue first for maximum damage or for Stealth Expertise [17:34–19:07]. One feat in the whole build [3:07].

**Ability scores.** Graceful Cloth variant (demoed): DEX 17, CON 14, CHA 16, WIS 10; Ethel's Hair to 18, Graceful Cloth to 20 [3:07–3:40, 4:42–5:12]. Gloves of Dexterity variant: respec to DEX 8 once the gloves are in hand, then CON 16, CHA 16, WIS 14, STR 12 [3:40–4:11, 5:12–5:43].

**Skills.** Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Persuasion, Deception; last pick Intimidation, Insight or Acrobatics [5:43–6:15]. Expertise at Swords 3: Persuasion and Sleight of Hand, or Stealth for Greater Invisibility play [11:25–12:27]. Rogue 1 Expertise: Stealth and Deception [23:18].

**Feat.** Sharpshooter at Bard 4 (char 4) [11:56–13:28].

**Fighting styles.** Two-Weapon Fighting at Swords 3, for the times a hand-crossbow pair or two passive daggers are equipped [10:24–11:25]. Archery at Ranger 2 (char 8) [20:11–20:42].

**Spells by level.** Bard 1: Minor Illusion, Friends; Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, Longstrider, Tasha's Hideous Laughter (or Disguise Self) [6:15–7:51]. Bard 2: Thunderwave [8:21–8:52]. Bard 3: Cloud of Daggers [9:53–10:24]. Bard 4: True Strike cantrip (a joke); Hold Person (over Enhance Ability, Heat Metal, Invisibility, Silence) [13:28–14:29]. Bard 5: Hypnotic Pattern (over Glyph of Warding, Plant Growth) [15:00–16:02]. Bard 6: Plant Growth [17:04–17:34]. Ranger 1: Favoured Enemy Ranger Knight (heavy armour, for Grymskull Helm), Natural Explorer Wasteland Wanderer: Fire, Insight [19:07–20:11]. Ranger 2: Hunter's Mark, Enhance Leap [20:42–21:14]. Ranger 3: Fog Cloud [22:17–22:48].

**Items [26:55–32:06].** Graceful Cloth + Ethel's Hair, or Gloves of Dexterity. Gloves of Archery until Ranger 1 grants longbows. Titanstring Bow with Elixir of Hill or Cloud Giant Strength (+5 / +8 a shot), "the best early-game damage weapon" [27:26–28:28]. Helldusk Gloves in the Graceful Cloth variant [28:28]. Late game: switch Titanstring for The Dead Shot [28:58–29:32]. Knife of the Undermountain King and Bloodthirst in the melee hands for passives [29:32–30:03]. Helmet of Arcane Acuity + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel [30:03–30:34]. Strange Conduit Ring [30:34]. Carry a hand-crossbow pair for the rare fight where it is better, and a wide stock of special arrows, "in particular the multi-shot arrow" (Arrow of Many Targets, resolving the caption) [30:34–31:05]. Drakethroat Glaive on the bow, plus the Snowburst Ring [31:05–31:35]. Mage Armour scroll on the Graceful Cloth for AC 18 at DEX 20; Cloak of Protection [31:35–32:06].

**Typical opening.** From stealth or invisibility, fire before combat (a Flourish if wanted); Assassin refunds the action and bonus action at combat start; two Flourishes are four shots, plus the Gloom Stalker attack, about seven attacks, up to ten with Haste, each an automatic critical for "40 or 50 damage" [24:52–25:53]. Leftover enemies are locked with Hold Person or Hypnotic Pattern through the Band [30:03–30:34].

**Stated weaknesses.** Takes longer to come online than other archers [0:00]. Sharpshooter's −5 is "a real penalty" at Bard 4; arrange stealth, high ground and Bless [12:58–13:28]. Only one feat [3:07]. Low AC and HP in the Graceful Cloth variant [4:11–4:42, 31:35–32:06].

## Mechanics claims

- Swords Bards do not get the Archery fighting style [10:24].
- Two-Weapon Fighting lets the off-hand hand crossbow add the full modifier [10:24–10:54].
- Sharpshooter's +10 applies to both Flourish attacks [12:27].
- Hold Person gives guaranteed critical hits when shooting from within 10 ft [13:59].
- Hypnotic Pattern is Illusion, so the Band can cast it as a bonus action [15:32].
- Extra Attack at Swords 6: four attacks with two Flourishes, 40 base Sharpshooter damage [16:34].
- Plant Growth is non-concentration; it quarters movement and pins enemies inside Hunger of Hadar [17:04–17:34].
- Ranger 1 grants longbow, medium armour and shield proficiency; Ranger Knight adds heavy armour [19:07–19:38].
- Hunter's Mark adds 4d6 across four attacks [20:42–21:14].
- Enhance Leap is a ritual [21:14].
- Gloom Stalker: +3 initiative (total +7 or +8 with DEX 18–20, "basically guaranteed to go before enemies"), bonus-action hide, first-turn extra attack with +1d8, bonus movement [21:45–22:48].
- Sneak Attack is doubled on a critical hit [22:48–23:18].
- Assassin: automatic critical hits against Surprised enemies; action and bonus action restored at combat start; advantage against any creature that has not yet acted [24:21–26:25].
- Sharpshooter's +10 is not doubled by a critical; Sneak Attack, the Gloom Stalker die and the Flourish die are [24:52].
- Titanstring adds the Strength modifier; Hill Giant elixir +5, Cloud Giant +8 [27:58–28:28].
- Helldusk Gloves add damage dice, attack-roll bonus and spell save DC [28:28].
- The Dead Shot raises hit chance and critical chance [28:58–29:32].
- Bloodthirst grants AC when held in the off-hand [29:32–30:03].
- Helmet of Arcane Acuity stacks per weapon hit; the Band casts Illusion or Enchantment as a bonus action after a weapon hit [30:03–30:34].
- Drakethroat Glaive can enchant any weapon, including a ranged one, for +1d4 cold or thunder [31:05].
- Snowburst Ring leaves ice surfaces on cold damage [31:35].
- Graceful Cloth is clothing, so Mage Armour applies; AC 18 at DEX 20 [31:35–32:06].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- "The very strongest endgame setup for an archery character in Baldur's Gate 3" [0:31]; "the best endgame archer" [32:06].
- Swords 10 / Fighter 2 is called "the overall strongest build in Baldur's Gate 3" and the recommended path if not taking this variant [17:34–18:05].
- Graceful Cloth variant "slightly better" than the Gloves of Dexterity variant, which is more defensive and more support-flexible [4:11–4:42].
- Ranger before Rogue "plays out smoother" [18:37].
- Ranger Knight is the only Favoured Enemy "that gives you a real bonus" [19:38].
- The Dead Shot over Titanstring late: "the additional hit chance I think is more important than the slight extra damage" [28:58].
- Two-handed bow setup "typically" better than a hand-crossbow pair; "chances are you're still using the Titanstring bow and an elixir of strength" [10:54, 30:34].

## Relevance to our party

The Swords 6 core, Sharpshooter at char 4, Hold Person at Bard 4, Hypnotic Pattern at Bard 5, Titanstring plus a Giant Strength elixir, stat-stick melee weapons, and the Acuity helmet plus Band all match Bonbon. The video's own claim that a two-handed bow beats hand crossbows supports the 2026-09-10 §3 decision. The Gloom Stalker and Assassin levels replace Bard 7–11, which on Bonbon would remove Hold Monster, Magical Secrets and the level 6 slot, so the split does not transfer. What does transfer for review: the initiative argument (going first so control lands before enemies act), the late-game Dead Shot recommendation, the Gloves of Dexterity respec to DEX 8, Plant Growth as a non-concentration pick, Minor Illusion at Bard 1, and stocking Arrows of Many Targets.
