## Build

**Character and race.** Astarion, for the hit bonus and the late damage bonus on a build that "makes more attacks than pretty much any other" [1:04–2:04]. Custom: Half-Wood Elf (shield proficiency and movement), Githyanki (medium armour, Astral Knowledge), Halfling [2:04–2:37].

**Level order.** Bard 1–6 (Swords at Bard 3) → Wizard 1–2 at char 7–8 (Bladesinging at Wizard 2) → Rogue 1–4 at char 9–12 (Thief at Rogue 3). An optional Wizard 1 dip at char 4 is mentioned and not taken [10:59–11:29]. The order is "the strongest at every point" but reorderable, always starting Bard [2:37–3:08]. Respec once the Gloves of Dexterity are obtained: DEX to 8, INT to 16 [6:16–6:49].

**Ability scores.** Start DEX 16, CON 14, INT 8, WIS 12, CHA 16 [6:16]. After the gloves: DEX 8 (18 worn), INT 16, so Bard and Wizard spells cast at full strength from both stats [6:16–6:49]. Alternatives named: Graceful Cloth, Ethel's Hair, Mirror of Loss [5:46].

**Skills.** Sleight of Hand and Persuasion are the two that matter; Stealth; Perception from High Elf [6:49–7:51]. Expertise at Swords 3: Sleight of Hand, Persuasion [10:28–10:59]; Rogue 1: Stealth [23:30–24:01].

**Feats.** Sharpshooter at Bard 4 (char 4) [12:31–13:34]. Rogue 4 (char 12): Alert recommended (+4 base initiative → +9); ASI or Mobile are the alternatives; ASI to DEX if using the Graceful Cloth or Hair instead of the gloves [25:34–27:07].

**Fighting style.** Two-Weapon Fighting at Swords 3 [9:26–9:58].

**Spells by level.** Bard 1: Minor Illusion, Friends; Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, then Longstrider, Tasha's Hideous Laughter or Feather Fall [3:08–5:46]. Bard 2: Thunderwave, Disguise Self, Speak with Animals or Feather Fall [7:51–8:55]. Bard 3: Cloud of Daggers; swap Feather Fall → Hold Person [9:58–10:59]. Bard 4: Mage Hand cantrip; Heat Metal (or Enhance Ability, Invisibility, Crown of Madness) [11:29–12:31]. Bard 5: Hypnotic Pattern; swap Cloud of Daggers → Glyph of Warding [14:05–15:08]. Bard 6: Plant Growth [15:40–16:11]. Wizard 1 (char 7): cantrips Ray of Frost, Bone Chill, True Strike (Fire Bolt is racial); spells Shield, Longstrider, Enhance Leap, Magic Missile, Fog Cloud, Grease or Chromatic Orb; scribe Haste [16:11–20:22]. Wizard 2 (char 8): Bladesinging; two spells from scrolls found [20:22–22:59]. Scroll shopping list: Haste, Misty Step, Invisibility, Greater Invisibility, Wall of Fire, Fear, Thunderwave, Gust of Wind; there is no Counterspell scroll [23:30–24:01, 35:56–36:58].

**Items [27:07–36:58].** Early: Marksmanship Hat, Gloves of Archery. Hellfire Hand Crossbow + Ne'er Misser; swap Ne'er Misser for a +2 hand crossbow once wearing Bhaalist Armour. Gloves of Dexterity (or Graceful Cloth with Helldusk Gloves). Potions of Speed. Helmet of Arcane Acuity + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel; alternatives Fistbreaker Helm, Diadem of Arcane Synergy, Helmet of Grit. Amulet of Greater Health. Melee hands: Phalar Aluve, Duellist's Prerogative, or Knife of the Undermountain King + Rhapsody. Snowburst Ring + Drakethroat Glaive (cold). Strange Conduit Ring. Scrolls as above.

**Typical turn.** Two main-hand attacks doubled by Flourishes is four shots; Hasted, six; plus two off-hand shots with the Thief bonus action, eight attacks before Terazul or Bloodlust [25:02–25:34]. Bonus actions rotate between the Band's Hold Person, Healing Word, Bladesong Climax, Dash or Hide, and the off-hand shot [24:31–25:34, 30:14–30:45].

**Stated weaknesses.** No Archery style, so Sharpshooter misses [34:55]. Casting another concentration spell while self-Hasted stuns you [17:14–17:45]. Bhaalist Armour locks the chassis to light armour [28:40–29:11]. Bladesong is two uses per long rest [36:58].

## Mechanics claims

- Healing Word (a bonus-action spell) builds Bladesong charges [3:40].
- Feather Fall is a ritual [5:15].
- Slashing Flourish builds two Bladesong healing charges per use [8:55–9:26].
- Two-Weapon Fighting adds the full modifier to the off-hand shot, +8 to +12 a turn [9:26–9:58].
- Hold Person plus archery within 10 ft is automatic critical hits [10:28].
- Mage Hand can throw a water bottle to make a target Wet, doubling cold and lightning [11:29–12:00].
- Sharpshooter's bonus is doubled by two hand crossbows and again by Flourish [12:31–13:34].
- Casting another concentration spell while Hasted on yourself stuns you [17:14–17:45].
- No scrolls exist for Longstrider or Enhance Leap; Shield is not available from a scroll; no Counterspell scroll [18:49, 19:50, 36:27].
- Bladesong needs a sword in the main hand and no medium or heavy armour or shield; charges come from any weapon attack; grants AC, movement, concentration and CON save bonuses, Acrobatics advantage [20:22–21:25].
- Alert takes the gloves build from +4 to +9 initiative [26:36].
- Bhaalist Armour doubles piercing damage within 10 ft, gives +2 initiative, is light armour [28:40–29:11].
- Ne'er Misser's Force damage is not doubled by Bhaalist Armour [29:11].
- Diadem of Arcane Synergy adds the spellcasting modifier to weapon damage once a condition is inflicted; Hellfire Hand Crossbow inflicts Burning when firing from hiding; illithid Ability Drain also triggers it [31:15–31:46].
- The Band of the Mystic Scoundrel works without the Helmet of Arcane Acuity [32:18].
- Amulet of Greater Health sets CON to 23 [32:49].
- Phalar Aluve's Shriek works from the main hand without swinging; without Two-Weapon Fighting or Dual Wielder there is no off-hand weapon beside it [33:20–33:52].
- Duellist's Prerogative grants an extra reaction and a crit bonus [33:52–34:23].
- Knife of the Undermountain King lowers the crit threshold; Rhapsody gives +3 attack, +3 damage, +3 spell save DC [34:23–34:55].
- Snowburst Ring's prone save uses the wearer's spellcasting DC [35:25].
- Strange Conduit Ring adds damage to every weapon attack while concentrating [35:56].
- Bladesong is two uses per long rest [36:58].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Helmet of Arcane Acuity is "probably the strongest" helmet; Helmet of Grit is the narrator's favourite [32:18–32:49].
- Alert is "the most powerful option for honour mode" at the char 12 feat [26:36].
- Bhaalist Armour is "the best armor in the game for this character" [28:40].
- Hellfire Hand Crossbow and Ne'er Misser are the two best hand crossbows [28:09].
- Shield is "the best defensive reaction in the game, basically mandatory for every character that can have it" [19:50].
- Cloud of Daggers is wanted by "almost every character that has access to it" [9:58].
- Combat cantrips are worse than firing an arrow on this chassis [17:45].
