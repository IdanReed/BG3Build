## Mechanics claims

- Below Honour, Barbarian 5 Extra Attack stacks with Deepened Pact for three attacks; in Honour it does not [22:23–22:54].
- Hold Person makes every attack a guaranteed crit, which triggers Mortal Reminder and the hammer [13:32–14:03].
- A crit hits regardless of AC, so GWM's -5 matters less on a crit build [15:37].
- The build should crit on 16+, or 14+ as the Dark Urge [18:44].
- Clown Hammer forces the save on both sides every crit; face paint grants immunity; Hideous Laughter works on Honour enemies immune to other disables [29:07–30:10].
- The Deadshot's crit bonus works from the bow slot without shooting [30:40].
- Frightened plus Prone leaves zero movement, so the target cannot stand [33:15].

## Verdicts and comparisons

- Duergar is "the most powerful pick" for a custom character [2:37].
- Oath of the Ancients is "generally speaking the best for a two-level dip" [23:58].
- Hunger of Hadar "wins encounters all by itself" [17:12].
- Arcane Acuity plus Command "may be more powerful on a Swords Bard archer" [34:15].

## Relevance to our party

Charles is the Dark Urge, but the video has almost no Dark Urge-specific content: no Deathstalker Mantle, no Slayer form, no Bhaal vendor stock. Its one Dark Urge mechanic is the crit-threshold "story event" at [18:44] and [31:11], which the wiki identifies as A Most Bloody Inheritance, a party-wide buff granted at High Hall to Bhaal's chosen; charles.md does not record it. The crit engine (GWM, Reckless Attack, threshold gear) is redundant on a Held target, the recorded reason charles.md already demotes Killer's Sweetheart, Covert Cowl and Sarevok's helmet. The one transferable idea is GWM's bonus-action attack on crits, which the wiki confirms works with one-handed weapons; charles.md rejected GWM only on the All In clause. The Barbarian 5 plus Deepened Pact statement supports party.md's three-attack assumption for Paladin 5 plus Deepened Pact below Honour.
