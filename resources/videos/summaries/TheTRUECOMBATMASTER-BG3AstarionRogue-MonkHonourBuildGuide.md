## Build

**Concept** [1:02–2:04]: a Dexterity Open Hand Monk / Swashbuckler Rogue that stuns, disarms, blinds, topples, pushes and staggers. Bonus-action economy over Tavern Brawler damage; armoured for most of the game.

**Origin and race** [2:37–4:40]: Astarion, for his early bonuses to hit and his endgame boost to all weapon and unarmed damage, both multiplied by attack count. Karlach also works (soul coins said to be bugged to double on unarmed strikes without Rage). Githyanki or Shield Dwarf for medium armour; Wood Elf for speed; High Elf for Booming Blade, which on Astarion is only selectable if he is the main character.

**Ability scores** [5:11–6:11]: DEX 16, CON 16, WIS 12, CHA 14 (armoured face). Unarmoured variant CON 14, CHA 12, WIS 16. Tavern Brawler variant: STR stays 8, drink Giant Strength. Skills: Persuasion and Sleight of Hand Expertise, Acrobatics, Stealth.

**Level order** (final Open Hand Monk 8 / Swashbuckler Rogue 4):

- Char 1 Rogue 1 [4:40]. Frequent respeccers: Rogue 4 to char 4, respec into this at char 5 [7:12].
- Char 2–4 Monk 1–3 [7:12–10:19]: Flurry, Dextrous Attacks, Unarmoured Defence; Patient Defence, Step of the Wind; Way of the Open Hand.
- Char 5 Monk 4 [12:24]: ASI DEX by default. Tavern Brawler plus elixir is "the mechanically most powerful option"; Moderately Armoured if Hag's Hair covers DEX.
- Char 6 Monk 5 [13:59]: Extra Attack, Stunning Strike.
- Char 7 Monk 6 [15:00]: Manifestation (radiant for items, psychic for a Resonance Stone party), Wholeness of Body.
- Char 8 Rogue 2 [17:07]: Cunning Action. "We've gotten all of the best things from Monk with six levels."
- Char 9 Rogue 3 Swashbuckler [18:10]: Fancy Footwork, Rakish Audacity.
- Char 10 Rogue 4 [19:45]: Dirty Tricks; ASI DEX to 20.
- Char 11–12 Monk 7–8 [21:19–25:28]: Evasion, Stillness of Mind, feat Sentinel. Alternatives weighed: Fighter 2, Barbarian 2, Spore Druid 2, Rogue 5–6; Alert, Tough, Mobile, Mage Slayer, ASI, Moderately Armoured, Tavern Brawler.

**Items** [25:58–36:45]: Duellist's Prerogative (best finesse weapon; its extra reaction pairs with Sentinel), Knife of the Undermountain King, Crimson Mischief, Blade of the First Blood. Bhaalist Armour or Elegant Studded Leather. Mask of Soul Perception or Helmet of Grit. Hellrider's Longbow, The Deadshot, or Ne'er Misser plus Hellfire Hand Crossbow with Sharpshooter. Amulet of Greater Health (drop CON to 8, move points to CHA and WIS), Amulet of the Harpers, Amulet of Misty Step. Ring and Cloak of Protection; Callous Glow Ring and Luminous Gloves with Manifestation of Soul. Helldusk Gloves, Flawed Helldusk Gloves, Martial Exertion Gloves. Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo with boosted WIS, else Disintegrating Night Walkers. Medium-armour options: Armour of Agility, Helm of Balduran, Adamantine pieces. Stat items: Giant Strength elixirs, Graceful Cloth (unarmoured style), Gloves of Dexterity, Warped Headband of Intellect.

**Typical turn** [0:00–1:33]: Stunning Strike, retry with Extra Attack; Withering Cut and Sneak Attack for damage; Flick o' the Wrist disarm; Flurry Topple. Wholeness of Body pre-cast adds a third bonus action.

**Stated weaknesses**: Grit HP management, Wholeness can heal past 50% [16:35]; Stillness of Mind casts itself [22:54]; Wisdom saves [31:05]; Flick needs a finesse weapon [19:45]; no shield with Duellist's Prerogative [35:13].

## Mechanics claims

- [3:09] Karlach's soul coins double on unarmed strikes without Rage (bug).
- [4:10] Astarion's High Elf cantrip changes only if he is the main character.
- [8:15] Dextrous Attacks lets a longsword attack with DEX.
- [8:46] A monk-weapon attack grants the bonus-action unarmed strike.
- [9:17] Unarmoured Defence may beat light armour early.
- [13:59] Stunning Strike costs part of an action; Ki refreshes on short rest.
- [15:31] Manifestation's d4 doubles on crits; psychic suits a Resonance Stone party.
- [16:04] Wholeness of Body: an action, three turns of an extra bonus action, Ki refund, heal.
- [18:40–19:12] Fancy Footwork stops opportunity attacks from your melee target. Rakish Audacity: +2 initiative, +3 at Rogue 5, Sneak Attack without advantage.
- [19:45–20:16] Flick o' the Wrist needs a finesse weapon and a CHA DC; Sand Toss uses a DEX DC, two-turn blind.
- [20:16] A prone, disarmed enemy usually loses its turn.
- [24:58] Savage Attacker does not apply to unarmed strikes.
- [26:30] Duellist's Prerogative grants an extra reaction: two opportunity attacks with Sentinel.
- [27:32] Blade of the First Blood main-hand inflicts piercing vulnerability.
- [29:03] Helmet of Grit is light armour; unarmoured monks cannot use it.
- [31:05] Amulet of the Harpers: advantage on Wisdom saves although the tooltip says checks.
- [31:36] Callous Glow Ring is radiant and works with Manifestation of Soul; Luminous Gloves stack Orb the same way.
- [32:06] Helldusk Gloves boost weapon and unarmed attacks.
- [33:08] Martial Exertion self-damage drops you back under Grit's 50%.
- [34:41] Helm of Balduran: stun and crit immunity; cannot pair with Grit.
- [35:45] Giant Strength elixirs move attack and damage to STR even at high DEX.

## Verdicts and comparisons

- [10:19] Open Hand "shines most in dexterity-based builds"; Tavern Brawler builds "become all about Tavern Brawler".
- [11:53] "Build your own Drunken Master, except it's actually good."
- [13:59] Stunning Strike is "probably the single most powerful disable in the entire game, except possibly Mantle of Majesty or Arcane Acuity Commands".
- [16:04] "This build uses its bonus actions better than just about any other character in the entire game"; Wholeness of Body is "ludicrously strong".
- [18:40] Rakish Audacity lets the build skip Alert; [23:57] Alert "still always good".
- [23:26] Two more Monk levels are "the most natural pick"; [25:28] Sentinel the favourite third feat.
- [26:30] Duellist's Prerogative best finesse weapon here; [28:33] Helmet of Grit "absolutely incredible"; [30:04] Amulet of Greater Health "awesome".

## Relevance to our party

Asterion is the same chassis (Open Hand Monk plus a Rogue tail on Astarion) built the other way: Tavern Brawler on Giant Strength, empty hands, unarmoured, Thief 3 for Fast Hands. The Swashbuckler tail depends on a main-hand finesse weapon (Flick, Sneak Attack) and on Helmet of Grit for bonus actions; Asterion's empty-hand and Unarmoured Defence rules exclude both. What transfers is the ordering argument (Rogue tail straight after Monk 6), the Astarion-specific damage sources it names (Happy, Ascension), and item placements that match ours (Mask of Soul Perception, Kushigo boots, Harpers, Flawed Helldusk, Graceful Cloth).
