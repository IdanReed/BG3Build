## Build

**Race.** Astarion. Race barely matters; Wood Elf / Half Wood Elf for speed, Duergar for invisibility, Githyanki for Misty Step and Astral Knowledge, Halfling for rerolling 1s [1:02–2:03].

**Class split and level order.** Final Fighter 7 / Swords Bard 5. Char 1 Bard 1 for face and lockpicking skills (alternative: Fighter to 5 first) [2:03–2:34]. Char 2 Fighter 1: Archery, medium armour + shields, Second Wind [7:17–8:20]. Char 3 Fighter 2: Action Surge [8:20]. Char 4 Fighter 3: Arcane Archer, Guidance cantrip, shots Banishing / Seeking / Piercing [9:20–14:31]. Char 5 Fighter 4: Sharpshooter [14:31]. Char 6 Fighter 5: Extra Attack [15:32]. Char 7 Bard 2: Song of Rest, Tasha's Hideous Laughter [16:34–17:37]. Char 8 Bard 3: College of Swords, Two-Weapon Fighting, Hold Person, Expertise Persuasion + Sleight of Hand or Stealth [17:37–20:45]. Char 9 Bard 4: ASI DEX, True Strike, Invisibility / Enhance Ability / Crown of Madness [20:45–21:47]. Char 10 Bard 5: Font of Inspiration, Hypnotic Pattern [21:47–23:19]. Char 11 Fighter 6: ASI to DEX 20 or Alert [23:50–24:51]. Char 12 Fighter 7: 7 Arcane Shot uses a short rest, Magic Arrow, Curving Shot ("bugs out pretty frequently"), fourth shot Shadow or Enfeebling [24:51–26:56]. Late respec once the Amulet of Greater Health is on: CON 8, CHA 16, WIS 12 [35:11–35:43].

**Ability scores.** STR 8, DEX 16, CON 14, INT 14 (Arcane Shot DC), CHA 14 (Bard DC, skills, scrolls), the odd point to WIS [4:41–6:16].

**Spells.** Bard 1: Dissonant Whispers, Healing Word, Heroism (self-concentrated for Frightened immunity and 5 temp HP a turn), Longstrider or Sleep or Tasha's [3:05–4:41]. Cantrips Friends + Minor Illusion, the latter to group enemies for Piercing Arrow [2:34–3:05].

**Arcane Shots.** Banishing always ("a 4th-level spell as an attack") [10:21–11:24]; Seeking (always hits, targets like Magic Missile through gaps) [11:24–11:54]; Piercing (33 ft line, save for half) [11:54–13:28]. Bursting, Enfeebling (half weapon damage, almost nothing immune), Grasping (most damage), Shadow (blind) also rated; Beguiling only with a Glamour Bard [13:28–14:31, 25:23–26:24].

**Items** [26:56–38:16]. Titanstring + Giant Strength elixir is "the highest damage setup throughout the entire game"; late he prefers The Dead Shot because hitting matters more than damage per shot once on-hit effects stack; or Hellfire Hand Crossbow + Ne'er Misser (or a +2 hand crossbow under Bhaalist) [27:27–28:30]. Bhaalist Armour: piercing vulnerability within 10 ft, "quadrupling damage" with Hold Person auto-crits, needs moving up close [28:30–29:00]; Graceful Cloth early; Armour of Agility for saves [29:00–29:31]. Melee passives: Knife of the Undermountain King, Rhapsody (+3 attack, damage, spell save DC; the DC applies to Arcane Shots), or Sentinel Shield; Agility + Sentinel = AC 24 [29:31–30:32]. Helmet of Arcane Acuity + Band of the Mystic Scoundrel; a Flourish triggers Acuity twice [30:32–31:33]. Drakethroat Glaive cold enchant + Snowburst Ring: ice under every arrow's target, DEX save against the Acuity-boosted item DC, prone on their turn loses it; fire melts it [31:33–32:36]. Gloves: Archery → Helldusk, Martial Exertion (extra attacks, "the extra damage mostly doesn't matter") or Luminous + Callous Glow Ring [32:36–34:08]. Rings: Strange Conduit, Ring of Free Action for the low WIS save [34:08–34:40]. Stat replacers: Gloves of Dexterity, Headband of Intellect (conflicts with the helmet), Amulet of Greater Health [34:40–35:43]. Hold Monster scrolls cast with CHA as a bonus action through the Band [35:43–36:43]. Endgame list at [36:43–38:16], called "overkill". Illithid: Favourable Beginnings, Luck of the Far Realms, Ability Drain [38:16].

**Typical turns.** Flourish, then Banishing Arrow on the most threatening enemy [18:40–19:10]. At Bard 5: two Flourishes, Action Surge, a third Flourish to max Acuity, then Banishing Arrow; or two Flourishes plus two Banishing Arrows to remove four enemies [22:18–22:49].

**Stated weaknesses.** Low WIS save against Hold Person and Frightened [3:38, 34:08]; saving throws generally [29:31]; three attributes to feed [34:40]; Curving Shot unreliable [25:23].

## Mechanics claims

- A shield's AC applies while attacking with a bow [7:48].
- Action Surge, Arcane Shots and (from Bard 5) Bardic Inspiration all refresh on a short rest [8:50–9:20, 21:47].
- Seeking and Piercing Arrow have no attack roll: no Sharpshooter bonus, some on-hit effects do not fire [11:24, 12:26, 14:31].
- Piercing Arrow's tooltip is wrong; the rule is save for half [12:26–13:28].
- Slashing Flourish and an Arcane Shot cannot share one attack [17:37–18:09].
- A Flourish triggers Acuity twice; each stack is +1 spell save DC and applies to Arcane Shots [18:40, 31:02–31:33]; Rhapsody's DC bonus applies to them too [30:01].
- Knife of the Undermountain King raises crit chance on ranged attacks while held [29:31–30:01].
- Bhaalist: enemies within 10 ft vulnerable to piercing; a Held target takes auto-crits, so 4× [28:30–29:00].
- Snowburst ice uses the item DC, boosted by Acuity; falling prone on your own turn ends it [31:33–32:36].
- Scrolls cast with CHA because the casting stat only changes when a class with one is taken [35:43–36:43].
- Amulet of Greater Health lets CON drop to 8 [35:11].

## Relevance to our party

A Fighter-heavy cousin of Bonbon's build: Fighter 7 / Swords Bard 5 against our Swords Bard 11 / Fighter 1. It caps at 3rd-level slots and takes Hold Monster from scrolls, so it cannot fill Bonbon's Hold Monster concentration lane or upcast Command to six targets; it gains Banishing Arrow, auto-hit arrows, Action Surge and a third feat. The items are where it touches us: Bhaalist Armour with Hold auto-crits, the Snowburst Ring with a cold Drakethroat enchant (we already cast the Glaive daily), Martial Exertion Gloves against Helldusk, The Dead Shot late, Rhapsody on the archer, and Ring of Free Action for the WIS-save hole both videos name. It also confirms the shield-with-a-bow AC rule and the two-triggers-per-Flourish count our helmet entry relies on.
