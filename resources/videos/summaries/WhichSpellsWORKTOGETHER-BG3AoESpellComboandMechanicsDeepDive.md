## Mechanics claims

Layer model [2:36–4:39]
- Six hazard classes: surfaces, super surfaces, clouds, objects, floating effects, special effects. One surface or super surface per spot. One cloud per spot. Objects as many as fit, never overlapping. Unlimited floating effects, limited only by concentration, but not two of the same spell. Unlimited special effects (Moonbeam, Sleet Storm, Glyph of Warding), Glyph capped at one.
- Rule of thumb: one from surfaces or super surfaces, one cloud, then as many floating and special effects as you can pay for.

Surfaces [5:40–10:53]
- Eleven common player surfaces: water, alcohol, blood, ice, acid, poison, lightning, web, grease, fire, wall-of-fire fire. Most recent overwrites. Environmental surfaces get overwritten by these.
- Electrified water and ice revert to water after two turns [7:16].
- Water + lightning damage = electrified water in either order [7:48]. Water + cold damage = ice, but only cold onto existing water; Create Water onto ice gives water [8:19–8:50]. Water + fire DAMAGE = nothing. Water + a fire SURFACE = the water vanishes and a steam cloud appears, either order [8:50–9:20].
- Blood freezes, does not electrify, no steam. Alcohol freezes and burns to a fire surface, does not electrify. Grease and web burn to a fire surface [9:52–10:22].

Super surfaces [10:53–14:00]
- Spike Growth, Plant Growth, Entangle share the surface layer but always beat ordinary surfaces in either order; among themselves most recent wins. Spike Growth eats an existing water surface; Create Water cast onto spikes does nothing [11:58–12:29].
- Plant Growth and Entangle burn to a fire surface from any fire damage. Spike Growth does not burn and overwrites a fire surface [13:00–14:00].

Clouds [14:00–17:41]
- One cloud per spot. Fog Cloud and Darkness are "super clouds": they always beat Stinking Cloud, Cloudkill, steam and the ice clouds from broken Wall of Ice pillars. Between Fog Cloud and Darkness, most recent wins [14:33–15:36].
- Demo: Darkness over steam removes the steam; Cloudkill cast over Darkness leaves Darkness intact and only exists outside it; Stinking Cloud over Cloudkill eats the Cloudkill [15:36–17:10].
- Steam from water meeting a fire surface can eat a Stinking Cloud or Cloudkill [17:10]. The presenter says he believed clouds simply overwrote by recency until this demo [14:33].

Objects [17:41–19:14]
- Wall of Stone and Wall of Ice pillars coexist with clouds and surfaces and take their damage, but cannot overlap other objects. Wall of Thorns is passable and can block placing other walls.

Floating effects [19:14–22:50]
- Cloud of Daggers, Insect Plague, Blade Barrier stack with each other, with clouds and with surfaces. Two copies of the same spell both deal initial damage when cast onto a creature, but only one damage-over-time condition ticks [20:47–21:47]. A creature walking into two overlapping copies often takes only one initial hit; called inconsistent and buggy [21:47–22:50].

Special effects [23:22–24:55]
- Glyph of Warding clips through walls, one at a time. Sleet Storm creates ice that overwrites fire instead of melting. Moonbeam moves and sometimes fails against solid objects.

Combos [25:26–33:15]
- L1: Create Water + Ray of Frost (cantrip) to freeze it + Fog Cloud. Blinded creatures cannot target beyond 5 ft and cannot jump, so they walk across the ice and roll a DEX save or fall prone each move [25:26–26:58].
- L2: Spike Growth (20 ft radius) + upcast Fog Cloud for the larger area. Half speed means their whole move stays inside, they must Dash, and they take damage per distance [26:58–28:02].
- L3: Plant Growth (quarter speed) + Hunger of Hadar (same 20 ft radius; blinded, cannot jump out). 30 ft of movement becomes 7.5 ft; "with no saving throw stuns entire encounters for multiple turns". Shove escapers back in [28:02–29:03].
- Sleet Storm is the alternative surface where fire is in play, because Plant Growth burns; Spike Growth cannot burn [29:03–29:34]. Add Stinking Cloud: Hunger of Hadar, Plant Growth and Stinking Cloud all coexist [29:34–30:06].
- L4: Evard's Black Tentacles (not flammable) + Wall of Fire, plus Cloud of Daggers or Hunger of Hadar [30:06–31:09].
- L6: Wall of Thorns + Blade Barrier; both have save-DC bugs that lower their damage [31:09–31:40].
- Everything at once: ice clouds from a cancelled Wall of Ice, Wall of Thorns, Blade Barrier, upcast Cloud of Daggers, Hunger of Hadar [32:10–33:15].

## Verdicts

No tier list. "Anything that you think would catch fire will catch fire... anything that you think would freeze will freeze" [10:22]. Plant Growth plus Hunger of Hadar is presented as the cleanest level-3 encounter shutdown [28:02]. Typical spend is two, at most three, spell slots per hard encounter [30:38].

## Relevance to our party

Charles's Hunger of Hadar is the party's one hazard spell, used only when nothing is Holdable. Gale's Armour of Landfall grants Plant Growth once per short rest, which is the other half of the video's level-3 combo, but Gale is a fire caster and any fire damage burns the Plant Growth away. Gale's optional Ice Storm lays ice that his own fire would melt. Charles's Darkness Arrow clouds are "super clouds" here, so steam or other clouds never remove them; Bonbon's Cloak of Cunning Brume fog is the only other super cloud in the party. The party has no Create Water, Fog Cloud, Spike Growth, Sleet Storm or Stinking Cloud, so the level-1, level-2 and cloud-stacking combos are not available as built.
