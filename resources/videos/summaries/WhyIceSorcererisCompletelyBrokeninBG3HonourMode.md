# Why Ice Sorcerer Is Completely Broken in BG3 Honour Mode

- Playlist index: 26
- Video ID: `GWZt137qtww`
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWZt137qtww
- Format: Solo Honour Mode challenge run with Dark Urge role-play

## Executive Summary

The video demonstrates a pure Draconic Bloodline Sorcerer built around Ray of Frost, cold vulnerability, additive Charisma damage, ice surfaces, and repeated cantrip casts. By level 6, Ray of Frost receives the Charisma modifier from Draconic Elemental Affinity, Potent Robe, and Necklace of Elemental Augmentation. With 24 Charisma in Act 2, that creates 21 guaranteed flat damage before dice—or 42 against a cold-vulnerable target. Mourning Frost can apply Chilled, water creates Wet, and either condition enables vulnerability; Snowburst Ring turns cold hits into ice surfaces that repeatedly deny enemy turns.

The run succeeds solo by combining this damage engine with stealth, Sanctuary-like safety tools, summons, forced movement cheese, Globe of Invulnerability, speed effects, and careful item acquisition. The final build uses 24 Charisma, dual-wields Mourning Frost and Markoheshkir, and can produce a 196-damage Ray of Frost burst against the Netherbrain with Gemini Gloves.

## Damage and Control Engine

Mourning Frost is assembled early from three Underdark pieces. It adds cold damage, improves cold damage, grants Ray of Frost, and may apply Chilled on a DC 12 Constitution save. Chilled makes a target vulnerable to cold; adding Wet to a Chilled enemy freezes it, skipping its turn. Mage Hand can throw water bottles without consuming the Sorcerer's action.

Three separate Charisma riders then stack on Ray of Frost:

- Draconic Bloodline's Elemental Affinity at level 6.
- Potent Robe, obtained by rescuing the Moonrise tieflings after preserving Alfira.
- Necklace of Elemental Augmentation from the crèche.

Cold-control equipment expands the loop. Winter's Clutches applies Encrusted with Frost; Boots of Stormy Clamour add Reverberation whenever a condition lands; Coldbrim Hat adds more frost stacks; Snowburst Ring creates ice under damaged targets. Ring of Free Action protects against paralysis and restraint. These effects make ordinary Ray of Frost casts damage, slow, debuff physical saves, generate prone risk, and sometimes freeze.

In Act 3, Birthright raises Charisma, the Mirror of Loss restores it to 24, Cloak of the Weave improves spell attacks and DC, and Gemini Gloves add another cantrip target once per short rest. Markoheshkir's cold attunement adds proficiency to cold damage and provides more ice magic. Dual Wielder allows Markoheshkir and Mourning Frost simultaneously, trading the shield's critical immunity for maximum offense.

## Encounter Findings

Sleet Storm and recast ice surfaces create a “hallway of doom” at Moonrise, letting the Sorcerer kill enemies gradually with little risk. The strategy is slow but safe: enemies slip, bunch at chokepoints, and struggle to reach the caster.

Against Ketheric, repeated Ray of Frost both deals high damage and creates prone opportunities. For Myrkul, Elemental Adept: Cold is strongly recommended because it bypasses resistance; alternatively, keep the boss Wet. The run uses summons, Scratch to free Nightsong, Elixir of Vigilance for initiative, Bloodlust and speed potions for actions, and area spells or Chain Lightning scrolls to prevent Necromites from healing the boss.

The Act 3 House of Grief encounter illustrates the full build: Globe of Invulnerability removes danger, water establishes vulnerability, and Cone of Cold clears clustered enemies. Orin is paralyzed through the surgeon's amulet plus a guaranteed critical, then burst with close-range Ray of Frost.

At the finale, Globe of Invulnerability protects the group while the ice engine and allied lightning kill the dragon and Illithids. The Netherbrain's rotating damage immunities require planning: use cold in one round, create Wet or switch damage during the immune round, then return to cold. Gemini Gloves produce the showcased 196-damage Ray of Frost burst.

## Practical Recommendations

Prioritize Mourning Frost and the Charisma riders; they are the build's true foundation. Use Mage Hand or summons to apply water and preserve the Sorcerer's actions. Carry initiative tools—Bow of Awareness, Hellrider Longbow, Elixir of Vigilance, or Alert—because control matters only if it happens before enemy turns.

Use ice surfaces deliberately and protect against your own terrain through positioning or appropriate gear. Preserve Alfira correctly if Potent Robe is required. Elemental Adept: Cold is most valuable when Wet cannot be applied safely, especially against Myrkul. Keep Shield, Misty Step, Counterspell, Globe of Invulnerability, summons, and scrolls available rather than insisting every action be cold-themed.

## Caveats and Context

This is a challenge-run narrative, not a minimal or universally reproducible leveling guide. It uses significant cheese: knocking out Alfira, forced movement, throwing Moonrise guards into chasms, temporary buffs, Strength elixirs, vendor access, and potentially the Angelic Reprieve Sorcery Point exploit. Several important Arcana and Religion checks succeeded on natural 20s. Item interactions and Markoheshkir behavior may change with patches.

## Conclusion

Ice Sorcerer is “broken” because one cantrip becomes an entire combat system. Ray of Frost scales through three Charisma riders, vulnerability doubles its output, and every hit can also slow, create ice, apply Reverberation, stack frost, or freeze. The build does not merely deal large numbers—it repeatedly removes enemy movement and turns while retaining Sorcerer's flexibility, Metamagic, and late-game invulnerability tools.
