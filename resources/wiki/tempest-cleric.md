# Tempest Domain Cleric — BG3 progression & spell reference

> Sources: bg3.wiki (Cleric, Tempest Domain, Destructive Wrath, Wrath of the Storm, Create or Destroy Water, Thunderwave, Fog Cloud pages) + local KB (`party_plan.json`). Patch 8, non-Honour. Used by **Toaster** (Draconic-Blue Sorcerer 10 / **Tempest Cleric 2** — a 2-level dip at character levels 11–12).

This is a **2-level dip**. The entire reason it exists for Toaster is three things, all delivered by levels 1–2 of Cleric with the Tempest Domain:

1. **Create or Destroy Water** (the Wet-setup — makes Lightning/Cold hit for **double**), available the moment you take Cleric 1.
2. **Destructive Wrath** (Channel Divinity, Cleric 2) — spend a charge to **maximise** any Thunder/Lightning damage roll, including the Sorcerer's Lightning Bolt / Chain Lightning / Markoheshkir bolts.
3. **Heavy armour + Martial weapon** proficiency (Cleric 1) — turns a squishy CHA caster into a plate-wearing front-tolerant caster.

Everything below covers only the depth this build uses (Cleric 1–2), with notes on what you deliberately give up by stopping at 2.

## Spellcasting

- **Ability:** **Wisdom.** Cleric spell save DC and spell attack rolls key off WIS. ⚠️ Toaster's WIS is only 12 (+1), so any *save-based* Cleric spell/cantrip has a weak DC. This is fine — the dip is not for save-based Cleric offence; it's for Create Water (no save), Destructive Wrath (maximises the Sorcerer's own lightning), and armour.
- **Prepared, not known, not scribed.** Cleric is a **prepared** caster: it *automatically knows the entire Cleric spell list up to its Cleric level* and you choose a subset to have "prepared." You do **not** learn a fixed spellbook and you do **not** scribe scrolls (that's Wizard).
- **How many:** At Cleric 1 you pick **3 cantrips** from the Cleric list (this stays 3 at Cleric 2 — no new cantrip at level 2). Prepared spells = **Cleric level + WIS modifier** (min 1): so **2** prepared at Cleric 1, **3** at Cleric 2 (with WIS 12). ⚠️ **Domain spells are always-prepared and do NOT count** against that number.
- **Level-up rule — no "replace":** Cleric has **no** spell-replacement-on-level-up mechanic (that's for known-casters like Sorcerer/Warlock). Instead, as a prepared caster you can **re-pick your prepared spells freely, any time outside combat** — swap them at will.
- **Multiclass slots:** Cleric is a **full caster**. Spellcasting spell slots are one **shared pool** sized by combined effective spellcaster level (Sorcerer levels + Cleric levels). Sorcerer 10 + Cleric 2 = **caster level 12 → 4/3/3/3/2/1**, i.e. the dip's practical slot payoff is unlocking a **6th-level slot** (Sorcerer 10 alone tops out at 5th). ⚠️ Sorcerer and Cleric draw from the same pool; sorcery-point conversion still works on it.

## Level-by-level

Toaster takes only Cleric 1–2 (character levels 11–12). "New spell slots" is shown as the **multiclass shared-pool** effect (see ⚠️ below the table), because in isolation the Cleric table would just fold into the pool.

| Class Lvl | Features gained | New spell slots | Spells you may add / replace | Cantrips |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cleric 1** (char 11) | **Choose subclass → Tempest Domain** (subclass is picked at Cleric 1); **Wrath of the Storm** (Reaction: retaliate 2d8 Lightning *or* Thunder, DEX save for half, attacker must be ≤2m and hit); **Heavy armour** + **Martial weapon** proficiency; WIS Spellcasting | Multiclass ESL 10→11 → adds a **6th-level** slot to the shared pool. *(Cleric-table standalone: two 1st-level slots.)* | Auto-knows all Cleric spells; **prepare 2** (1 + WIS 1). Domain spells **Thunderwave** + **Fog Cloud** are always-prepared (free). Key preparable pick: **Create or Destroy Water**. | Pick **3** Cleric cantrips (Guidance strongly recommended). |
| **Cleric 2** (char 12) | **Channel Divinity (1 charge)** → **Turn Undead** + **Destructive Wrath** (spend a charge to maximise a Thunder/Lightning damage roll). Recharges on **short OR long rest**. | Multiclass ESL 11→12 → **no new slot tier** (L12 table = L11). *(Cleric-table standalone would add a 3rd 1st-level slot and 2nd-level slots, but the pool is already maxed for caster-level.)* | **Prepare 3** (2 + WIS 1). Domain spells still only Thunderwave/Fog Cloud. | No new cantrip (stays 3). |

**Not gained by stopping at Cleric 2 (context):**
- ⚠️ **No feat.** Cleric feats land at **Cleric 4 / 8 / 12** — a 2-level dip grants none. Toaster's feats come from Sorcerer 4 & 8 (War Caster, Elemental Adept).
- ⚠️ **No Extra Attack** (Cleric never gets it).
- ⚠️ **No Gust of Wind / Shatter** — those Tempest domain spells arrive at **Cleric 3**, not reached. (Divine Strike: Thunder is Cleric 8; Destructive Wave Cleric 9.)
- ⚠️ Second Channel Divinity charge is at **Cleric 6** — you only have **1 Destructive Wrath/rest** from the class. (The **Amulet of the Devout** grants an extra Channel Divinity charge → effectively a 2nd Destructive Wrath; it's in Toaster's Act 3 gear.)

## Spell picks for this party

### Toaster — Draconic-Blue Sorcerer 10 / Tempest Cleric 2

Remember: **Thunderwave + Fog Cloud are already free** (domain, always-prepared), and Toaster only has **3 preparable slots** (WIS 12). Lean on no-save utility/buff spells since the WIS-based DC is low. The real firepower stays on the Sorcerer (CHA) side; Destructive Wrath just supercharges it.

**Mandatory**
- **Create or Destroy Water** (Lvl 1 · Transmutation · **no save**) — THE dip's core. Cast the **Create Water** variant to apply **Wet** (double Lightning/Cold) to a cluster, then nuke. Takes one of your 3 prepared slots. Draconic-Blue Sorcerer can't get this itself — the Cleric dip is the source. ⚠️ Friendly-fire caveat still applies (party members in electrified water take the doubled hit).
- **Destructive Wrath** (Channel Divinity feature, Cleric 2 · not a spell) — the payoff. When you roll Lightning/Thunder damage, spend the charge to **max every die**. Maximises Sorcerer Lightning Bolt / Witch Bolt / Chain Lightning / Markoheshkir bolts and the domain Thunderwave. 1/rest from the class (2 with Amulet of the Devout).
- **Guidance** (cantrip · Divination · **no save**) — take at Cleric 1. +1d4 to any ability check; best-in-slot party utility cantrip, unaffected by low WIS.

**Recommended**
- **Thunderwave** (Lvl 1 · Evocation · **CON save**, 2d8 Thunder + push) — *free* domain spell. A Thunder damage roll → **Destructive Wrath maximises it** (16 guaranteed), plus knockback for chasm kills and it triggers Reverberation gear (Boots of Stormy Clamour / Gloves of Belligerent Skies). Low WIS hurts the save DC, but the push + Destructive Wrath value still lands.
- **Fog Cloud** (Lvl 1 · Conjuration · **no save**) — *free* domain spell; heavy obscurement for repositioning/breaking line of sight. Zero opportunity cost.
- **Aid** (Lvl 2 · Abjuration · **no save**) — party max-HP buff, no concentration; strong use of a prepared slot that ignores WIS DC. (Needs a 2nd-level slot and must stay prepared to persist.)
- **Healing Word** (Lvl 1 · Evocation · **no save**) — bonus-action ranged pick-up; cheap insurance that doesn't care about WIS.
- **Sacred Flame** *or* **Toll the Dead** (cantrip · DEX / WIS save · Radiant / Necrotic) — optional 2nd/3rd cantrip if you want a Cleric damage cantrip; ⚠️ weak DC at WIS 12, though the **Potent Robe** adds CHA to cantrip damage so the flat add is still respectable. Otherwise take pure utility cantrips (**Resistance**, **Light**, **Thaumaturgy**, **Blade Ward**).

⚠️ **Multiclass order note:** Toaster reaches Cleric 1 *after* Sorcerer, so the "most recently added class" is Cleric → **scroll** spellcasting ability becomes **Wisdom** (low). This does not touch Sorcerer spell DCs (still CHA) and doesn't matter for Create Water (no save) or Destructive Wrath — but avoid relying on high-DC scrolls on this character.

⚠️ **Heavy armour caveat:** the dip grants proficiency (so no casting penalty), but heavy plate has a **STR requirement**; at STR 8 the heaviest armours impose a **movement-speed penalty** (full AC still applies). Pick a heavy set without a punishing STR gate, or accept the slower move for the AC.
