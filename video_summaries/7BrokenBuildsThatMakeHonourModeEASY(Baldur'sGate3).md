## Executive Summary

The video presents seven Patch 8-capable builds designed to make Honour Mode highly manageable. Although their damage engines differ, they share a pattern: multiply the number of attacks or rays, add independent damage riders, impose conditions that deny enemy turns, and select gear that reinforces the same loop.

The builds are not merely endgame showcases. Each section supplies a leveling path and early equipment so the character contributes before its final combination comes online. Several depend on character-specific mechanics—Gale’s shadow spell slot, Karlach’s Soul Coin interaction, or Minthara’s Soul Branding—so party composition matters.

## Detailed Findings

### Shadow Blade Eldritch Knight

Gale is the preferred character because his Act 2 interaction with the shadow weave can grant a permanent third-level shadow spell slot. That slot upcasts Shadow Blade beyond what a normal Eldritch Knight can do. The build levels first as a conventional two-handed Fighter, then respecs around Shadow Blade once the required mechanics arrive.

At high level, Improved Extra Attack gives three weapon attacks per action, while War Magic adds a bonus-action weapon attack after Booming Blade. The Resonance Stone makes nearby creatures vulnerable to psychic damage, doubling the Shadow Blade’s output. Eldritch Knight also supplies Longstrider, Enhanced Leap, Feather Fall, Knock, See Invisibility, and similar utility. The build’s main constraint is protecting concentration and managing the Resonance Stone’s effects.

### Stars Druid / Light Cleric

Patch 8’s Circle of the Stars gives Light Cleric an unusually strong opening and concentration toolset. Starry Form: Archer supplies early bonus-action radiant damage; Dragon later stabilizes concentration. The build transitions into Light Cleric for Spirit Guardians, Radiance of the Dawn, Warding Flare, and Destroy Undead.

Radiating Orb lowers enemy attack rolls, Reverberation can knock targets prone, and Spirit Guardians applies these effects across groups. The result is support that prevents damage while still dealing meaningful area damage. It is especially dominant against Act 2 undead but remains valuable through Guidance, Fireball, Remove Curse, Aid, and other utility.

### Divine Smite Sorcadin

The suggested start is a Half-Orc Oath of Vengeance Paladin. Half-Orc critical features amplify the build’s focus on guaranteed critical smites, while Vow of Enmity, Misty Step, and Hold Person are all highly relevant. Sorcerer levels add high-level spell slots, Shield, Counterspell, and Metamagic.

The Band of the Mystic Scoundrel is a crucial late-game piece: after a weapon hit, the character can cast an illusion or enchantment spell as a bonus action. This enables an attack, bonus-action Hold spell, then automatic melee criticals and smites. The Diadem of Arcane Synergy adds Charisma to weapon damage after inflicting a condition.

### Open Hand Monk

The build uses a Wood Elf or Wood Half-Elf for movement and begins Monk immediately. Tavern Brawler turns Strength into exceptional accuracy and damage; Open Hand Flurries add multiple attacks and control. Soul Coins on Karlach have an unusually strong interaction, adding two instances of fire damage to unarmed hits under the covered conditions. Since the build attacks so often, small riders scale dramatically.

Defense remains respectable through Unarmoured Defence, Deflect Missiles, Evasion, and extreme mobility. Manifestations of Body, Mind, or Soul later add necrotic, psychic, or radiant damage to each unarmed attack.

### Eldritch Blast Sorlock

This Charisma-focused build begins Warlock, taking Eldritch Blast, Agonising Blast, and Repelling Blast, then uses Sorcerer levels and Quickened Spell to cast more often. Haste, a Bloodlust Elixir, and Quickened Spell can reach four Eldritch Blast casts in a turn under the video’s setup.

Multiple rays multiply every compatible rider. Great Old One’s Mortal Reminder frightens nearby enemies on critical hits; Reverberation and related effects knock them prone. A frightened creature cannot move to stand, so the combination can force it to skip turns. Crit chance and Charisma are therefore as important as raw spell damage.

### Tavern Brawler Thrower

Tavern Brawler adds twice the Strength modifier to throw attacks and damage, and its extra damage can behave as a separate source. Returning weapons such as Nyrulna or the Dwarven Thrower make the loop effortless. The build begins Barbarian, gains Berserker’s bonus-action throw and Extra Attack, and can temporarily respec through Fighter when equipment or level breakpoints favor it.

Minthara’s Soul Branding is especially valuable because throwing does not consume the effect the way an ordinary weapon attack does, allowing repeated bonus fire damage. One equipment warning is emphasized: switching immediately between thrown returning weapons can unequip the off-hand and lose Dueling or off-hand stat benefits.

### Arcane Archer

The Arcane Archer is framed as underrated. Banishing Shot is available early and can keep multiple enemies removed from the battle at once, a major advantage in Honour Mode. Later features increase both the number of uses and the subclass’s control. The build also exploits a special interaction discussed in its leveling section and combines class shots with strong magical-arrow options.

## Practical Recommendations

- Choose the character as carefully as the class: Gale, Karlach, and Minthara can materially change a build.
- Acquire core condition gear early; these builds scale through riders, not only ability scores.
- Protect concentration for Shadow Blade and Spirit Guardians.
- Use guaranteed critical effects on the largest smite or multi-rider attack, not a routine hit.
- For Sorlock, prioritize Charisma, critical range, and ray-compatible riders.
- For throwers, keep returning-weapon and off-hand behavior in mind before chaining throws.
- Treat enemy-turn denial as damage prevention: Banishment, prone plus frightened, and Radiating Orb are central Honour Mode defenses.

## Caveats and Context

The transcript was obtained from a fallback source whose rolling captions repeat phrases roughly three times; this document deduplicates those captions. Several interactions are highly patch-sensitive and some depend on behavior the video itself treats as unusually strong or bug-like. Exact “best in slot” gear also arrives at different points, so the leveling version of a build will not match its final showcase.

## Conclusion

All seven builds succeed by turning one strong mechanic into a repeated engine. Shadow Blade, Spirit Guardians, smites, unarmed attacks, Eldritch Blast rays, throws, and Arcane Shots become overwhelming only after action economy, riders, and control are aligned around them. The practical takeaway is to build a coherent loop, not merely collect individually powerful items.
