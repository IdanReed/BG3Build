# The 20 Best Items in Act 1

- Playlist index: 25
- Video ID: `X2xy8yw6ZlM`
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X2xy8yw6ZlM
- Scope: Equipable gear only; weapons and consumables are excluded

## Executive Summary

The Gloves of Dexterity rank as the most impactful equipable item in Act 1—and, in the narrator's view, the entire game—because they set Dexterity to 18, add +1 to attack rolls, free ability points and feats, improve initiative and armour class, and enable otherwise stat-starved multiclasses. Luminous Armour ranks second by turning radiant damage into rapidly stacking Radiating Orb, which can reduce enemy attack rolls so severely that they stop landing hits. The Graceful Cloth ranks third for +2 Dexterity, advantage on Dexterity checks, better saves, mobility, initiative, and build flexibility.

The ranking values items by how much they improve the chance of completing Honour Mode. Raw strength matters, but so do early availability, reliability, versatility, and campaign longevity. Accordingly, many early utility, mobility, defensive, and buff-on-heal pieces outrank flashier late-Act gear.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Power: how large the direct combat or utility benefit is.
- Timing: earlier items influence more dangerous encounters and more of the run.
- Versatility: items useful across many builds and parties rank higher.
- Reliability: reducing randomness, preserving actions, and preventing deaths matter more than narrow peak output.
- Longevity: several selections remain best-in-slot or useful through Acts 2 and 3.

## Core Strategic Findings

Initiative, action access, and variance reduction repeatedly outperform simple armour or damage comparisons. The Haste Helm, Boots of Speed, and Night Walkers prevent turns from being lost to distance or terrain. Adamantine equipment removes random critical-hit spikes that can snowball into failed Honour runs. Dexterity items rank highest because one attribute improves initiative, AC, attacks, damage, saves, and skills simultaneously.

The guide also identifies an unusually strong support package. The Whispering Promise and Hellrider's Pride turn the same heal into Bless and Blade Ward. A bonus-action Healing Word or potion splash can therefore increase accuracy, saves, and physical durability across multiple allies. This provides party-wide value without devoting a character exclusively to healing.

Condition gear creates another progression path. Boots of Stormy Clamour begin the Reverberation package, while Luminous Armour begins Radiating Orb. Both convert ordinary damage or conditions into enemy action denial, illustrating why the guide values debuffing gear that works passively.

## Practical Recommendations

Steal the Breastplate +1 early for immediate AC. At Grymforge, distribute Adamantine crafts to maximize critical immunity across the party; the shield is especially efficient when another character can use it. Build a healer around The Whispering Promise and Hellrider's Pride, ideally with bonus-action or area healing.

Give mobility items to the character most likely to waste a turn out of position. Put Strange Conduit and Caustic Band on multiattackers. Use Boots of Stormy Clamour on anyone who applies conditions reliably. Assign Luminous Armour to a radiant-damage character—most naturally a Spirit Guardians Cleric.

Once the Gloves of Dexterity arrive, respec their wearer rather than accepting only the jump from their old Dexterity. Reinvest the freed points into Strength, Constitution, Wisdom, or Charisma and spend saved feats on Alert, Great Weapon Master, Sharpshooter, or Tavern Brawler. The Graceful Cloth is preferable when a character genuinely wants Dexterity above 18 or needs its skill advantages.

## Caveats

Several items are build- or origin-specific. Deathstalker Mantle requires Dark Urge, Ring of Flinging assumes a thrower, and Luminous Armour needs radiant damage and medium armour proficiency. The Boots of Speed's opportunity-attack wording was reported as bugged, but the bonus-action Dash remains the main attraction. Ratings also exclude weapons and consumables and may shift with patches.

## Conclusion

Act 1 gear matters because it shapes the most fragile part of an Honour run and often remains relevant for dozens of hours. The best items do more than add numbers: they prevent lost turns, suppress randomness, create free buffs, and release character-building resources. The Gloves of Dexterity embody that philosophy by improving immediate performance while expanding what builds are possible at all.
