# Hexblade Warlock (Pact of the Blade) — BG3 progression & spell reference

> Sources: bg3.wiki + local party KB (`party_plan.json`). Patch 8, non-Honour mode. Used by **Durc** (Oathbreaker Paladin 7 / **Hexblade Warlock 5**, Half-Orc Dark Urge) as the Pact-Magic/CHA melee half of the build (invocations, Deepened Pact extra attack, Hexblade's Curse). This doc covers **Warlock levels 1–5** (the depth Durc takes) only.

**The Hexblade is real Patch-8 content**, not a mod — it was added to BG3's base Warlock on 2025-04-15, so everything below is vanilla-verifiable. Durc's build only goes to Warlock 5, so the level-6/level-10 Hexblade features (Accursed Spectre, etc.) are out of scope.

## Spellcasting

- **Ability:** Charisma (spell save DC and spell attack rolls). Durc's CHA reaches 20 (17 → 18 Hag's Hair → 20 Mirror of Loss).
- **Pact Magic — Known, not prepared.** Warlock spells are **Known** spells that are **Always Prepared** once learned; you do **not** re-pick a loadout each long rest the way Cleric/Paladin/Druid do. You choose from the Warlock spell list (plus the Hexblade's expanded list, which is folded into your choosable options ⚠️ — you still spend a "spell known" pick to learn one).
- **Replacing spells on level-up:** **Yes.** Each level-up offers an optional "Replacement Spell" — swap **one** known spell for another Warlock-list spell. (The wiki shows this at Warlock 3/4/5; ⚠️ whether Warlock 2 also offers it isn't explicitly confirmed.) **Invocations, by contrast, can NOT be swapped on level-up — only a Withers respec changes them.**
- **Counts (this build's depth):** Cantrips known = **2** at W1, rising to **3** at **W4**. Spells known = **2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6** at W1 / W2 / W3 / W4 / W5.
- **Pact slots are weird.** You get very few, they **all sit at your highest spell level**, lower-level spells are **always upcast** to that level, and they **recharge on a Short Rest** (not just Long). At Warlock 5 Durc has **2 pact slots, both level 3** — so any Warlock spell he casts (or a Divine Smite fed from a pact slot) comes out at 3rd level. These pact slots are tracked separately from his Paladin/multiclass slots.
- Not a "dip that skips casting": even at Warlock 1 he is a full CHA caster with a level-1 pact slot and Hexblade's Curse.

**Invocation correction (important):** the party plan lists Durc's invocations as *Thirsting Blade, Improved Pact Weapon, Devil's Sight*. In BG3 **neither "Thirsting Blade" nor "Improved Pact Weapon" exists** — Thirsting Blade's extra attack is replaced by the free **Deepened Pact** feature, and Improved Pact Weapon (and its +1 the nova math assumes) simply isn't in the game ⚠️. So Durc has **3 real invocation slots** to spend (2 at W2, +1 at W5). Recommended for this melee CHA smiter:
- **Devil's Sight** (see in magical + non-magical darkness, 24 m) — synergizes with obscured-crit gear (Covert Cowl) and any Darkness play; essentially mandatory.
- **Agonising Blast** (+CHA to each Eldritch Blast beam) — with CHA 20 this makes Eldritch Blast a genuine ranged option for turns he can't reach melee.
- **Flex:** **Repelling Blast** (control knock-back), **Thief of Five Fates** (Bane — shave enemy attacks/saves, helps land smites/CC), or **Fiendish Vigour** (False Life at-will → 7 temp HP for a frontliner).

## Spell picks for this party

### Durc — Oathbreaker Paladin 7 / **Hexblade Warlock 5**

Only 6 Warlock spells known by W5, and pact slots also feed Divine Smite, so keep the Warlock list lean. Note the **Concentration** clashes: Shadow Blade is *not* concentration (great), but Wrathful Smite, Hex, and Blur each are — you can only hold one at a time.

**Mandatory**
- **Shadow Blade** (Lvl 2 · Illusion · no save — summons a weapon; Advantage vs obscured targets) — the entire early/mid weapon set. Bonus action, **not concentration**, lasts until long rest; base 2d8 psychic, **upcasts to 3d8** once W5 gives level-3 pact slots. Learned at **char 5 (Warlock 3)**. ⚠️ The summoned blade uses STR/DEX by default (it's finesse) — you must **Bind Hexed Weapon** (or Bind Pact Weapon) onto it to swing with **CHA**; this works but is a fiddly interaction.

**Recommended**
- **Wrathful Smite** (Lvl 1 · Evocation · WIS save) — the Hexblade signature, learnable from **char 1 (Warlock 1 expanded list)**. Bonus-action-on-hit for **+1d6 psychic** (shows as 2d6 in the plan's nova because a Held target auto-crits and doubles the die) and **Frightens** on a failed WIS save. **Concentration**; no upcast benefit. This is the "Wrathful Smite" line in the nova math.
- **Hex** (Lvl 1 · Enchantment · no save, attack-triggered) — **+1d6 Necrotic per successful attack**; with 3 attacks that's a sustained +3d6/turn, plus Disadvantage on a chosen ability's checks. **Concentration** (so it competes with Wrathful Smite/Blur). Better for long fights than the one-shot nova.
- **Armour of Agathys** (Lvl 1 · Abjuration · no save) — 5 temp HP + 5 Cold retaliation **per slot level**; cast from a W5 level-3 pact slot that's **15 temp HP / 15 Cold**, **no concentration** — excellent cheap frontline armour that scales with the pact slot.
- **Shield** (Lvl 1 · Abjuration · reaction, Hexblade expanded) — reaction **+5 AC** to dodge an incoming hit; not concentration; strong survival pick for a melee body.
- **Branding Smite** (Lvl 2 · Hexblade expanded · no save) — bonus-action **+2d6 radiant** smite that also makes the target glow (strips invisibility). A spare non-radiant-resist smite; learnable from **char 5 (Warlock 3)**.
- **Blur** (Lvl 2 · Abjuration · no save, Hexblade expanded) — attackers roll at Disadvantage. **Concentration** defensive alternative when not running Hex.
- **Eldritch Blast** (Cantrip · Evocation · attack roll) — take it as one of the 2 starting cantrips; 1d10 Force/beam (2 beams at char 5, 3 at char 10), and with **Agonising Blast** + CHA 20 it's Durc's real ranged fallback. Other cantrips matter little on a melee smiter ⚠️ (Friends for face utility, or a filler damage cantrip).

## Quick verification notes / uncertainties
- ⚠️ **Thirsting Blade / Improved Pact Weapon are not in BG3** — the plan's invocation list and the "Improved Pact Weapon +1" in the nova need revising; the 2nd attack is free from Deepened Pact.
- ⚠️ **Shadow Blade → CHA** requires binding the summoned blade (Bind Hexed/Pact Weapon); confirm in-game it took the Hexed condition.
- ⚠️ Whether the optional **spell replacement** is offered at Warlock 2 specifically (it's confirmed at 3/4/5).
- ⚠️ Warlock skill choices at level 1 (choose 2, from a list including Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, Religion) — not re-verified against the wiki this pass; saving-throw proficiencies are **Wisdom + Charisma**.
- ⚠️ **Savage-Attacker-rerolls-smite** and **Hexblade's Curse's +damage riding the weapon's damage type** are non-Honour nova assumptions the plan already flags as "unknown if bugged/intended."
