# War Domain Cleric — BG3 progression & spell reference

> Sources: bg3.wiki (Cleric, War Domain, War Priest, Command, Create Water, Guidance, Sacred Flame, Resistance, Multiclassing) + local KB (`party_plan.json`). Patch 8, non-Honour. Used by **Batman** (Astarion) — Config A only, as a **1-level dip** on Arcane Trickster Rogue 11 / War Domain Cleric 1. (Config B swaps this dip for Fighter 1 and does **not** use Cleric.)

## Spellcasting

- **Ability:** Wisdom. Every Cleric spell, Cleric cantrip, and the Cleric spell-save DC / spell-attack roll keys off the **WIS modifier**. For Batman that is WIS 15 (+2) at the dip, rising to WIS 17 (+3) after the Rogue-8 feat.
- **Prepared, not known, not scribed.** Cleric is a **prepared caster**: it automatically has access to the *entire* Cleric spell list up to its Cleric level and you "prepare" a small working set. There is **no "learn N spells" pick** and **no level-up spell-replacement** the way Arcane Trickster / Bard / Sorcerer have — prepared casters simply **re-pick their prepared list freely at any time outside combat.** (Batman's *Arcane Trickster* spells — Shield, Disguise Self, Shadow Blade — are a separate, INT-based known list and are unaffected by this dip.)
- **Cantrips at Cleric 1:** **3** chosen from the Cleric list (this is on top of Astarion's High-Elf bonus cantrip and any AT cantrips).
- **Prepared level-1 spells at Cleric 1:** count = **Cleric level (1) + WIS modifier**, minimum 1. So **3 prepared** at WIS 15, **4 prepared** after WIS→17. This uses *Cleric* level (1), not character level, so it never grows past 1 + WIS mod on a 1-level dip.
- **Spell slots at Cleric 1:** **2 level-1 slots** (these stack into Batman's shared multiclass slot pool alongside the AT slots).
- **Domain spells are free:** War Domain's always-prepared spells (**Divine Favour, Shield of Faith** at level 1) are always prepared and **do not count** against the prepared limit above.
- **Subclass timing:** in BG3 the Cleric **Domain is chosen at Cleric level 1** — i.e. the moment you take the dip you *are* a War Domain Cleric; there is no separate later subclass level.
- **Why the dip keys scrolls off WIS:** per bg3.wiki, a multiclass character uses "the spellcasting modifier from the most recent **new** class taken for item spellcasting." Rogue is added at character level 1 (not yet a caster; Arcane Trickster/INT only unlocks at Rogue 3) and **Cleric is the most-recently-*added* new class**, so Batman's stolen **scrolls key off WIS**, matching the WIS investment. ⚠️ This item-DC selection rule is a finicky, order-dependent multiclass interaction — verify in-game after respec that scroll DC shows WIS, not INT.

Batman takes **exactly one** Cleric level (at **character level 2**, after Rogue 1). Only the **Cleric 1** row is part of the build; Cleric 2–3 are shown greyed as reference for anyone tempted to deepen the dip (they are **not taken** here).

Notes on the dip:
- **Extra Attack:** Cleric never grants it. The dip's martial contribution is **War Priest** (a *bonus-action* attack) — but Batman's bonus action is almost always spent on **Cunning Action: Hide** (to arm Magical Ambush + Sneak Attack) or **Bite**, and Sneak Attack only fires once per turn, so War Priest is **largely redundant** for this build. The dip is really bought for **heavy armour early, WIS-keyed scroll casting, and the Guidance/Command/Create Water utility**, not for War Priest damage.
- **Saving throws:** multiclassing **never** grants save proficiencies — Batman keeps Rogue's **DEX + INT** save proficiency; the dip gives **no** WIS/CHA save proficiency. The Rogue-8 "+2 WIS" feat only raises the *modifier* on WIS saves, not proficiency. ⚠️
- **Feat:** Cleric grants feats at Cleric 4/8/12, so the **1-level dip yields no feat** (Batman's 3 feats all come from Rogue 4/8/10).

## Spell picks for this party

### Batman — Arcane Trickster Rogue 11 / War Domain Cleric 1 (Config A)

The dip carries **3 cantrips** + **3 prepared L1 spells** (→4 after WIS 17) + the 2 free domain spells. Lean the prepared set toward setup/utility that keys off WIS; leave raw damage to stolen DEX-save scrolls.

**Mandatory**
- **Guidance** (Cantrip · Divination · no save) — +1d4 to *any* Ability Check, concentration, touch. The single best reason a skill-monkey wants this dip: stacks with Expertise + Reliable Talent for near-guaranteed Stealth / Sleight of Hand / dialogue checks. Pick at the dip (char lvl 2).
- **Create Water** (Lvl 1 · Transmutation · no save) — applies **Wet** (double **Cold** and **Lightning** damage, 3 turns) in a 4 m radius. Core party synergy: sets up Toaster's lightning *and* Batman's cold-first scroll lane; Batman can hard-cast it while the invisible Mage Hand handles water bottles. Prepare at the dip.
- **Command** (Lvl 1 · Enchantment · **WIS save**) — single-target hard control (Halt/Grovel/Drop/Flee), 18 m, keys off Batman's WIS DC and can be thrown at disadvantage via Magical Ambush; upcast adds +1 target per slot above 1st (no effect on undead). Prepare at the dip.

**Recommended**
- **Shield of Faith** (Lvl 1 · Abjuration · concentration) — **free domain spell**, +2 AC for the squishy assassin. ⚠️ Concentration — cannot run at the same time as a concentration-based Shadow Blade or Bless/Divine Favour; use when not concentrating on the blade.
- **Healing Word** (Lvl 1 · Evocation · no save) — **bonus-action** ranged pick-up for a party with no dedicated healer; clutch revive when Batman is out of Hide range anyway. Prepare situationally.
- **Sanctuary** (Lvl 1 · Abjuration · WIS save to attackers) — bonus-action "don't hit me" bubble; good escape/eat-a-turn tool on a frail striker.
- **Resistance** (Cantrip · Abjuration · concentration) *or* **Sacred Flame** (Cantrip · Evocation · **DEX save**, 1d8→3d8 Radiant scaling with character level) — flex cantrip #2/#3. Resistance = +1d4 saves buffer; Sacred Flame = a free ranged radiant poke (low priority — Batman would rather Hide + scroll, and its DEX save keys off his modest WIS DC). **Toll the Dead** (WIS save necrotic) is a stronger damage cantrip if you want one that rides the WIS DC. ⚠️ Exact Sacred Flame/Toll die values scale with **character** level, not Cleric level.
- **Divine Favour** (Lvl 1 · concentration · always-prepared domain) — free radiant rider on weapon hits, but ⚠️ concentration conflicts with Shadow Blade; effectively a no-op for the melee-blade plan.

⚠️ **Uncertainties flagged:** the WIS-vs-INT scroll-DC selection (order-dependent item-casting rule) and whether Batman's self-cast Shadow Blade actually frees concentration both need in-game confirmation on the specific save/patch; the exact Divine Favour die was not re-verified on the wiki here.
